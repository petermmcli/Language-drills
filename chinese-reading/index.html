Easy 去超市买东西
我要去超市买东西。🔊 我妈妈告诉我买水果和鸡蛋。🔊 走路差不多十分钟就到。🔊 超市里水果很多，苹果和西瓜都有。🔊 我买了一些，一共不太贵。🔊
▸ Pinyin ▸ English
Wǒ yào qù chāoshì mǎi dōngxi. Wǒ māma gàosu wǒ mǎi shuǐguǒ hé jīdàn. Zǒulù chàbuduō shí fēnzhōng jiù dào. Chāoshì lǐ shuǐguǒ hěn duō, píngguǒ hé xīguā dōu yǒu. Wǒ mǎi le yì xiē, yígòng bú tài guì.
I want to go to the supermarket to buy things. My mom told me to buy fruit and eggs. It's about a ten-minute walk. The supermarket has lots of fruit — apples and watermelon both. I bought some; it wasn't too expensive overall.

Harder 每天去公司
今天去公司以后，天阴了，开始下小雨。🔊 我没有带伞，很不方便。🔊 我先打车，然后坐地铁，比开车快多了。🔊 一个同事问我："你今天怎么来的？"🔊 我告诉他，他说："坐地铁真快！"🔊 工作完了，我给朋友发消息问她怎么回家。🔊 她说："我家就在饭店旁边，走路差不多五分钟就到。"🔊 我觉得她很好，我要开车四十分钟。🔊
▸ Pinyin ▸ English
Jīntiān qù gōngsī yǐhòu, tiān yīn le, kāishǐ xià xiǎoyǔ. Wǒ méiyǒu dài sǎn, hěn bù fāngbiàn. Wǒ xiān dǎchē, ránhòu zuò dìtiě, bǐ kāichē kuài duō le. Yí ge tóngshì wèn wǒ: "Nǐ jīntiān zěnme lái de?" Wǒ gàosu tā, tā shuō: "Zuò dìtiě zhēn kuài!" Gōngzuò wán le, wǒ gěi péngyǒu fā xiāoxi wèn tā zěnme huíjiā. Tā shuō: "Wǒ jiā jiù zài fàndiàn pángbiān, zǒulù chàbuduō wǔ fēnzhōng jiù dào." Wǒ juéde tā hěn hǎo, wǒ yào kāichē sìshí fēnzhōng.
After arriving at the office today, the sky turned cloudy and it started to drizzle. I didn't have an umbrella — very inconvenient. I took a taxi first, then the subway, which was much faster than driving. A colleague asked me: "How did you get here today?" I told him, and he said: "The subway is really fast!" After finishing work, I messaged a friend to ask how she gets home. She said: "My home is right next to the hotel — about a five-minute walk." I think she's lucky; I have to drive forty minutes.
