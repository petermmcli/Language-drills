Māma kāixīn de shuō, tā zài wǎngshàng kàndào Jiāzhōu yě yǒu zhè zhǒng huā, hé Yúnnán de yíyàng de. Wǒmen liáo le hěn jiǔ, wǒ juéde tā bǐ yǐqián hǎo duō le, zhēn de tài hǎo le!
Every morning, I first get up and then go to the kitchen to make breakfast. After finishing breakfast, I take the subway to the hospital to see my mom. She was hospitalized last month, so I go to see her every day. When I got to the hospital, I first bought flowers to give to her. The flowers were from Yunnan, very beautiful — I also bought her a flower pot. Mom said happily that she had seen online that California also has this kind of flower, the same as the ones from Yunnan. We chatted for a long time. I feel she is much better than before — truly wonderful!
