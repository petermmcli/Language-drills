Easy 我的猫
我家有一只猫。🔊 猫很可爱，喜欢玩。🔊 每天吃饭的时候，我也给猫吃东西。🔊 买东西的时候，我总是买猫喜欢吃的东西。🔊 我觉得猫是很好的朋友。🔊
▸ Pinyin ▸ English
Wǒ jiā yǒu yì zhī māo. Māo hěn kěài, xǐhuān wán. Měi tiān chīfàn de shíhou, wǒ yě gěi māo chī dōngxi. Mǎi dōngxi de shíhou, wǒ zǒngshì mǎi māo xǐhuān chī de dōngxi. Wǒ juéde māo shì hěn hǎo de péngyou.
My home has a cat. The cat is very cute and loves to play. Every time we eat, I also give the cat something to eat. When I go shopping, I always buy things the cat likes to eat. I think cats are wonderful friends.

Harder 修房间
我们家的房间漏水了。🔊 爸爸说这是一件很重要的事情，需要修。🔊 我们先打电话给房东，然后又买东西来修。🔊 房东来了以后，我和爸爸帮助他修好了。🔊 修好以后，我们才有时间吃饭。🔊 吃饭的时候，妹妹看着电视吃饭，妈妈告诉她别这样。🔊 爸爸觉得帮助别人也是一件很重要的事情。🔊
▸ Pinyin ▸ English
Wǒmen jiā de fángjiān lòu shuǐ le. Bàba shuō zhè shì yī jiàn hěn zhòngyào de shìqíng, xūyào xiū. Wǒmen xiān dǎ diànhuà gěi fángdōng, ránhòu yòu mǎi dōngxi lái xiū. Fángdōng lái le yǐhòu, wǒ hé bàba bāngzhù tā xiū hǎo le. Xiū hǎo yǐhòu, wǒmen cái yǒu shíjiān chīfàn. Chīfàn de shíhou, mèimei kànzhe diànshì chīfàn, māma gàosu tā bié zhèyàng. Bàba juéde bāngzhù biérén yě shì yī jiàn hěn zhòngyào de shìqíng.
Our room at home was leaking water. Dad said this was a very important matter that needed to be fixed. We first called the landlord, then went and bought things to fix it. After the landlord arrived, my dad and I helped him fix it. After it was fixed, we finally had time to eat. While eating, my younger sister was watching TV while eating — Mom told her not to do that. Dad thinks helping others is also a very important thing.
