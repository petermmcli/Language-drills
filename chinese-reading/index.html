Bàba chī le hěn duō, wǒ shuō: “Bié zài chī le, nǐ yǐjīng chī duō le!” Tā xiào zhe shuō: “Hǎo, wǒ bǎo le, bù néng zài chī le.” Chī wán fàn, wǒmen yìqǐ kàn diànshì, juéde fēicháng kuàilè. Wǒ juéde, hé jiārén zài yìqǐ shì jiǎqī zuì zhòngyào de shì.
This year's holiday, our family planned lots of fun things. Mom said this year should be better than last year — we'd first cook together, then eat together. Dad ate a lot, so I said: "Stop eating — you've already eaten too much!" He laughed and said: "Okay, I'm full, I can't eat any more."
