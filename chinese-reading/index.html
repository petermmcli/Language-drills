Harder 找房间
上个星期，我去看了几个房间。🔊 第一个地方很好，但是太贵了，所以我拒绝了。🔊 第二个地儿不大，可是离地铁站很近，行走五分钟就到了。🔊 看完以后，我觉得第二个更好。🔊 我告诉朋友，她说："对吧？住在那里，方便多了！"🔊 周末，朋友来看我的新房间。🔊 我先去超市买了东西，然后做饭，我们吃得非常开心。🔊
▸ Pinyin ▸ English
Shàng ge xīngqī, wǒ qù kàn le jǐ ge fángjiān. Dì yī ge dìfāng hěn hǎo, dànshì tài guì le, suǒyǐ wǒ jùjué le. Dì èr ge dìr bù dà, kěshì lí dìtiězhàn hěn jìn, xíngzǒu wǔ fēnzhōng jiù dào le. Kàn wán yǐhòu, wǒ juéde dì èr ge gèng hǎo. Wǒ gàosù péngyǒu, tā shuō: "Duì ba? Zhù zài nàlǐ, fāngbiàn duō le!" Zhōumò, péngyǒu lái kàn wǒ de xīn fángjiān. Wǒ xiān qù chāoshì mǎi le dōngxi, ránhòu zuò fàn, wǒmen chī de fēicháng kāixīn.
Last week, I went to look at a few rooms. The first place was great, but it was too expensive, so I turned it down. The second place wasn't big, but it was very close to the subway station — just a five-minute walk. After looking, I felt the second one was better. I told my friend, and she said: "Right? Living there is much more convenient!" On the weekend, my friend came to see my new room. I first went to the supermarket to get some things, then cooked dinner — we had a great time eating together.
