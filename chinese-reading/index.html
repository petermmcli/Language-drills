Easy 看医生
我昨天不太舒服，嗓子不好，一直咳嗽，还发烧了。🔊 今天我去医院看医生。🔊 医生说我需要多休息，还让我戴口罩。🔊 他给了我感冒药。🔊 我觉得好多了。🔊
▸ Pinyin ▸ English
Wǒ zuó tiān bú tài shū fu, sǎng zi bù hǎo, yì zhí ké sou, hái fā shāo le. Jīn tiān wǒ qù yī yuàn kàn yī shēng. Yī shēng shuō wǒ xū yào duō xiū xi, hái ràng wǒ dài kǒu zhào. Tā gěi le wǒ gǎn mào yào. Wǒ jué de hǎo duō le.
Yesterday I didn't feel well—my throat was bad, I kept coughing, and I also had a fever. Today I went to the hospital to see a doctor. The doctor said I need to rest a lot, and also had me wear a mask. He gave me cold medicine. I feel much better.

Brother: Why are you so sleepy today? Younger sister: Because I went to bed very late yesterday, and got up very early today. Brother: I think going to bed early and getting up early is good for your health. You should get plenty of rest. Younger sister: I know. Harder 看电影
上个星期五，我和朋友一起去电影院看电影。🔊 我们先买了爆米花，然后开始看电影。🔊 可是我们坐在后面，看不清，也听不清。🔊 我觉得这个电影太没意思了，后来我有点困。🔊 看完电影以后，朋友说上一次的电影比这个有意思。🔊 也许下次我们应该坐在前面。🔊
▸ Pinyin ▸ English
Shàng ge xīng qī wǔ, wǒ hé péng you yì qǐ qù diàn yǐng yuàn kàn diàn yǐng. Wǒ men xiān mǎi le bào mǐ huā, rán hòu kāi shǐ kàn diàn yǐng. Kě shì wǒ men zuò zài hòu miàn, kàn bù qīng, yě tīng bù qīng. Wǒ jué de zhè ge diàn yǐng tài méi yì si le, hòu lái wǒ yǒu diǎn kùn. Kàn wán diàn yǐng yǐ hòu, péng you shuō shàng yí cì de diàn yǐng bǐ zhè ge yǒu yì si. Yě xǔ xià cì wǒ men yīng gāi zuò zài qián miàn.
Last Friday, I went to the cinema with a friend to see a movie. We first bought popcorn, then started watching the movie. But we sat at the back, and couldn't see clearly or hear clearly. I thought this movie was too boring, and later I got a bit sleepy. After the movie, my friend said the previous movie was more interesting than this one. Perhaps next time we should sit at the front.
