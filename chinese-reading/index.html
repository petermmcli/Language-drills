Harder 周末的一天
上个周末，我先去书店看书，然后去朋友家吃饭。🔊 朋友做的饭非常好吃，我们都吃得很舒服。🔊 吃完饭以后，我们一起看了一部电影，很有意思。🔊 我觉得和朋友一起过周末比一个人在家有意思多了。🔊 回家的时候，我才想起来还有一个作业没做完。🔊 虽然很累，我还是坐在桌子旁边把作业写完了。🔊 太晚了！我下次一定要早一点儿做作业。🔊
▸ Pinyin ▸ English
Shàngge zhōumò，wǒ xiān qù shūdiàn kàn shū，ránhòu qù péngyǒu jiā chīfàn. Péngyǒu zuòde fàn fēicháng hǎochī，wǒmen dōu chīde hěn shūfu. Chīwán fàn yǐhòu，wǒmen yìqǐ kàn le yì bù diànyǐng，hěn yǒuyìsi. Wǒ juéde hé péngyǒu yìqǐ guò zhōumò bǐ yī ge rén zàijiā yǒuyìsi duō le. Huíjiāde shíhou，wǒ cái xiǎng qǐlai hái yǒu yī ge zuòyè méi zuòwán. Suīrán hěn lèi，wǒ háishi zuò zài zhuōzi pángbiān bǎ zuòyè xiěwán le. Tài wǎn le！Wǒ xiàcì yīdìng yào zǎo yìdiǎnr zuò zuòyè.
Last weekend, I first went to the bookstore to read, then went to a friend's house for dinner. My friend's cooking was delicious — we all ate very comfortably. After eating, we watched a movie together — it was really interesting. I think spending the weekend with friends is much more interesting than being home alone. On the way home, I suddenly remembered there was still some homework I hadn't finished. Even though I was very tired, I still sat down at the table and finished writing the homework. It was so late! Next time I'll definitely do my homework earlier.
