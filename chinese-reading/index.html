Harder 网上接单

Yǒushíhou, tā yòng wúrénjī sòng dōngxi, bǐ kāichē fāngbiàn duō le. Nà tiān, yào sòng de dìfang hěn yuǎn, wúrénjī bù kěyǐ qù. Tā dǎ diànhuà gàosu nàge rén: ó, duìbuqǐ, yào duō děng yī tiān. Nàge rén shuō: hǎode, méi wèntí, wǒ děng nǐ. Xiǎo Hóng xiān kāichē qù mǎi dōngxi, mǎiwán yǐhòu sòng guòqu le. Tā juéde zhège gōngzuò hěn yǒuqù, tǐng hǎo de!
My friend Xiao Hong takes orders online and helps people buy and deliver things every day. Last Saturday, she first typed out everything she needed to do. Sometimes she uses a drone to deliver — much more convenient than driving. That day, the delivery location was very far, so the drone couldn't go. She called the customer: "Oh, I'm sorry — you'll need to wait one more day." The customer said: "Sure, no problem, I'll wait." Xiao Hong drove out first to buy the things, then delivered them after she had finished buying. She thinks this job is very interesting — quite good!
