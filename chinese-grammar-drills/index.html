Fill-in-the-Blank Translation

一、Fill-in-the-Blank 填空

Today's drill covers a fresh batch from your latest lesson — the separable verb 生病, completed-action 了, and Verb+着+Verb — plus a wildcard on asking the time.

1.他 了，没有去上课。 🔊

▸ Show Pinyin
Tā ___ le ， méi yǒu qù shàng kè 。
▸ Show Answer
A. 生病
他生病了，没有去上课。🔊 Tā shēng bìng le ， méi yǒu qù shàng kè 。
He got sick and didn't go to class.
生病 (shēngbìng) — Separable Verb (离合词)

2.我昨天买 一件新衣服。 🔊

▸ Show Pinyin
Wǒ zuó tiān mǎi ___ yí jiàn xīn yī fu 。
▸ Show Answer
A. 了
我昨天买了一件新衣服。🔊 Wǒ zuó tiān mǎi le yí jiàn xīn yī fu 。
I bought a new piece of clothing yesterday.
了 (le) — Completed Action, Not Just Past Tense

3.妹妹 音乐做作业。 🔊

▸ Show Pinyin
Mèi mei ___ yīn yuè zuò zuò yè 。
▸ Show Answer
A. 听着
妹妹听着音乐做作业。🔊 Mèi mei tīng zhe yīn yuè zuò zuò yè 。
Younger sister does homework while listening to music.
Verb + 着 + Verb (Accompanying Manner)

4.你 起床？ 🔊

▸ Show Pinyin
Nǐ ___ qǐ chuáng ？
▸ Show Answer
A. 几点钟
你几点钟起床？🔊 Nǐ jǐ diǎn zhōng qǐ chuáng ？
What time do you get up?
G158 — S + 几点 + Verb? — Asking the Time
