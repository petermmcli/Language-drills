1.我 衣服洗干净了。 🔊

三、Error Correction 改错

Each sentence has a grammar mistake. Find it and correct it.

1.Find the error: 我把洗衣服干净了。🔊

▸ Show Pinyin
Wǒ bǎ xǐ yī fu gān jìng le 。
▸ Show Answer
Error: The object must come right after 把, before the verb — 衣服 (the object) needs to come before 洗 (the verb), not after.
Correct:
我把衣服洗干净了。🔊 Wǒ bǎ yī fu xǐ gān jìng le 。
I washed the clothes clean.
把 Construction (把字句) — fronting a known object

2.Find the error: 这个很比那个贵。🔊

▸ Show Pinyin
Zhè ge hěn bǐ nà ge guì 。
▸ Show Answer
Error: 很 cannot go before 比 in a comparison. Drop 很 and put a degree word like 多了 or 一点儿 after the adjective instead.
Correct:
这个比那个贵多了。🔊 Zhè ge bǐ nà ge guì duō le 。
This one is a lot more expensive than that one.
A 比 B + adjective + degree (comparison with 比)

3.Find the error: 她已经回了北京。🔊

▸ Show Pinyin
Tā yǐ jīng huí le Běi jīng 。
▸ Show Answer
Error: With 回 + place, 了 goes at the end of the sentence, not right after 回.
Correct:
她已经回北京了。🔊 Tā yǐ jīng huí Běi jīng le 。
She has already gone back to Beijing.
回 + place — returning to a location

4.Find the error: 你吃火锅了吗？🔊

▸ Show Pinyin
Nǐ chī huǒ guō le ma ？
▸ Show Answer
Error: To ask about life experience ("have you ever..."), use 过, not 了 — 了 marks a specific completed action, not experience.
Correct:
你吃过火锅吗？🔊 Nǐ chī guò huǒ guō ma ？
Have you ever eaten hot pot?
