Fill-in-the-Blank Translation

一、Fill-in-the-Blank 填空

Today's mix: 3 patterns from your latest lesson (让 vs 请, 比较 as a softer degree word, and 从…就… for "since as early as") plus one wildcard — 像…一样.

1.妈妈 妹妹做作业。 🔊

▸ Show Pinyin
Mā ma ___ mèi mei zuò zuò yè 。
▸ Show Answer
A. 让
妈妈让妹妹做作业。🔊 Mā ma ràng mèi mei zuò zuò yè 。
Mom has younger sister do her homework.
让 vs 请 (A 让/请 B + verb — having vs asking someone do something)

2.这个房间 贵。 🔊

▸ Show Pinyin
Zhè ge fáng jiān ___ guì 。
▸ Show Answer
A. 比较
这个房间比较贵。🔊 Zhè ge fáng jiān bǐ jiào guì 。
This room is fairly expensive.
比较 + adjective — a softer "relatively / fairly"

3.妹妹从小 喜欢音乐。 🔊

▸ Show Pinyin
Mèi mei cóng xiǎo ___ xǐ huan yīn yuè 。
▸ Show Answer
A. 就
妹妹从小就喜欢音乐。🔊 Mèi mei cóng xiǎo jiù xǐ huan yīn yuè 。
Younger sister has liked music since she was little.
从 + time + 就 + verb — "since as early as"

4.那个杯子 新的一样。 🔊

▸ Show Pinyin
Nà ge bēi zi ___ xīn de yí yàng 。
▸ Show Answer
A. 像
那个杯子像新的一样。🔊 Nà ge bēi zi xiàng xīn de yí yàng 。
That cup looks just like new.
