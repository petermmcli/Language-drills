多长时间🔊 duō cháng shíjiān how long (duration) 多长时间没见哥哥了？🔊
Duō cháng shíjiān méi jiàn gēge le?
How long has it been since you saw your older brother?
每个夏天🔊 měi ge xiàtiān every summer 每个夏天他都去暑假学校。🔊
Měi ge xiàtiān tā dōu qù shǔjià xuéxiào.
Every summer he goes to summer school.
去英国以前🔊 qù Yīngguó yǐqián before going to the UK 去英国以前，我学了两年英文。🔊
Qù Yīngguó yǐqián, wǒ xué le liǎng nián Yīngwén.
Before going to the UK, I studied English for two years.
一般学校🔊 yìbān xuéxiào a regular school 暑假学校比一般学校容易。🔊
Shǔjià xuéxiào bǐ yìbān xuéxiào róngyì.
Summer school is easier than regular school.
下了几次雪🔊 xià le jǐ cì xuě snowed a few times 今年冬天下了几次雪。🔊
Jīnnián dōngtiān xià le jǐ cì xuě.
It snowed a few times this winter.
大多了🔊 dà duō le much bigger; much heavier 哈尔滨的雪比上海的雪大多了。🔊
Hā'ěrbīn de xuě bǐ Shànghǎi de xuě dà duō le.
The snow in Harbin is much heavier than the snow in Shanghai.
的时候🔊 de shíhou when...; at the time of 旅游的时候，我们要穿舒服的衣服。🔊
Lǚyóu de shíhou, wǒmen yào chuān shūfu de yīfu.
When traveling, we need to wear comfortable clothes.
写得真漂亮🔊 xiě de zhēn piàoliang written really beautifully 你的字写得真漂亮！🔊
Nǐ de zì xiě de zhēn piàoliang!
Your handwriting is really beautiful!
笑着说🔊 xiào zhe shuō to say with a smile 她笑着说她懂了。🔊
Tā xiào zhe shuō tā dǒng le.
She said with a smile that she understood.
不用来🔊 búyòng lái don't need to come 你不用来接我。🔊
Nǐ búyòng lái jiē wǒ.
You don't need to come pick me up.
一件很快乐的事情🔊 yí jiàn hěn kuàilè de shìqing a very happy thing 我觉得帮助人是一件很快乐的事情。🔊
Wǒ juéde bāngzhù rén shì yí jiàn hěn kuàilè de shìqing.
I think helping people is a very happy thing.

Grammar

1. 生病 (shēngbìng) — Separable Verb (离合词)

生病 is a verb-object compound — 生 (to get) plus 病 (illness) — so it behaves like "get sick," not like a single verb. 了 attaches after the whole unit for a completed event (生病了), while the negative uses 没有 rather than 不 because illness is a past event, not a refusal. Anything measuring the illness slots between the halves: 生了三天病. Structure: 生 + 病; affirmative past: Subject + 生病 + 了; negative: Subject + 没有 + 生病.

你生病了，明天不用来公司了。🔊
Nǐ shēngbìng le, míngtiān búyòng lái gōngsī le.
You're sick, so you don't need to come to the office tomorrow.
这个冬天我没有生病。🔊
Zhège dōngtiān wǒ méiyǒu shēngbìng.
I didn't get sick this winter.
他生了三天病，现在好了。🔊
Tā shēng le sān tiān bìng, xiànzài hǎo le.
He was sick for three days, and now he's better.

2. 请 + Time Words + 假 (Asking for Leave)

请假 is another separable verb, and the duration always goes inside, between 请 and 假 — never after it. Use a measure-word duration (一天, 两天, 一个星期) or the question word 几天 to ask how many days. 请几天假 is the standard way to ask "how many days off?" Structure: Subject + 请 + [duration] + 假.

我想请一天假。🔊
Wǒ xiǎng qǐng yì tiān jià.
I'd like to take one day off.
你要请几天假？🔊
Nǐ yào qǐng jǐ tiān jià?
How many days off do you want to take?
她请了一个星期假去旅游。🔊
Tā qǐng le yí ge xīngqī jià qù lǚyóu.
She took a week off to travel.

3. 要 / 不要 / 不 / 不用 (Want, Be Going To, Need)

要 means "want," "be going to," and "need," so context decides. The negatives are what disambiguate: 不要 negates wanting, plain 不 negates the future intention, and 不用 negates necessity. Learners often overuse 不要 for all three; remember that "you don't need to" is 不用, never 不要. Structure: Subject + 要 + noun/verb; negatives: 不要 (don't want), 不 + verb (not going to), 不用 + verb (don't need to).

旅游的时候，我们要穿舒服的衣服。🔊
Lǚyóu de shíhou, wǒmen yào chuān shūfu de yīfu.
When traveling, we need to wear comfortable clothes.
你生病了，明天不用来公司了。🔊
Nǐ shēngbìng le, míngtiān búyòng lái gōngsī le.
You're sick, so you don't need to come to the office tomorrow.
我不要旅游，我要在家休息。🔊
Wǒ bú yào lǚyóu, wǒ yào zài jiā xiūxi.
I don't want to travel, I want to rest at home.

4. 往 + Direction + 走 (Movement Toward)

往 means "in the direction of," so it marks where the motion is heading rather than a destination reached. It pairs most often with 走, but also with 开 (drive) and 看 (look). Note the pattern is fixed 往 + direction + verb — the direction word can never follow the verb here. Structure: 往 + 前/后/左/右 + 走.

你往前走两分钟就到了。🔊
Nǐ wǎng qián zǒu liǎng fēnzhōng jiù dào le.
Walk straight ahead for two minutes and you're there.
到路口往左走。🔊
Dào lùkǒu wǎng zuǒ zǒu.
Turn left at the intersection.
请往右走，医院就在那儿。🔊
Qǐng wǎng yòu zǒu, yīyuàn jiù zài nàr.
Please go right, the hospital is right there.

5. ...的时候 (When / While)

的时候 turns any verb phrase into a "when..." clause, and it always comes first in the sentence, before the main clause. Unlike English, the time clause cannot be tacked on at the end. Compare with ...以前 (before) and ...以后 (after), which follow the same clause-first word order. Structure: [Verb phrase] + 的时候, Main clause.

旅游的时候，我们要穿舒服的衣服。🔊
Lǚyóu de shíhou, wǒmen yào chuān shūfu de yīfu.
When traveling, we need to wear comfortable clothes.
我小的时候住在上海。🔊
Wǒ xiǎo de shíhou zhù zài Shànghǎi.
When I was little I lived in Shanghai.
下雪的时候，我喜欢在家看电视。🔊
Xiàxuě de shíhou, wǒ xǐhuan zài jiā kàn diànshì.
When it snows, I like to watch TV at home.

6. ...以前 (Before Doing Something)

Chinese puts 以前 after the event it refers to, which is the mirror image of English "before going to the UK." The whole 以前 clause sits at the front of the sentence. 以前 on its own also means "in the past / formerly." Structure: [Verb phrase] + 以前, Main clause.

去英国以前，他住在上海。🔊
Qù Yīngguó yǐqián, tā zhù zài Shànghǎi.
Before going to the UK, he lived in Shanghai.
睡觉以前我喜欢看书。🔊
Shuìjiào yǐqián wǒ xǐhuan kàn shū.
Before going to bed I like to read.
出国以前，一定要检查护照。🔊
Chūguó yǐqián, yídìng yào jiǎnchá hùzhào.
Before going abroad, you must check your passport.

7. A 没有 B + Adjective (Not as ... as)

This is the standard negative comparison, and it uses 没有 rather than 不比 in everyday speech. No 很 appears before the adjective. The affirmative counterpart is A 比 B + adjective, so 暑假学校没有一般学校难 flips to 一般学校比暑假学校难. Structure: A + 没有 + B + Adjective.

暑假学校没有一般学校难。🔊
Shǔjià xuéxiào méiyǒu yìbān xuéxiào nán.
Summer school isn't as hard as regular school.
今年的冬天没有去年冷。🔊
Jīnnián de dōngtiān méiyǒu qùnián lěng.
This winter isn't as cold as last year.
上海的雪没有哈尔滨的雪大。🔊
Shànghǎi de xuě méiyǒu Hā'ěrbīn de xuě dà.
The snow in Shanghai isn't as heavy as the snow in Harbin.

8. A 比 B + Adjective + 多了 (Much More Than)

Adding 多了 after the adjective upgrades a plain 比 comparison to "much/far more." 得多 is interchangeable and slightly more formal. Never insert 很 into a 比 sentence — 比 already carries the comparison, so 比...很大 is ungrammatical. Structure: A + 比 + B + Adjective + 多了 (or 得多).

哈尔滨的雪比上海的雪大多了。🔊
Hā'ěrbīn de xuě bǐ Shànghǎi de xuě dà duō le.
The snow in Harbin is much heavier than the snow in Shanghai.
今天比昨天冷多了。🔊
Jīntiān bǐ zuótiān lěng duō le.
Today is much colder than yesterday.
这个考试比那个容易多了。🔊
Zhège kǎoshì bǐ nàge róngyì duō le.
This exam is much easier than that one.

9. A 和 B 一样 (A Is the Same as B)

一样 alone means "the same"; adding an adjective afterwards narrows the sameness to that quality (A 和 B 一样大 = A is as big as B). The negative is A 和 B 不一样. Note the fixed order — 和 links the two items and 一样 always closes the phrase. Structure: A + 和 + B + 一样 (+ Adjective).

夏天和秋天一样。🔊
Xiàtiān hé qiūtiān yíyàng.
Summer and autumn are the same.
他的衣服和我的一样漂亮。🔊
Tā de yīfu hé wǒ de yíyàng piàoliang.
His clothes are just as nice as mine.
这家餐馆和那家餐馆一样好吃。🔊
Zhè jiā cānguǎn hé nà jiā cānguǎn yíyàng hǎochī.
This restaurant is just as good as that one.

10. 没 vs 不 with 下雪 (Past Fact vs. Habit)

没 negates completion — something simply did not occur. 不 negates habit, willingness or general truth, so it pairs naturally with frequency adverbs like 经常. 今年春天没下雪 reports one specific spring; 春天不经常下雪 describes what springs are generally like. Structure: 没 + Verb (a specific event did not happen); 不 + 经常/常 + Verb (it does not habitually happen).

今年春天没下雪。🔊
Jīnnián chūntiān méi xiàxuě.
It didn't snow this spring.
在我的城市，春天不经常下雪。🔊
Zài wǒ de chéngshì, chūntiān bù jīngcháng xiàxuě.
In my city, it doesn't often snow in spring.
昨天没下雪，但是今天下雪了。🔊
Zuótiān méi xiàxuě, dànshì jīntiān xiàxuě le.
It didn't snow yesterday, but it's snowing today.

11. Verb + 得 + Complement (Describing How Well)

得 links a verb to a description of how it is done — 写得很好 (writes well), 说得很快 (speaks fast). If there is an object, either repeat the verb (他写字写得很漂亮) or front the object as a topic (他的字写得很漂亮). Don't confuse this 得 (de) with 的 or 地. Structure: Subject + (Object) + Verb + 得 + Adjective phrase.

你的字写得真漂亮！🔊
Nǐ de zì xiě de zhēn piàoliang!
Your handwriting is really beautiful!
他中文说得很好。🔊
Tā Zhōngwén shuō de hěn hǎo.
He speaks Chinese very well.
她跑得很快。🔊
Tā pǎo de hěn kuài.
She runs very fast.

12. Verb + 着 + Verb (Accompanying Manner)

The first verb becomes background posture or manner while the second is the main action — 笑着说 (say while smiling), 站着吃 (eat standing up). This 着 marks a continuing state, not a completed action, so it never takes 了. Word order is fixed: the manner verb always comes first. Structure: Verb1 + 着 + Verb2.

她笑着说她懂了。🔊
Tā xiào zhe shuō tā dǒng le.
She said with a smile that she understood.
他听着音乐做作业。🔊
Tā tīng zhe yīnyuè zuò zuòyè.
He does his homework while listening to music.
他们聊着天走回家。🔊
Tāmen liáo zhe tiān zǒu huí jiā.
They walked home while chatting.

13. 是一件...的事情 (It Is a ... Kind of Thing)

件 is the measure word for 事情, so the frame is 一件...的事情. The adjective slot usually takes 很 plus an adjective (很快乐, 很难, 很重要). It commonly follows 我觉得 to soften the statement into an opinion. Structure: Topic + 是 + 一件 + Adjective phrase + 的 + 事情.

我觉得帮助人是一件很快乐的事情。🔊
Wǒ juéde bāngzhù rén shì yí jiàn hěn kuàilè de shìqing.
I think helping people is a very happy thing.
学中文是一件很有意思的事情。🔊
Xué Zhōngwén shì yí jiàn hěn yǒuyìsi de shìqing.
Learning Chinese is a very interesting thing.
忘带护照是一件很麻烦的事情。🔊
Wàng dài hùzhào shì yí jiàn hěn máfan de shìqing.
Forgetting to bring your passport is a very troublesome thing.

14. 了 (le) — Completed Action, Not Just Past Tense

了 marks that an action is completed, not that it happened in the past — it can appear in future-oriented sentences too, as long as the action itself will be complete before the next one starts. Compare with change-of-state 了 (see below), which attaches to the end of a sentence to show a new situation. Structure: Verb + 了 (+ Object), can appear inside future-oriented sentences.

明天我吃了饭去运动。🔊
Míngtiān wǒ chī le fàn qù yùndòng.
Tomorrow, after I eat, I'll exercise.
他看了电影才回家。🔊
Tā kàn le diànyǐng cái huí jiā.
He went home only after watching the movie.
我吃了饭就去学校。🔊
Wǒ chī le fàn jiù qù xuéxiào.
I'll go to school right after I eat.

15. Verb + 完 (wán) — Result Complement Meaning 'Finish'

完 is a result complement meaning the action reached completion — "finish doing X." It attaches directly after the verb, and 了 can be added afterward for a completed event: 做完了作业. Structure: Verb + 完 (+ Object), often followed by 了.

我做完作业了。🔊
Wǒ zuò wán zuòyè le.
I've finished my homework.
他吃完饭就去上班。🔊
Tā chī wán fàn jiù qù shàngbān.
He goes to work right after finishing his meal.
你看完这本书了吗？🔊
Nǐ kàn wán zhè běn shū le ma?
Have you finished reading this book?

16. Result Complements — 到 / 完 / 见 (What Happened As a Result)

A result complement is a second verb or adjective directly after the main verb that tells you the outcome — 到 (reached/attained), 完 (finished), 见 (perceived/noticed). 找到 means "searched and found," 看到 means "looked and saw," 看完 means "read/watched to completion." Without the complement, the sentence only says the action happened, not whether it succeeded. Structure: Verb + Result Complement (+ Object).

我找到我的护照了。🔊
Wǒ zhǎodào wǒ de hùzhào le.
I found my passport.
你看到他了吗？🔊
Nǐ kàndào tā le ma?
Did you see him?
我看完那本书了。🔊
Wǒ kàn wán nà běn shū le.
I finished reading that book.

17. 过 (guo) — Past Experience

过 marks that someone has had the experience of doing something at some point, without specifying when — "have ever done X." The negative is 没 + Verb + 过. Structure: Verb + 过; negative: 没 + Verb + 过.

我去过英国。🔊
Wǒ qù guo Yīngguó.
I've been to the UK.
你看到过这样的雪吗？🔊
Nǐ kàndào guo zhèyàng de xuě ma?
Have you ever seen snow like this?
我没吃过饺子。🔊
Wǒ méi chī guo jiǎozi.
I've never eaten dumplings.

18. 着 (zhe) — Ongoing / Continuous State

着 marks an action or state as ongoing — the doing is still in progress. It contrasts with result complements like 到/完, which mark that an action is finished; 着 instead freezes the action mid-way as a backdrop. Structure: Verb + 着 (+ Verb2 / as background state).

他看着电视吃饭。🔊
Tā kàn zhe diànshì chīfàn.
He eats while watching TV.
我试着说中文。🔊
Wǒ shì zhe shuō Zhōngwén.
I'm trying to speak Chinese.
她拿着护照在大堂等。🔊
Tā ná zhe hùzhào zài dàtáng děng.
She's waiting in the lobby holding her passport.

19. 了 (le) for a New State — 'Now,' Not 'Then'

When 了 sits at the end of a sentence (rather than right after the verb), it usually marks a change of state — something that wasn't true before is true now. 我很饿 just describes hunger; 我饿了 says "I've become hungry" (I wasn't a minute ago). The same applies to 猫的身体不错了 (the cat's health has gotten better). Structure: Adjective/Verb + 了 (at the end of the sentence).

猫的身体不错了。🔊
Māo de shēntǐ búcuò le.
The cat's health is better now.
我饿了。🔊
Wǒ è le.
I'm hungry now.
天气冷了，你要多穿衣服。🔊
Tiānqì lěng le, nǐ yào duō chuān yīfu.
The weather has turned cold, you should wear more clothes.

20. 给 (gěi) + Person + 打电话 (Calling Someone)

When saying "call someone," 给 (to/for) introduces the person before the verb 打电话 — never after it. 她打电话给朋友 sounds unnatural; the standard order is 她给朋友打电话. Structure: Subject + 给 + Person + 打电话.

她给朋友打电话。🔊
Tā gěi péngyou dǎ diànhuà.
She calls her friend.
她给北京的朋友打电话。🔊
Tā gěi Běijīng de péngyou dǎ diànhuà.
She calls her friend in Beijing.
请给我打电话。🔊
Qǐng gěi wǒ dǎ diànhuà.
Please call me.

21. Location + Verb Word Order

Chinese places the location phrase before the verb, not after it as in English. "Her friend found the passport under the book" becomes literally "her friend at book's-underneath found passport": 她的朋友在书的下面找到护照。The location always comes right before the verb it modifies. Structure: Subject + 在 + Location + Verb (+ Object).

她的朋友在书的下面找到护照。🔊
Tā de péngyou zài shū de xiàmiàn zhǎodào hùzhào.
Her friend found the passport under the book.
我在酒店大堂等你。🔊
Wǒ zài jiǔdiàn dàtáng děng nǐ.
I'll wait for you in the hotel lobby.
他在商场买了衣服。🔊
Tā zài shāngchǎng mǎi le yīfu.
He bought clothes at the mall.

22. Directional Verbs 回来 / 回去

回 means "return." When you add a place, the place goes directly after 回 — 回北京 (return to Beijing) — and 来/去 (if used) attaches after the place: 回北京来 / 回北京去. 回来北京 / 回去北京 are not standard. Structure: 回 + Place (+ 来/去).

你什么时候回来？🔊
Nǐ shénme shíhou huílái?
When are you coming back?
天晚了，我们回去吧。🔊
Tiān wǎn le, wǒmen huíqù ba.
It's late, let's go back.
他明年要回北京去。🔊
Tā míngnián yào huí Běijīng qù.
He's going back to Beijing next year.

23. 一起 (yìqǐ) + Verb (Doing Something Together)

一起 means "together" and sits directly before the verb it modifies. Structure: Subject(s) + 一起 + Verb.

我们一起玩。🔊
Wǒmen yìqǐ wán.
We hang out/play together.
我们一起聊天。🔊
Wǒmen yìqǐ liáotiān.
We chat together.
我们一起去看电影吧。🔊
Wǒmen yìqǐ qù kàn diànyǐng ba.
Let's go watch a movie together.

24. 有点儿 (yǒudiǎnr) and 儿化 Pronunciation

有点儿 means "a little/somewhat" and usually introduces something undesirable — 有点儿难, 有点儿累. Separately, 儿化 is a Beijing-accent pronunciation feature where 儿 fuses onto the end of a word, e.g. 聊天儿 (liáotiānr) instead of 聊天. Both 玩 and 玩儿 are heard depending on the speaker's accent. Structure: 有点儿 + Adjective (usually negative feeling); 儿化: syllable + 儿 in Beijing-accent speech.

这个问题有点儿难。🔊
Zhège wèntí yǒudiǎnr nán.
This question is a little difficult.
今天我有点儿累。🔊
Jīntiān wǒ yǒudiǎnr lèi.
I'm a little tired today.
我们聊天儿吧。🔊
Wǒmen liáotiānr ba.
Let's chat.

25. 几 + Measure Word: 几天 (jǐ tiān)

几 ("how many / a few," for small countable numbers) attaches directly to a measure word — 几天, never 几个天, since 天 is already its own measure word for days. Structure: 几 + Measure Word (+ Noun).

几天以后我们就回家了。🔊
Jǐ tiān yǐhòu wǒmen jiù huí jiā le.
We'll go home in a few days.
他请了几天假？🔊
Tā qǐng le jǐ tiān jià?
How many days off did he take?
我们要在这儿住几天。🔊
Wǒmen yào zài zhèr zhù jǐ tiān.
We're going to stay here for a few days.

26. 请问 (qǐngwèn) — Polite Question Opener

请问 literally means "may I ask" and is placed at the very start of a question to make it polite — roughly "excuse me." Structure: 请问 + Question.

请问，洗手间在哪儿？🔊
Qǐngwèn, xǐshǒujiān zài nǎr?
Excuse me, where's the restroom?
请问，现在几点？🔊
Qǐngwèn, xiànzài jǐ diǎn?
Excuse me, what time is it now?
请问，这个多少钱？🔊
Qǐngwèn, zhège duōshao qián?
Excuse me, how much is this?

27. 请 + Verb — Polite Invitation (请进, 请坐)

请 before a verb turns it into a polite invitation or request — 请进 ("please come in"), 请坐 ("please sit"), 请说 ("please speak/go ahead"). Structure: 请 + Verb.

请进。🔊
Qǐng jìn.
Please come in.
请坐。🔊
Qǐng zuò.
Please sit down.
请说慢一点。🔊
Qǐng shuō màn yìdiǎn.
Please speak a bit more slowly.
