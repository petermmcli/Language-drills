Grammar

1. 是……的 (shì … de): stressing a detail of a past event

Highlights when, where, how, with whom or why a known past event happened. The fact that the event happened is already shared; the new information goes between 是 and 的, before the verb. 是 can be dropped in speech, but 的 marks the structure. Do not use 过 inside the frame; a pronoun object can sit before 的 (我是在北京认识他们的), or the object can be omitted if obvious. Structure: Subject + 是 + [time / place / manner / companion / purpose] + Verb + 的.

我是十七岁去北京的。🔊
Wǒ shì shíqī suì qù Běijīng de.
I went to Beijing when I was seventeen.
你是和谁一起去的？🔊
Nǐ shì hé shéi yìqǐ qù de?
Who did you go with?
我是昨天来的。🔊
Wǒ shì zuótiān lái de.
I came yesterday.

2. 比 comparisons with 更 and 有更多

Says A is more [adjective] than B. 比 sits between the two things and the adjective stands bare, with no 很, 非常 or 特别 before it. Add 得多 or 多了 for "much more". 更 alone means "even more" without naming B, and 最 means "most". Do not add 里 after the place names. Structure: A + 比 + B + Adjective; A + 比 + B + 有 + 更多 + Noun; A + 比 + B + Adjective + 得多 / 多了.

坐高铁比坐出租车快。🔊
Zuò gāotiě bǐ zuò chūzūchē kuài.
Taking the high-speed train is faster than taking a taxi.
上海比北京有更多的外国人。🔊
Shànghǎi bǐ Běijīng yǒu gèng duō de wàiguórén.
Shanghai has more foreigners than Beijing.
这个比那个贵。🔊
Zhège bǐ nàge guì.
This one is more expensive than that one.

3. 有点 + adjective and 不 + adjective

有点 means "a bit" (often slightly unwanted); 不 simply negates the adjective. 有点 goes right before the adjective or verb phrase it softens. 一点 goes after the adjective in comparisons and requests (快一点). 不太 means "not very", and 太……了 means "too". Structure: Subject + 有点 + Adjective; Subject + 不 + Adjective.

上海菜有点甜，不辣。🔊
Shànghǎi cài yǒudiǎn tián, bú là.
Shanghai food is a bit sweet, not spicy.
今天有点累。🔊
Jīntiān yǒudiǎn lèi.
I am a bit tired today.
这个有点贵。🔊
Zhège yǒudiǎn guì.
This is a bit expensive.

4. 需要 and 不用: need and no need

Expresses need, and the natural way to say there is no need. The negative of 需要 is 不需要 or 不用, not 不要, which means "don't". 不用 is shorter and more relaxed in conversation; 不需要 is a bit more formal or direct. Structure: Subject + 需要 + Verb / Noun; Subject + 不用 / 不需要 + Verb.

不用换飞机。🔊
Búyòng huàn fēijī.
No need to change planes.
你需要这个 Amap。🔊
Nǐ xūyào zhège Amap.
You need this Amap.
你不用等我。🔊
Nǐ búyòng děng wǒ.
You do not need to wait for me.

5. Approximate numbers, 住得 + adjective, 前 + number

Gives approximate numbers and describes how close people live. 十几 means "ten-something", so 十几岁 is the teens. Two adjacent numbers together mean "about" (三四天), and 天 takes no 个. 得 links a verb to how it is done, as in 住得很近. 住得很近 means live near; 住在一起 means live together. Structure: 十几 + 岁; Number + Number + Measure (三四天); 住 + 得 + Adjective; 前 + Number + Measure.

前三四天我住在酒店。🔊
Qián sān sì tiān wǒ zhù zài jiǔdiàn.
For the first three or four days I stayed in a hotel.
我喜欢和朋友住得很近。🔊
Wǒ xǐhuan hé péngyou zhù de hěn jìn.
I like living close to friends.
我有十几个朋友。🔊
Wǒ yǒu shí jǐ ge péngyou.
I have a dozen or so friends.

6. Sound-alikes by tone: 派对 / 排队 / 拍

Distinguishes three words with identical syllables that differ only in tone. Only the tone separates 派, 排 and 拍, so tone drills matter. 派 falls, 排 rises, and 拍 is high and flat. Structure: pài (4th tone) 派对 = party; pái (2nd tone) 排队 = line up; pāi (1st tone) 拍 = film or shoot.

要排队。🔊
Yào páiduì.
You have to queue.
一个派对。🔊
Yí ge pàiduì.
A party.
拍照。🔊
Pāi zhào.
Take a photo.

7. 得 (de) after a verb: how an action is done

Describes how well, how fast or how much an action is done. The thing evaluated is the action, so the verb sits right before 得, and the description goes after it. When the action has an object, the object takes the first verb and the verb is repeated (开车开得很快). Negatives and degree words go after 得 (唱得不好), never before the verb. Structure: Subject + Verb + 得 + description; with an object: Subject + Verb + Object + Verb + 得 + description.

我开车开得很快。🔊
Wǒ kāichē kāi de hěn kuài.
I drive fast.
唱歌唱得不好。🔊
Chànggē chàng de bù hǎo.
I sing badly.
她说得很快。🔊
Tā shuō de hěn kuài.
She speaks fast.

8. Adjective + 得 + result: so ... that ...

Shows a feeling or state so strong that it causes a result. 得 links the feeling to its result, packing cause and result into one sentence. 睁不开 (Verb + 不 + result) means "cannot achieve the result". Without 得 the sentence breaks (never 我很困睁不开眼睛). Structure: Adjective / feeling + 得 + result clause.

我困得睁不开眼睛。🔊
Wǒ kùn de zhēng bu kāi yǎnjing.
I am so sleepy I cannot open my eyes.
我累得不想说话。🔊
Wǒ lèi de bù xiǎng shuōhuà.
I am so tired I do not want to talk.
我饿得不想动。🔊
Wǒ è de bù xiǎng dòng.
I am so hungry I do not want to move.

9. 得 (děi): must, have to

Expresses an obligation you would rather not have but cannot avoid. Same character as 得 (de) but a different sound (third tone, děi), and it comes before the verb rather than after. Compared with 要 (neutral "need to"), 得 adds a flavour of "I don't really want to". It sounds odd with things you enjoy. Structure: Subject + 得 + Verb.

我得去工作。🔊
Wǒ děi qù gōngzuò.
I have to go to work.
你得睡觉了，不要玩手机了。🔊
Nǐ děi shuìjiào le, bú yào wán shǒujī le.
You have to go to sleep; stop playing on your phone.
太晚了，我得回家了。🔊
Tài wǎn le, wǒ děi huíjiā le.
It is too late; I have to go home.

10. 有 / 没有 + 时差 and 更 (comparing time differences)

Describes whether and how big a time difference exists between two places. 时差 is a noun, so use 有 / 没有 rather than an adjective, and join the two places with 和. 更 means "even more" and needs two things in mind; the adjective phrase links to the noun with 的. Do not use 比 with 一样. Amount phrases go before 时差. Structure: A + 和 + B + 有 / 没有 + (number + 个小时) + 时差; A + 和 + B + 有 + 更 + Adjective + 的 + 时差.

上海和香港没有时差。🔊
Shànghǎi hé Xiānggǎng méiyǒu shíchā.
Shanghai and Hong Kong have no time difference.
中国和美国有更大的时差。🔊
Zhōngguó hé Měiguó yǒu gèng dà de shíchā.
China and the USA have a bigger time difference.
上海和北京没有时差。🔊
Shànghǎi hé Běijīng méiyǒu shíchā.
Shanghai and Beijing have no time difference.

11. 让 and 帮 + person + verb: let / help someone do something

Says that something lets, makes or helps someone do an action. The person after 让 or 帮 is both its object and the subject of the next verb. 让 stresses cause (makes it happen); 帮 stresses help. Never put 的 between 帮 + person and the verb (帮我集中, not 帮我的集中). Structure: Subject + 让 / 帮 + Person + Verb.

睡7个小时可以让你集中。🔊
Shuì qī ge xiǎoshí kěyǐ ràng nǐ jízhōng.
Seven hours of sleep lets you focus.
我的同事帮我准备手术。🔊
Wǒ de tóngshì bāng wǒ zhǔnbèi shǒushù.
My colleague helps me prepare for surgery.
他帮我拿东西。🔊
Tā bāng wǒ ná dōngxi.
He helps me carry things.

12. 上个 / 下个 + 星期: last week and next week

Places an event in last week or next week. 上 means "last / previous" and 下 means "next". The time word goes before the verb (time, subject, verb). Future plans usually take 要; finished events take 了. 这个 is "this". Structure: 上个 / 下个 + 星期 + Subject + Verb (time word before the verb).

下个星期我要去上海。🔊
Xià ge xīngqī wǒ yào qù Shànghǎi.
Next week I am going to Shanghai.
上个星期我去了诊所。🔊
Shàng ge xīngqī wǒ qù le zhěnsuǒ.
Last week I went to the clinic.
上个星期我很忙。🔊
Shàng ge xīngqī wǒ hěn máng.
I was busy last week.

13. Duration and clock time: 几点 vs 几个小时

Separates "at what time" from "for how long". A time of day goes before the verb (十点睡觉); a duration goes after it (睡7个小时). 几点 asks the clock time, 几个小时 or 多长时间 asks the length. 多久 asks "how long" and does not fit a clock time. Structure: Time of day + Verb; Verb + Number + 个小时.

你每天睡几个小时？🔊
Nǐ měitiān shuì jǐ ge xiǎoshí?
How many hours do you sleep a day?
我每天晚上十点半睡觉。🔊
Wǒ měitiān wǎnshang shí diǎn bàn shuìjiào.
I go to bed at ten thirty every night.
你几点起床？🔊
Nǐ jǐ diǎn qǐchuáng?
What time do you get up?

14. Means + 去 + purpose, and 起床以后

Describes how you travel to do something and sequences steps in a routine. Two verbs in a row show a means and a goal; without 去 the pair reads as "drive and work" (not 我开车工作). 以后 after an event means "after", and 的时候 means "at the time of". 用 + tool + verb works the same way (我用牙刷刷牙). Structure: Means (Verb phrase) + 去 + Purpose; Event + 以后, + Clause.

你开车去工作。🔊
Nǐ kāichē qù gōngzuò.
You drive to work.
起床以后我洗脸，吃早饭。🔊
Qǐchuáng yǐhòu wǒ xǐ liǎn, chī zǎofàn.
After I get up I wash my face and eat breakfast.
我坐地铁去上班。🔊
Wǒ zuò dìtiě qù shàngbān.
I take the subway to work.
