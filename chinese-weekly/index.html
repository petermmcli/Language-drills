Grammar

1. 就 for emphasis / identification (就是, 就在)

Structure: Subject + 就 + 是/在 + Object. Function: Places 就 before 是 or 在 to emphasize that this — and precisely this — is the right person, thing, or place. Here 就 (jiù) means "exactly," "precisely," or "none other than." 我就是 is the standard way to answer when someone asks for you by name — it's the Chinese equivalent of "that's me" or "speaking." With 在, it stresses that a location is exactly where stated, often implying it's very close or easy to find. Without 就 the sentence is merely factual; with 就 it becomes confirming or reassuring. Structure: Subject + 就 + 是/在 + Object.

谁是王经理？我就是。🔊
Shéi shì Wáng jīnglǐ? Wǒ jiù shì.
Who is Manager Wang? That's me.
我家就在饭馆旁边。🔊
Wǒ jiā jiù zài fànguǎn pángbiān.
My home is right next to the restaurant.

4. Degree modifiers with adjectives (不 / 不太 / 很 / 非常)

Structure: 不 / 不太 / 很 / 非常 + Adjective. Function: Scales an adjective from full negation up to a strong positive. The four degrees run 不 (not) → 不太 (not very, a softened negative) → 很 (very, often just a neutral connector before adjectives) → 非常 (extremely). 不太 is the polite way to express a mild complaint. Watch the tone sandhi: 不 is bù before 舒服 (first tone) but bú before 太 (fourth tone). Note that a bare adjective without any modifier usually implies comparison, which is why 很 is added even when no real emphasis is intended. Structure: 不 / 不太 / 很 / 非常 + Adjective.

这个房间非常舒服。🔊
Zhège fángjiān fēicháng shūfu.
This room is extremely comfortable.

5. A 和 B 一样 vs A 和 B 差不多 (same vs about the same)

Structure: A + 和 + B + 一样 (+ Adj); A + 和 + B + 差不多 (+ Adj). Function: Compares two things as identical (一样) or merely similar (差不多). Explanation: 一样 claims equivalence; 差不多 (literally "differ not much") softens to "roughly the same" — a very common hedge. Both can be followed by an adjective: 一样高, 差不多高. Negate 一样 with 不: 不一样. Structure: A + 和 + B + 一样 / 差不多 (+ Adjective).

我的手机和你的一样。🔊
Wǒ de shǒujī hé nǐ de yíyàng.
My phone is the same as yours.
他们两个人差不多高。🔊
Tāmen liǎng ge rén chàbuduō gāo.
The two of them are about the same height.

6. 有点儿 + Adjective (slightly, with negative feeling)

Structure: Subject + 有点儿 + Adjective/Verb. Function: Expresses that something is "a little" or "somewhat" a certain way, where the speaker finds it undesirable. Explanation: 有点儿 (yǒudiǎnr) goes BEFORE the adjective and carries a complaint or mild dissatisfaction — 有点儿贵 (a bit too expensive), 有点儿伤心 (a bit sad). Contrast with 一点儿, which goes AFTER the adjective and is neutral or comparative: 便宜一点儿 (a little cheaper). Memory trick: 有点儿 = "I have a problem with this"; 一点儿 = just a small amount. Structure: Subject + 有点儿 + Adjective/Verb.

我有点儿伤心。🔊
Wǒ yǒudiǎnr shāngxīn.
I'm a little sad.
这件衣服有点儿贵。🔊
Zhè jiàn yīfu yǒudiǎnr guì.
This piece of clothing is a bit expensive.

7. 结果 as a discourse connector ("as it turned out")

Structure: [Situation], 结果 + [Outcome]. Function: Introduces the actual outcome of a situation, often one that resolves or contradicts what came before. Explanation: At the start of a clause, 结果 (jiéguǒ) works like "in the end," "as it turned out," or "so then." It is not the same as 所以 (therefore), which states logical cause; 结果 reports what actually happened, frequently with a note of surprise. It can also be a plain noun meaning "result": 考试结果 (exam results). Structure: [Situation], 结果 + [Outcome].

结果，房东找到了一个人修地板。🔊
Jiéguǒ, fángdōng zhǎodàole yí ge rén xiū dìbǎn.
As it turned out, the landlord found someone to fix the floor.
我等了一个小时，结果他没来。🔊
Wǒ děngle yí ge xiǎoshí, jiéguǒ tā méi lái.
I waited an hour, and in the end he didn't come.

8. 找到 — resultative complement 到 (success in finding)

Structure: Verb + 到 (+ 了) + Object. Function: Marks that the action was carried out successfully and reached its target. Explanation: 找 (zhǎo) alone means "to look for" — it says nothing about whether you succeeded. Adding the resultative complement 到 (dào) means you actually found it. The same pattern appears in 听到 (heard), 看到 (saw), 买到 (managed to buy). Negate with 没: 没找到 = looked but didn't find. Structure: Verb + 到 (+ 了) + Object.

房东找到了一个人修地板。🔊
Fángdōng zhǎodàole yí ge rén xiū dìbǎn.
The landlord found someone to fix the floor.
我找了半天，可是没找到。🔊
Wǒ zhǎole bàntiān, kěshì méi zhǎodào.
I looked for ages, but didn't find it.
