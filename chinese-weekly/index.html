Grammar

2. Negative result complement — 没有🔊 (méiyǒu) (optional 有🔊 (yǒu)) + 动词🔊 (dòngcí) + 结果🔊 (jiéguǒ) (no 了🔊 (le))

States that the action did not reach its result. 没🔊 (méi) or 没有🔊 (méiyǒu) goes in front of the verb (both are fine) and the 了🔊 (le) of the positive form disappears completely. 了🔊 (le) marks a completed or changed situation, but 没🔊 (méi) already says the event never happened, so there is nothing left to mark. The positive is 动词🔊 (dòngcí) + 结果🔊 (jiéguǒ) + 了🔊 (le); the negative is 没🔊 (méi) + 动词🔊 (dòngcí) + 结果🔊 (jiéguǒ), full stop. The classic learner error is 没听懂了🔊 (méi tīng dǒng le) — drop the 了🔊 (le) every time. Structure: 没🔊 (méi) / 没有🔊 (méiyǒu) + 动词🔊 (dòngcí) + 结果🔊 (jiéguǒ) (+ 宾语🔊 (bīnyǔ)) — never with 了🔊 (le).

我没有听懂你说的话。🔊
Wǒ méiyǒu tīng dǒng nǐ shuō de huà。
I didn't understand what you said.
孩子们没有做完作业。🔊
Háizimen méiyǒu zuò wán zuòyè。
The kids haven't finished their homework.

4. Duration with double 了🔊 (le) — 学了一年了🔊 (xué le yì nián le)

Says how long an ongoing activity has been going on and that it is still going on. The first 了🔊 (le) closes off the stretch of time, and the sentence-final 了🔊 (le) says "up to now", which together give "has been doing it for a year and still is". 学🔊 (xué) and 学习🔊 (xuéxí) are interchangeable here, with 学🔊 (xué) the shorter spoken form. Leaving off the final 了🔊 (le) turns it into a finished past stretch instead. Structure: 动词🔊 (dòngcí) + 了🔊 (le) + 时间🔊 (shíjiān) (+ 宾语🔊 (bīnyǔ)) + 了🔊 (le).

他学了一年了。🔊
Tā xué le yì nián le。
He has been studying for a year.
女儿已经学习十二年汉语了。🔊
Nǚ'ér yǐjīng xuéxí shí'èr nián Hànyǔ le。
My daughter has already been studying Chinese for twelve years.
我在这家医院工作三年了。🔊
Wǒ zài zhè jiā yīyuàn gōngzuò sān nián le。
I've been working at this hospital for three years.

14. Result complement 到🔊 (dào) (dào) with 送🔊 (sòng) (sòng)

到🔊 (dào) attached to a verb says the action arrived somewhere or succeeded. 送到🔊 (sòngdào) is "deliver to (and it gets there)," 看到🔊 (kàndào) is "catch sight of," 听到🔊 (tīngdào) is "manage to hear." The destination follows 到🔊 (dào) directly, with no extra preposition. Without 到🔊 (dào), plain 送🔊 (sòng) only names the act of sending, not its arrival. Structure: Verb + 到🔊 (dào) + destination / object.

好的，我要一盆，可以送到家吗？🔊
Hǎo de, wǒ yào yì pén, kěyǐ sòngdào jiā ma？
Okay, I'll take a pot — can you deliver it to my home?
可以送到十八楼吗？🔊
Kěyǐ sòngdào shíbā lóu ma？
Can you deliver it to the 18th floor?
你听到我说话了吗？🔊
Nǐ tīngdào wǒ shuōhuà le ma？
Did you hear me speaking?

15. Measure words — 盆🔊 (pén) / 枝🔊 (zhī) / 个🔊 (gè)

Chinese requires a measure word between a number and a noun, and the right one depends on the shape or container. 盆🔊 (pén) counts anything in a pot or basin (一盆花🔊 (yì pén huā), 一盆水🔊 (yì pén shuǐ)); 枝🔊 (zhī) — with the wood radical — counts stems and branches (一枝花🔊 (yì zhī huā)). 个🔊 (gè) is the general fallback. Don't confuse 枝🔊 (zhī) with 只🔊 (zhī) (zhī), which counts animals: 一只猫🔊 (yì zhī māo). Structure: Number + measure word + noun.

我要一盆花，不要十枝。🔊
Wǒ yào yì pén huā, bú yào shí zhī。
I want one pot of flowers, not ten stems.
十五块一枝，你要几枝？🔊
Shíwǔ kuài yì zhī, nǐ yào jǐ zhī？
Fifteen yuan a stem — how many do you want?
我要十枝花。🔊
Wǒ yào shí zhī huā。
I want ten flowers.

16. 没有🔊 (méiyǒu) + 机会🔊 (jīhuì) vs 时间🔊 (shíjiān)

时间🔊 (shíjiān) is clock time — hours in the day. 机会🔊 (jīhuì) is an occasion or opportunity for something to happen at all. If California is too hot to ever need a coat, the problem isn't the clock, so 没有机会穿外套🔊 (méiyǒu jīhuì chuān wàitào) is right and 没有时间穿外套🔊 (méiyǒu shíjiān chuān wàitào) shifts the meaning to being in a rush. Structure: Subject + 没有🔊 (méiyǒu) + 机会🔊 (jīhuì) / 时间🔊 (shíjiān) + verb phrase.

在美国没有机会穿外套，因为很热。🔊
Zài Měiguó méiyǒu jīhuì chuān wàitào, yīnwèi hěn rè。
In America there's no chance to wear a coat, because it's hot.
我今天没有时间去花店。🔊
Wǒ jīntiān méiyǒu shíjiān qù huādiàn。
I don't have time to go to the flower shop today.
我今天没有时间。🔊
Wǒ jīntiān méiyǒu shíjiān。
I don't have time today.

17. Chinese address order (big to small)

Chinese addresses run the opposite way from English: city, then district, then street, then the street number, then the building, then the floor. 号🔊 (hào) (hào) marks a number on the street; 号楼🔊 (hào lóu) (hào lóu) is a building within a compound; bare 楼🔊 (lóu) after a number is the floor. So 4号楼🔊 (sì hào lóu) is Building 4 and 8楼🔊 (bā lóu) is the 8th floor — same character, different job. Structure: 市🔊 (shì) + 区🔊 (qū) + 街🔊 (jiē)/路🔊 (lù) + 号🔊 (hào) + 号楼🔊 (hào lóu) + 楼🔊 (lóu).

北京市朝阳区秀水街1号，4号楼，8楼。🔊
Běijīng Shì Cháoyáng Qū Xiùshuǐ Jiē yī hào, sì hào lóu, bā lóu。
Beijing City, Chaoyang District, Xiushui Street No. 1, Building 4, 8th floor.
你家地址是什么？朝阳区秀水街1号。🔊
Nǐ jiā dìzhǐ shì shénme？ Cháoyáng Qū Xiùshuǐ Jiē yī hào。
What's your address? No. 1 Xiushui Street, Chaoyang District.
我家在四号楼，我们在八楼。🔊
Wǒ jiā zài sì hào lóu, wǒmen zài bā lóu。
My home is in Building 4, we're on the 8th floor.

18. 当🔊 (dāng) … 的时候🔊 (de shíhou)

The whole time clause comes first, wrapped by optional 当🔊 (dāng) at the front and obligatory 的时候🔊 (de shíhou) at the end — the 的时候🔊 (de shíhou) is what actually does the work, so 当🔊 (dāng) can be dropped in speech. Everything that happens then follows in the second clause. Structure: (当🔊 (dāng)) + clause + 的时候🔊 (de shíhou), main clause.

当他们不想让妈妈听懂的时候，他们说西班牙语。🔊
Dāng tāmen bù xiǎng ràng māma tīngdǒng de shíhou, tāmen shuō Xībānyáyǔ。
When they don't want their mom to understand, they speak Spanish.
我看病人的时候不看手机。🔊
Wǒ kàn bìngrén de shíhou bú kàn shǒujī。
When I'm seeing patients I don't look at my phone.
上课前我们闲聊了几分钟。🔊
Shàngkè qián wǒmen xiánliáo le jǐ fēnzhōng。
We chatted for a few minutes before class.

19. Duration after the verb

The length of time goes after the verb, not before it — the opposite of place phrases, which go before. With an object present, the duration sits between the verb and the object: 学了一年西班牙语🔊 (xué le yì nián Xībānyáyǔ). Putting the duration first (我一年学了西班牙语🔊 (wǒ yì nián xué le Xībānyáyǔ)) is a classic English-interference error. Structure: Subject + verb + 了🔊 (le) + duration + object.

他学了一年西班牙语。🔊
Tā xué le yì nián Xībānyáyǔ。
He studied Spanish for a year.
我做了三个小时手术。🔊
Wǒ zuò le sān gè xiǎoshí shǒushù。
I did surgery for three hours.
他们从五岁开始学习汉语。🔊
Tāmen cóng wǔ suì kāishǐ xuéxí Hànyǔ。
They started learning Chinese at the age of five.
