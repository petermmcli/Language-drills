2. Grammar — Priority

A. Simple vs continuous subjunctive (απλή / συνεχής υποτακτική)Taught

Pattern να + verb in the aorist stem = simple subjunctive (απλή υποτακτική) να + verb in the present stem = continuous subjunctive (συνεχής υποτακτική) (The teacher did not name the stems out loud in this recording; the stem labels are an added explanation.)

Meaning (what you said, and what the teacher confirmed) Simple: "η δράση έχει τελειώσει" — a single, completed action. Continuous: something ongoing or habitual. Teacher's wording: the continuous is "κάτι που γίνεται πάντα", while the simple is "κάτι που γίνεται μόνο μία φορά".

How and why it works Greek verbs have two stems that express a point of view, not a time. The aorist stem sees the action as one whole event, a single dot on the timeline (to buy, to go, to see). The present stem sees the action as a line or a repeated pattern (to be playing, to be doing regularly). In the subjunctive the time is already decided by the main verb (θέλω, μου αρέσει, πρέπει), so the stem choice only says "one event" or "ongoing/habitual". Practical test: can you picture it as one finished event? Use the aorist. Is it a habit, a general truth, or an ongoing activity? Use the present stem. English translation hides the difference. "I want to buy / I like to play" both use "to + verb". Greek must choose.

Compare / contrast Same main verb, different stem, different picture:

Θέλω να παίξω κιθάρα.I want to play guitar (now, a session).
Θέλω να παίζω κιθάρα.I want to play guitar (as an ongoing thing).

Verbs of liking and habit (μου αρέσει να..., συνήθως, πάντα, κάθε μέρα) lean toward the continuous. Verbs of wanting a specific result (θέλω να αγοράσω, θα πρέπει να πάω) lean toward the simple. Time words help: τώρα / αύριο / μία φορά → simple; πάντα / κάθε μέρα / συχνά → continuous. Some verbs look the same in both stems. κάνω gives να κάνω in both forms, so you cannot hear the difference (your question "μπορώ να κάνω τίποτα;"). Other verbs do change (αγοράζω → αγοράσω; τρώω → φάω; παίζω → παίξω; βλέπω → δω; βρίσκω → βρω; πηγαίνω → πάω).

Common learner mistake No mistake was made in your two example sentences; the teacher accepted both. The usual trap for English speakers is using the present stem everywhere because the English "to + verb" looks the same. A good check: after θέλω, πρέπει, μπορώ, ask "one event or ongoing?". B. Πριν από + time expression — "4 years ago"Self-correction

Pattern πριν από + number + noun in the accusative = "X ago / X before"

Meaning Έκανα εγχείρηση πριν από 4 χρόνια = I had surgery 4 years ago.

How and why it works Πριν από, like many prepositions, takes the accusative. The plural χρόνια (years) is the same in the nominative and accusative, so it is the form you want after πριν από.

6. Best sentences to memorize

1. Simple subjunctive model

Θέλω να αγοράσω ένα καινούριο βιβλίο.I want to buy a new book.

Why useful: The model sentence for the simple subjunctive (single completed action).

2. Continuous subjunctive model

Μου αρέσει να παίζω κιθάρα.I like to play the guitar.

Why useful: The model sentence for the continuous subjunctive (ongoing habit); also the "μου αρέσει να" pattern.

3. πρέπει να — habit

Δεν πρέπει να τρώει το βράδυ.He/she shouldn't eat at night.

Why useful: A teacher-spoken example of πρέπει να with the present stem for a habit.

4. πρέπει να — one trip

Θα πρέπει να πάω Αυστρία.I will have to go to Austria.

Why useful: The same πρέπει να with the aorist stem for one trip. Compare with sentence 3.

5. Chain of aorists

Θέλω να μπορείς να μπεις, να κάνεις κλικ, να βρεις κάτι.I want you to be able to get in, click, and find something.

Why useful: A chain of aorist subjunctives, natural spoken Greek from the teacher.

6. "Ago" with πριν από

Έκανα εγχείρηση πριν από 4 χρόνια.I had surgery 4 years ago.

Why useful: πριν από + accusative for "ago", plus the surgery vocabulary.

7. Pain, no pronoun

Πονάει λίγο.It hurts a little.

Why useful: The one-word, no-pronoun way to talk about pain.

8. Appointment

Έχω ραντεβού με γιατρό. Μπορούμε Παρασκευή;I have a doctor's appointment. Can we do Friday?

Why useful: ραντεβού in a real scheduling sentence; useful for moving lessons.

9. Meeting

Why useful: Συνάντηση. — Meeting. The word for a Zoom or work meeting, distinct from ραντεβού (appointment).

10. Roof / πρόβλημα

Η στέγη έχει πρόβλημα.The roof has a problem.

Why useful: Everyday home vocabulary and the corrected word πρόβλημα (not ζήτημα).

11. Turning things off

Κλείνουν, εννοείς.They shut off, you mean.

Why useful: The correction for turning internet or lights off; also the polite "you mean" (εννοείς).

12. όταν + θα + aorist

Μόνο όταν πάει 200.000 θα πουλήσω.I will sell only when it reaches 200,000.

Why useful: όταν with an aorist-style clause plus θα + aorist for a one-time future action (teacher's sentence).

13. ελπίζω ότι + future

Ελπίζω ότι το Bitcoin θα πάει στο 100.I hope the Bitcoin will go to 100 (thousand).

Why useful: ελπίζω ότι + future; contrast with Ναι, μακάρι.
