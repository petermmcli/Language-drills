Exercise 1: 我的学校生活 — My School Day Easy

Short narrative, single speaker. Listen and answer the questions below.

🔊 Listen to full passage

1. 他每天早上去哪儿？

▸ Pinyin
Tā měi tiān zǎo shang qù nǎr？
▸ answer
他每天早上去学校上课。
Tā měi tiān zǎo shang qù xué xiào shàng kè。
He goes to school for class every morning.

2. 他觉得中文课怎么样？

▸ Pinyin
Tā jué de zhōng wén kè zěn me yàng？
▸ answer
他觉得中文课很有趣，但是也有点儿难。
Tā jué de zhōng wén kè hěn yǒu qù， dàn shì yě yǒu diǎn r nán。
He thinks Chinese class is interesting, but also a little hard.

3. 他为什么有时候听不懂？

▸ Pinyin
Tā wèi shén me yǒu shí hou tīng bu dǒng？
▸ answer
因为老师说得很快。
Yīn wèi lǎo shī shuō de hěn kuài。
Because the teacher speaks quickly.

4. 他下个星期要做什么？

▸ Pinyin
Tā xià ge xīng qī yào zuò shén me？
▸ answer
他下个星期要考试，他要准备。
Tā xià ge xīng qī yào kǎo shì， tā yào zhǔn bèi。
Next week he has a test; he needs to prepare.
▸ Hanzi Transcript
我每天早上去学校上课。🔊 中文课很有趣，但是也有点儿难。🔊 老师说得很快，我有时候听不懂。🔊 下课以后，我回家做作业，复习新词。🔊 下个星期我们要考试，我要准备。🔊 这次的题跟上次不一样。🔊
▸ Pinyin Transcript
Wǒ měi tiān zǎo shang qù xué xiào shàng kè。 Zhōng wén kè hěn yǒu qù， dàn shì yě yǒu diǎn r nán。 Lǎo shī shuō de hěn kuài， wǒ yǒu shí hou tīng bu dǒng。 Xià kè yǐ hòu， wǒ huí jiā zuò zuò yè， fù xí xīn cí。 Xià ge xīng qī wǒ men yào kǎo shì， wǒ yào zhǔn bèi。 Zhè cì de tí gēn shàng cì bù yí yàng。
▸ English Transcript
I go to school for class every morning. Chinese class is interesting, but it's also a little hard. The teacher speaks quickly, and sometimes I can't understand by listening. After class, I go home to do homework and review new words. Next week we have a test, I need to prepare. This time's questions are different from last time.
Exercise 2: 去医院看朋友 — Visiting a Friend in the Hospital Medium

Two-speaker dialogue. Listen and answer the questions below.

🔊 Listen to full dialogue (2 voices)

1. 小李怎么了？

▸ Pinyin
Xiǎo Lǐ zěn me le？
▸ answer
她住院了。
Tā zhù yuàn le。
She's in the hospital.

2. 他们想送小李什么？

▸ Pinyin
Tā men xiǎng sòng Xiǎo Lǐ shén me？
▸ answer
他们想送花和花盆。
Tā men xiǎng sòng huā hé huā pén。
They want to give her flowers and a flower pot.

3. 谁付钱给花店？

▸ Pinyin
Shéi fù qián gěi huā diàn？
▸ answer
他们一起付钱给花店。
Tā men yì qǐ fù qián gěi huā diàn。
They pay the flower shop together.

4. 他们什么时候去医院看她？

▸ Pinyin
Tā men shén me shí hou qù yī yuàn kàn tā？
▸ answer
这个周末。
Zhè ge zhōu mò。
This weekend.
▸ Hanzi Transcript
B: 你知道小李住院了吗？🔊 A: 知道，我们应该去看她。🔊 B: 好啊，我们可以送花给她。🔊 A: 好主意，花店里有花盆，我们买一个吧。🔊 B: 那谁付钱给花店？🔊 A: 我们一起付钱给花店吧。🔊 B: 好，同意吧？🔊 A: 同意。这个周末我们一起去医院看她。🔊 B: 那我们先在网上找一下医院的地址。🔊 A: 好的，我在网上找找。🔊
▸ Pinyin Transcript
B: Nǐ zhī dào Xiǎo Lǐ zhù yuàn le ma？ A: Zhī dào， wǒ men yīng gāi qù kàn tā。 B: Hǎo a， wǒ men kě yǐ sòng huā gěi tā。 A: Hǎo zhǔ yì， huā diàn lǐ yǒu huā pén， wǒ men mǎi yí ge ba。 B: Nà shéi fù qián gěi huā diàn？ A: Wǒ men yì qǐ fù qián gěi huā diàn ba。 B: Hǎo， tóng yì ba？ A: Tóng yì。 Zhè ge zhōu mò wǒ men yì qǐ qù yī yuàn kàn tā。 B: Nà wǒ men xiān zài wǎng shàng zhǎo yí xià yī yuàn de dì zhǐ。 A: Hǎo de， wǒ zài wǎng shàng zhǎo zhao。
▸ English Transcript
B: Do you know Xiao Li is in the hospital? A: I know, we should go see her. B: Okay, we can send her flowers. A: Good idea, the flower shop has flower pots — let's buy one. B: Then who's going to pay the flower shop? A: Let's pay the flower shop together. B: Okay, agreed? A: Agreed. This weekend we'll go to the hospital together to see her. B: Then let's first look up the hospital's address online. A: Okay, I'll look it up online.
Exercise 3: 周末逛街 — A Weekend Out Shopping Harder

Longer narrative, single speaker. More complex grammar. Listen carefully.

🔊 Listen to full passage

1. 周末的时候他决定做什么？

▸ Pinyin
Zhōu mò de shí hou tā jué dìng zuò shén me？
▸ answer
他决定在家休息。
Tā jué dìng zài jiā xiū xi。
He decided to rest at home.

2. 他们买的衬衫怎么样？

▸ Pinyin
Tā men mǎi de chèn shān zěn me yàng？
▸ answer
他们的衬衫是一样的。
Tā men de chèn shān shì yí yàng de。
Their shirts are the same.

3. 花店的花怎么卖？

▸ Pinyin
Huā diàn de huā zěn me mài？
▸ answer
十五块一只。
Shí wǔ kuài yì zhī。
Fifteen yuan each.

4. 他们叫的奶茶怎么到家的？

▸ Pinyin
Tā men jiào de nǎi chá zěn me dào jiā de？
▸ answer
他们在网上点了外卖，奶茶送到家。
Tā men zài wǎng shàng diǎn le wài mài， nǎi chá sòng dào jiā。
They ordered it online, and the milk tea was delivered to their home.

5. 他朋友明年想去哪儿旅游？他自己呢？

▸ Pinyin
Tā péng you míng nián xiǎng qù nǎr lǚ yóu？ Tā zì jǐ ne？
▸ answer
他朋友想去云南，他自己想去加州。
Tā péng you xiǎng qù Yún nán， tā zì jǐ xiǎng qù Jiā zhōu。
His friend wants to go to Yunnan; he himself wants to go to California.
▸ Hanzi Transcript
这个周末我不忙，所以我决定在家休息。🔊 可是我朋友叫我去逛商场，买东西。🔊 我们先去了商场，看到一些衣服和鞋，颜色很好看。🔊 我买了一件衬衫，我朋友也买了一件，我们的衬衫是一样的。🔊 逛完商场以后，我们去市场买水果。🔊 那里的苹果很新鲜，但是有点儿贵。🔊 我们还去了花店，买了几枝花，十五块一只，不贵。🔊 买完东西以后，我们在网上点了外卖，叫了奶茶送到家。🔊 晚上我们聊天，聊明年的旅游计划。🔊 我朋友想去云南旅游，我想去加州看朋友。🔊 虽然地方不一样，但是我们出去玩，很高兴。🔊 我觉得这个周末过得很不错，跟别的周末不一样，很开心。🔊
▸ Pinyin Transcript
Zhè ge zhōu mò wǒ bù máng， suǒ yǐ wǒ jué dìng zài jiā xiū xi。 Kě shì wǒ péng you jiào wǒ qù guàng shāng chǎng， mǎi dōng xi。 Wǒ men xiān qù le shāng chǎng， kàn dào yì xiē yī fu hé xié， yán sè hěn hǎo kàn。 Wǒ mǎi le yí jiàn chèn shān， wǒ péng you yě mǎi le yí jiàn， wǒ men de chèn shān shì yí yàng de。 Guàng wán shāng chǎng yǐ hòu， wǒ men qù shì chǎng mǎi shuǐ guǒ。 Nà li de píng guǒ hěn xīn xiān， dàn shì yǒu diǎn r guì。 Wǒ men hái qù le huā diàn， mǎi le jǐ zhī huā， shí wǔ kuài yì zhī， bú guì。 Mǎi wán dōng xi yǐ hòu， wǒ men zài wǎng shàng diǎn le wài mài， jiào le nǎi chá sòng dào jiā。 Wǎn shang wǒ men liáo tiān， liáo míng nián de lǚ yóu jì huà。 Wǒ péng you xiǎng qù Yún nán lǚ yóu， wǒ xiǎng qù Jiā zhōu kàn péng you。 Suī rán dì fang bù yí yàng， dàn shì wǒ men chū qù wán， hěn gāo xìng。 Wǒ jué de zhè ge zhōu mò guò de hěn bú cuò， gēn bié de zhōu mò bù yí yàng， hěn kāi xīn。
▸ English Transcript
I'm not busy this weekend, so I decided to rest at home. But my friend asked me to go to the mall to buy things. We went to the mall first, saw some clothes and shoes — the colors were very nice. I bought a shirt, and my friend also bought one — our shirts are the same. After finishing at the mall, we went to the market to buy fruit. The apples there were very fresh, but a little expensive. We also went to a flower shop and bought a few stems of flowers, fifteen yuan each — not expensive. After finishing shopping, we ordered takeout online and had milk tea delivered to our home. In the evening we chatted, talking about next year's travel plans. My friend wants to travel to Yunnan, and I want to go to California to see a friend. Although the places are different, we went out and had fun — very happy. I think this weekend turned out really well — different from other weekends, very happy.
