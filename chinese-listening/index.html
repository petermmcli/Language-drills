Exercise 1: 看医生 — Seeing the Doctor Easy

Short narrative, single speaker. Listen and answer the questions below.

🔊 Listen to full passage

1. 他回来以后觉得怎么样？

▸ Pinyin
tā huí lái yǐ hòu jué de zěn me yàng？
▸ answer
他觉得不舒服，肝也有点儿疼。
tā jué de bù shū fú， gān yě yǒu diǎn ér téng。
He felt unwell, and his liver hurt a little.

2. 医生问他喝了什么酒？

▸ Pinyin
yī shēng wèn tā hē le shén me jiǔ？
▸ answer
医生问他喝的是白酒还是红酒。
yī shēng wèn tā hē de shì bái jiǔ hái shì hóng jiǔ。
The doctor asked whether it was baijiu or red wine.

3. 他喝了什么酒？喝得多吗？

▸ Pinyin
tā hē le shén me jiǔ？ hē dé duō ma？
▸ answer
他喝了一点儿鸡尾酒，喝得不多。
tā hē le yì diǎn ér jī wěi jiǔ， hē dé bù duō。
He had a little cocktail; he didn't drink much.

4. 医生让他做什么？

▸ Pinyin
yī shēng ràng tā zuò shén me？
▸ answer
医生让他多休息，这几天不要喝酒。
yī shēng ràng tā duō xiū xī， zhè jǐ tiān bú yào hē jiǔ。
The doctor told him to rest more and not drink these next few days.

5. 他的病要多长时间才能好？

▸ Pinyin
tā de bìng yào duō zhǎng shí jiān cái néng hǎo？
▸ answer
一个星期就可以好了。
yí gè xīng qī jiù kě yǐ hǎo le。
One week would be enough.
▸ Hanzi Transcript
我上星期出差，工作很多，也很累。🔊 回来以后，我觉得不舒服，肝也有点儿疼，所以我去医院看病。🔊 医生问我最近是不是喝了很多酒，是白酒还是红酒。🔊 我说我喝了一点儿鸡尾酒，可是喝得不多。🔊 医生说我要多休息，这几天不要喝酒。🔊 我问医生要多长时间才能好。🔊 医生说一个星期就可以了，让我放心。🔊
▸ Pinyin Transcript
wǒ shàng xīng qī chū chāi， gōng zuò hěn duō， yě hěn lèi。 huí lái yǐ hòu， wǒ jué de bù shū fú， gān yě yǒu diǎn ér téng， suǒ yǐ wǒ qù yī yuàn kàn bìng。 yī shēng wèn wǒ zuì jìn shì bú shì hē le hěn duō jiǔ， shì bái jiǔ hái shì hóng jiǔ。 wǒ shuō wǒ hē le yì diǎn ér jī wěi jiǔ， kě shì hē dé bù duō。 yī shēng shuō wǒ yào duō xiū xī， zhè jǐ tiān bú yào hē jiǔ。 wǒ wèn yī shēng yào duō zhǎng shí jiān cái néng hǎo。 yī shēng shuō yí gè xīng qī jiù kě yǐ le， ràng wǒ fàng xīn。
▸ English Transcript
Last week I went on a business trip; I had a lot of work and got very tired. After I got back, I felt unwell — my liver even hurt a little — so I went to the hospital to see a doctor. The doctor asked me whether I'd been drinking a lot lately, and whether it was baijiu or red wine. I said I'd had a little cocktail, but hadn't drunk much. The doctor said I should rest more and not drink these next few days. I asked the doctor how long it would take to get better. The doctor said one week would be enough, and told me not to worry.
Exercise 2: 去图书馆 — Going to the Library Medium

Two-speaker dialogue. Listen and answer the questions below.

🔊 Listen to full dialogue (2 voices)

1. A为什么想去图书馆？

▸ Pinyin
A wèi shén me xiǎng qù tú shū guǎn？
▸ answer
A想去图书馆借书。
A xiǎng qù tú shū guǎn jiè shū。
A wants to go to the library to borrow a book.

2. 走路去图书馆要多长时间？

▸ Pinyin
zǒu lù qù tú shū guǎn yào duō zhǎng shí jiān？
▸ answer
走路大概要十分钟。
zǒu lù dà gài yào shí fēn zhōng。
On foot it takes about ten minutes.

3. A要怎么还书？

▸ Pinyin
A yào zěn me huán shū？
▸ answer
A只要把书给那边的人就可以了。
A zhǐ yào bǎ shū gěi nà biān de rén jiù kě yǐ le。
A just needs to give the book to the person there.

4. 书可以借多久？

▸ Pinyin
shū kě yǐ jiè duō jiǔ？
▸ answer
可以借两个星期。
kě yǐ jiè liǎng gè xīng qī。
You can borrow it for two weeks.

5. B最后要做什么？

▸ Pinyin
B zuì hòu yào zuò shén me？
▸ answer
B要陪A一起去图书馆。
B yào péi A yì qǐ qù tú shū guǎn。
B will go to the library together with A.
▸ Hanzi Transcript
A: 你知道去图书馆要怎么走吗？我想去借书。🔊 B: 要开车还是走路？如果开车，你往前开一点儿，到路口往左转就到了。🔊 A: 走路要多长时间？🔊 B: 走路大概十分钟，不用开车，很近。🔊 A: 好，那我走路去。到了以后，要怎么还书？🔊 B: 很简单，你把书给那边的人就可以了。🔊 A: 我可以借多久？🔊 B: 可以借两个星期，对吧？我记得是这样。🔊 A: 行吗？我怕我忘了还书，会不会有问题？🔊 B: 没问题，图书馆会提前告诉你，你放心吧。🔊 A: 好，那我们走吧，我现在就想去。🔊 B: 行，我陪你一起去。🔊
▸ Pinyin Transcript
A: nǐ zhī dào qù tú shū guǎn yào zěn me zǒu ma？ wǒ xiǎng qù jiè shū。 B: yào kāi chē hái shì zǒu lù？ rú guǒ kāi chē， nǐ wǎng qián kāi yì diǎn ér， dào lù kǒu wǎng zuǒ zhuǎn jiù dào le。 A: zǒu lù yào duō zhǎng shí jiān？ B: zǒu lù dà gài shí fēn zhōng， bù yòng kāi chē， hěn jìn。 A: hǎo， nà wǒ zǒu lù qù。 dào le yǐ hòu， yào zěn me huán shū？ B: hěn jiǎn dān， nǐ bǎ shū gěi nà biān de rén jiù kě yǐ le。 A: wǒ kě yǐ jiè duō jiǔ？ B: kě yǐ jiè liǎng gè xīng qī， duì ba？ wǒ jì de shì zhè yàng。 A: xíng ma？ wǒ pà wǒ wàng le huán shū， huì bú huì yǒu wèn tí？ B: méi wèn tí， tú shū guǎn huì tí qián gào sù nǐ， nǐ fàng xīn ba。 A: hǎo， nà wǒ men zǒu ba， wǒ xiàn zài jiù xiǎng qù。 B: xíng， wǒ péi nǐ yì qǐ qù。
▸ English Transcript
A: Do you know how to get to the library? I want to borrow a book. B: Driving or walking? If you drive, go forward a bit, and turn left at the intersection — you'll be there. A: How long does it take on foot? B: About ten minutes on foot — no need to drive, it's very close. A: Okay, I'll walk then. Once I get there, how do I return a book? B: It's simple — just give the book to the person over there. A: How long can I borrow it for? B: You can borrow it for two weeks, right? That's how I remember it. A: Is that okay? I'm worried I'll forget to return it — will that be a problem? B: No problem, the library will remind you ahead of time, don't worry. A: Okay, let's go then — I want to go right now. B: Sure, I'll go with you.
Exercise 3: 去超市买东西 — A Supermarket Run Harder

Longer narrative, single speaker. More complex grammar. Listen carefully.

🔊 Listen to full passage

1. 她今天晚上要做什么？

▸ Pinyin
tā jīn tiān wǎn shàng yào zuò shén me？
▸ answer
她要给客人准备一顿特色的晚饭。
tā yào gěi kè rén zhǔn bèi yī dùn tè sè de wǎn fàn。
She needs to prepare a special dinner for the guests.

2. 她为什么买了一点儿肝？

▸ Pinyin
tā wèi shén me mǎi le yì diǎn ér gān？
▸ answer
因为客人喜欢吃肝。
yīn wèi kè rén xǐ huān chī gān。
Because the guests like to eat liver.

3. 服务员问她什么，她怎么回答？

▸ Pinyin
fú wù yuán wèn tā shén me， tā zěn me huí dá？
▸ answer
服务员问她要不要试一下酒，她拒绝了，因为她已经知道要买红酒。
fú wù yuán wèn tā yào bù yào shì yī xià jiǔ， tā jù jué le， yīn wèi tā yǐ jīng zhī dào yào mǎi hóng jiǔ。
The attendant asked if she wanted to try the wine; she declined because she already knew she wanted red wine.

4. 她为什么只买了四分之一的东西？

▸ Pinyin
tā wèi shén me zhǐ mǎi le sì fēn zhī yī de dōng xī？
▸ answer
因为排到她的时候，她发现钱不够。
yīn wèi pái dào tā de shí hòu， tā fā xiàn qián bù gòu。
Because when it was her turn, she realized she didn't have enough money.

5. 客人觉得她做的饭怎么样？

▸ Pinyin
kè rén jué de tā zuò de fàn zěn me yàng？
▸ answer
客人觉得很好吃。
kè rén jué de hěn hǎo chī。
The guests thought it was delicious.
▸ Hanzi Transcript
今天晚上家里有客人，我要去超市买一些东西，准备一顿特色的晚饭。🔊 我先去买肉，看到牛肉在打折，就买了一点儿，也买了一点儿肝，因为客人喜欢吃肝。🔊 然后我去找酒，超市里有白酒，红酒，还有做好的鸡尾酒，我不知道买哪种好。🔊 服务员过来问我要不要试一下，我拒绝了，因为我已经知道自己要买红酒。🔊 我拿了一瓶红酒，又拿了一点儿水果，准备饭后给客人吃。🔊 排队的人很多，我拍了一张照片，发给朋友，说这家超市今天人特别多。🔊 排到我的时候，我才发现钱不够，只好把一些东西放回去，只买了四分之一的东西。🔊 服务员说没关系，下次再来，他们这儿每天都有新鲜的东西。🔊 回家的路上，我用手机拍视频，记录今天买的特色菜的做法。🔊 到家以后，客人已经到了，我赶快把东西放好，开始做饭。🔊 做完饭，我对客人说今天的菜不多，可是都是我们这儿的特色，让他们吃吃看。🔊 客人说看起来很好吃，谢谢我准备了这么多。🔊
▸ Pinyin Transcript
jīn tiān wǎn shàng jiā lǐ yǒu kè rén， wǒ yào qù chāo shì mǎi yī xiē dōng xī， zhǔn bèi yī dùn tè sè de wǎn fàn。 wǒ xiān qù mǎi ròu， kàn dào niú ròu zài dǎ zhé， jiù mǎi le yì diǎn ér， yě mǎi le yì diǎn ér gān， yīn wèi kè rén xǐ huān chī gān。 rán hòu wǒ qù zhǎo jiǔ， chāo shì lǐ yǒu bái jiǔ， hóng jiǔ， hái yǒu zuò hǎo de jī wěi jiǔ， wǒ bù zhī dào mǎi nǎ zhǒng hǎo。 fú wù yuán guò lái wèn wǒ yào bù yào shì yī xià， wǒ jù jué le， yīn wèi wǒ yǐ jīng zhī dào zì jǐ yào mǎi hóng jiǔ。 wǒ ná le yī píng hóng jiǔ， yòu ná le yì diǎn ér shuǐ guǒ， zhǔn bèi fàn hòu gěi kè rén chī。 pái duì de rén hěn duō， wǒ pāi le yī zhāng zhào piān， fā gěi péng yǒu， shuō zhè jiā chāo shì jīn tiān rén tè bié duō。 pái dào wǒ de shí hòu， wǒ cái fā xiàn qián bù gòu， zhǐ hǎo bǎ yī xiē dōng xī fàng huí qù， zhǐ mǎi le sì fēn zhī yī de dōng xī。 fú wù yuán shuō méi guān xì， xià cì zài lái， tā men zhè ér měi tiān dōu yǒu xīn xiān de dōng xī。 huí jiā de lù shàng， wǒ yòng shǒu jī pāi shì pín， jì lù jīn tiān mǎi de tè sè cài de zuò fǎ。 dào jiā yǐ hòu， kè rén yǐ jīng dào le， wǒ gǎn kuài bǎ dōng xī fàng hǎo， kāi shǐ zuò fàn。 zuò wán fàn， wǒ duì kè rén shuō jīn tiān de cài bù duō， kě shì dōu shì wǒ men zhè ér de tè sè， ràng tā men chī chī kàn。 kè rén shuō kàn qǐ lái hěn hǎo chī， xiè xiè wǒ zhǔn bèi le zhè me duō。
▸ English Transcript
Tonight we have guests at home, so I need to go to the supermarket to buy some things and prepare a special dinner. I went to buy meat first — I saw beef on sale, so I bought a little, and I also bought a little liver, because the guests like to eat liver. Then I went to look for wine — the supermarket had baijiu, red wine, and ready-made cocktails; I didn't know which one to get. An attendant came over and asked if I wanted to try one; I declined, because I already knew I wanted red wine. I took a bottle of red wine, and also took some fruit, to give the guests after the meal. There were a lot of people in line; I took a photo and sent it to a friend, saying the supermarket was especially crowded today. When it was my turn, I realized I didn't have enough money, so I had to put some things back — I only bought a quarter of what I wanted. The attendant said it was fine, come back next time, they have fresh things every day there. On the way home, I used my phone to film a video, recording how to make today's special dish. When I got home, the guests had already arrived, so I quickly put the things away and started cooking. After cooking, I told the guests that there wasn't much food tonight, but it was all our local specialty, and told them to try it. The guests said it looked delicious, and thanked me for preparing so much.
