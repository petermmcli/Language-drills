Exercise 1: 去银行换钱 — Going to the Bank to Exchange Money Easy

Short narrative, single speaker. Listen and answer the questions below.

🔊 Listen to full passage

1. 他去银行做什么？

▸ Pinyin
Tā qù yín háng zuò shén me？
▸ answer
他去银行换美元。
Tā qù yín háng huàn měi yuán。
He goes to the bank to exchange US dollars.

2. 银行在他家附近吗？

▸ Pinyin
Yín háng zài tā jiā fù jìn ma？
▸ answer
对，走路十分钟就到。
Duì， zǒu lù shí fēn zhōng jiù dào。
Yes, a 10-minute walk gets there.

3. 前台的人让他做什么？

▸ Pinyin
Qián tái de rén ràng tā zuò shén me？
▸ answer
让他把名字写下来，还要看他的护照。
Ràng tā bǎ míng zì xiě xià lái， hái yào kàn tā de hù zhào。
The counter staff has him write down his name and wants to see his passport.

4. 他一共换了多少美元？

▸ Pinyin
Tā yī gòng huàn le duō shǎo měi yuán？
▸ answer
他一共换了二百美元。
Tā yī gòng huàn le èr bǎi měi yuán。
He exchanged 200 US dollars in total.
▸ Hanzi Transcript
今天我去银行换美元。🔊 银行在我家附近，走路十分钟就到。🔊 我先到前台，前台的人很好。🔊 他让我把名字写下来，还要看我的护照。🔊 我把护照给他，他看了一下，说没问题。🔊 我一共换了二百美元，他给了我现金。🔊 我谢谢他，他说不客气。🔊
▸ Pinyin Transcript
Jīn tiān wǒ qù yín háng huàn měi yuán。 Yín háng zài wǒ jiā fù jìn， zǒu lù shí fēn zhōng jiù dào。 Wǒ xiān dào qián tái， qián tái de rén hěn hǎo。 Tā ràng wǒ bǎ míng zì xiě xià lái， hái yào kàn wǒ de hù zhào。 Wǒ bǎ hù zhào gěi tā， tā kàn le yī xià， shuō méi wèn tí。 Wǒ yī gòng huàn le èr bǎi měi yuán， tā gěi le wǒ xiàn jīn。 Wǒ xiè xiè tā， tā shuō bú kè qì。
▸ English Transcript
Today I'm going to the bank to exchange US dollars. The bank is near my house — a 10-minute walk gets there. I go to the counter first; the counter staff are very nice. He has me write down my name, and also wants to see my passport. I give him my passport, he looks at it, and says no problem. I exchanged 200 US dollars in total; he gave me cash. I thank him, and he says you're welcome.
Exercise 2: 外卖到了 — The Delivery Has Arrived Medium

Two-speaker dialogue. Listen and answer the questions below.

🔊 Listen to full dialogue (2 voices)

1. 骑手在哪儿？

▸ Pinyin
Qí shǒu zài nǎ ér？
▸ answer
骑手在门口。
Qí shǒu zài mén kǒu。
The rider is at the door.

2. A 是在哪儿下载的？

▸ Pinyin
A Shì zài nǎ ér xià zài de？
▸ answer
A 是在美团下载的。
A Shì zài měi tuán xià zài de。
A downloaded it through Meituan.

3. A 要用什么付钱？

▸ Pinyin
A Yào yòng shén me fù qián？
▸ answer
A 要用手机付钱。
A Yào yòng shǒu jī fù qián。
A will pay with her phone.

4. 谁说“辛苦了”？

▸ Pinyin
Shuí shuō“ xīn kǔ le”？
▸ answer
A 说“辛苦了”。
A Shuō“ xīn kǔ le”。
A says "you've worked hard."
▸ Hanzi Transcript
B: 喂，你好，我在门口，你的外卖到了。🔊 A: 好的，谢谢你，我一会儿过来。🔊 B: 请问你的收货地址是这个吗？🔊 A: 对，地址没问题，谢谢你。🔊 B: 你是在美团下载的还是在饿了么下载的？🔊 A: 我在美团下载的，也在淘宝闪购看了一下别的东西。🔊 B: 你确定地址写好了吗？🔊 A: 确定了，我下载好美团以后就选好了店，写好了地址。🔊 B: 好，那你要用什么付钱？🔊 A: 我要用手机付钱，一点问题也没有。🔊 B: 好的，东西给你了，麻烦你收一下。🔊 A: 谢谢你，辛苦了。🔊
▸ Pinyin Transcript
B: Wèi， nǐ hǎo， wǒ zài mén kǒu， nǐ de wài mài dào le。 A: Hǎo de， xiè xiè nǐ， wǒ yī huì er guò lái。 B: Qǐng wèn nǐ de shōu huò dì zhǐ shì zhè ge ma？ A: Duì， dì zhǐ méi wèn tí， xiè xiè nǐ。 B: Nǐ shì zài měi tuán xià zài de hái shì zài è le me xià zài de？ A: Wǒ zài měi tuán xià zài de， yě zài táo bǎo shǎn gòu kàn le yī xià bié de dōng xī。 B: Nǐ què dìng dì zhǐ xiě hǎo le ma？ A: Què dìng le， wǒ xià zài hǎo měi tuán yǐ hòu jiù xuǎn hǎo le diàn， xiě hǎo le dì zhǐ。 B: Hǎo， nà nǐ yào yòng shén me fù qián？ A: Wǒ yào yòng shǒu jī fù qián， yì diǎn wèn tí yě méi yǒu。 B: Hǎo de， dōng xī gěi nǐ le， má fán nǐ shōu yī xià。 A: Xiè xiè nǐ， xīn kǔ le。
▸ English Transcript
B: Hello, I'm at the door, your delivery has arrived. A: OK, thank you, I'll come over in a moment. B: Excuse me, is this your delivery address? A: Yes, the address is correct, thank you. B: Did you download it through Meituan or through Eleme? A: I downloaded it through Meituan, and also checked some other things on Taobao Shangou. B: Are you sure you finished writing the address? A: Confirmed — after I finished downloading Meituan, I picked the store and wrote the address. B: OK, so what will you use to pay? A: I'll pay with my phone, no problem at all. B: OK, here it is, please take it. A: Thank you, you've worked hard.
Exercise 3: 生病和回老家 — Getting Sick and Going Home Harder

Longer narrative, single speaker. More complex grammar. Listen carefully.

🔊 Listen to full passage

1. 他十几岁的时候做什么工作？

▸ Pinyin
Tā shí jǐ suì de shí hòu zuò shén me gōng zuò？
▸ answer
他做过外卖骑手，送过外卖。
Tā zuò guò wài mài qí shǒu， sòng guò wài mài。
He worked as a delivery rider and delivered takeout.

2. 医生说他身体里有什么？

▸ Pinyin
Yī shēng shuō tā shēn tǐ lǐ yǒu shén me？
▸ answer
医生说他身体里有一个肿瘤。
Yī shēng shuō tā shēn tǐ lǐ yǒu yí gè zhǒng liú。
The doctor said there was a tumor inside his body.

3. 他为什么没有别的选择？

▸ Pinyin
Tā wèi shén me méi yǒu bié de xuǎn zé？
▸ answer
因为如果不去掉肿瘤，以后会有问题。
Yīn wèi rú guǒ bù qù diào zhǒng liú， yǐ hòu huì yǒu wèn tí。
Because if the tumor wasn't removed, there would be problems later.

4. 他为什么决定回老家？

▸ Pinyin
Tā wèi shén me jué dìng huí lǎo jiā？
▸ answer
因为中秋节和国庆节调休，假期比较长。
Yīn wèi zhōng qiū jié hé guó qìng jié diào xiū， jià qī bǐ jiào zhǎng。
Because Mid-Autumn Festival and National Day have adjusted rest days this year, so the holiday is fairly long.

5. 老家的天气怎么样？

▸ Pinyin
Lǎo jiā de tiān qì zěn me yàng？
▸ answer
老家的天气很好，看不到那么多车和人。
Lǎo jiā de tiān qì hěn hǎo， kàn bú dào nà me duō chē hé rén。
The weather at home is very nice — you can't see so many cars and people.
▸ Hanzi Transcript
我十几岁的时候做过外卖骑手，送过外卖。🔊 那个时候我每天收货，看地址，送货地址常常很远。🔊 然后我去医院检查身体，医生说要看一下。🔊 医生让我做MRI 检查，我有点儿怕。🔊 检查以后，医生说我身体里有一个肿瘤，但是不大。🔊 如果不去掉，以后会有问题，所以医生要把它去掉。🔊 我没有别的选择，就同意了。🔊 手术以后，我在医院休息了几天。🔊 医生说我很好，以后不用再检查了。🔊 我有过一次，现在记得，还有点儿怕。🔊 今年中秋节和国庆节调休，假期比较长。🔊 所以我决定回老家，看看爸爸妈妈。🔊 老家的天气很好，那儿看不到那么多车和人。🔊 这次回老家，我觉得特别开心。🔊
▸ Pinyin Transcript
Wǒ shí jǐ suì de shí hòu zuò guò wài mài qí shǒu， sòng guò wài mài。 Nà ge shí hòu wǒ měi tiān shōu huò， kàn dì zhǐ， sòng huò dì zhǐ cháng cháng hěn yuǎn。 Rán hòu wǒ qù yī yuàn jiǎn chá shēn tǐ， yī shēng shuō yào kàn yī xià。 Yī shēng ràng wǒ zuò MRI jiǎn chá， wǒ yǒu diǎn ér pà。 Jiǎn chá yǐ hòu， yī shēng shuō wǒ shēn tǐ lǐ yǒu yí gè zhǒng liú， dàn shì bù dà。 Rú guǒ bù qù diào， yǐ hòu huì yǒu wèn tí， suǒ yǐ yī shēng yào bǎ tā qù diào。 Wǒ méi yǒu bié de xuǎn zé， jiù tóng yì le。 Shǒu shù yǐ hòu， wǒ zài yī yuàn xiū xī le jǐ tiān。 Yī shēng shuō wǒ hěn hǎo， yǐ hòu bù yòng zài jiǎn chá le。 Wǒ yǒu guò yī cì， xiàn zài jì de， hái yǒu diǎn ér pà。 Jīn nián zhōng qiū jié hé guó qìng jié diào xiū， jià qī bǐ jiào zhǎng。 Suǒ yǐ wǒ jué dìng huí lǎo jiā， kàn kàn bà bà mā mā。 Lǎo jiā de tiān qì hěn hǎo， nà ér kàn bú dào nà me duō chē hé rén。 Zhè cì huí lǎo jiā， wǒ jué de tè bié kāi xīn。
▸ English Transcript
When I was in my teens I worked as a delivery rider, delivering takeout. At that time I received packages every day and checked addresses — delivery addresses were often far away. Later I went to the hospital to check my body; the doctor said they needed to take a look. The doctor had me get an MRI checkup; I was a little scared. After the checkup, the doctor said there was a tumor inside my body, but it wasn't big. If it wasn't removed, there would be problems later, so the doctor needed to remove it. I had no other choice, so I agreed. After the surgery, I rested in the hospital for a few days. The doctor said I was fine and wouldn't need to be checked again. I've had this experience once before; I still remember it now, and I'm still a little scared. This year Mid-Autumn Festival and National Day have adjusted rest days, so the holiday is fairly long. So I decided to go back to my hometown to see my mom and dad. The weather at home is very nice; you can't see so many cars and people there. This trip home, I feel especially happy.
