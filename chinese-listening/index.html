Exercise 1: 公园的下午 — An Afternoon at the Park Easy

Short narrative, single speaker. Listen and answer the questions below.

🔊 Listen to full passage

1. 今天天气怎么样？

▸ Pinyin
Jīn tiān tiān qì zěn me yàng？
▸ answer
今天是春天，天气不错。
Jīn tiān shì chūn tiān， tiān qì bú cuò。
Today is spring; the weather isn't bad.

2. 公园在哪儿？

▸ Pinyin
Gōng yuán zài nǎr？
▸ answer
公园就在他家旁边，走路五分钟就到。
Gōng yuán jiù zài tā jiā páng biān， zǒu lù wǔ fēn zhōng jiù dào。
The park is right next to his house; on foot it's five minutes away.

3. 他跟谁一起去公园？

▸ Pinyin
Tā gēn shéi yì qǐ qù gōng yuán？
▸ answer
他和朋友一起去公园玩。
Tā hé péng you yì qǐ qù gōng yuán wán。
My friend and I go to the park together to play.

4. 他们玩了多长时间？

▸ Pinyin
Tā men wán le duō cháng shí jiān？
▸ answer
他们玩了差不多一个小时。
Tā men wán le chà bu duō yí ge xiǎo shí。
They played for about an hour.
▸ Hanzi Transcript
今天是春天，天气不错。🔊 公园就在我家旁边，走路五分钟就到。🔊 我和朋友一起去公园玩。🔊 我们在公园看树，也看鸟。🔊 然后天阴了，可能要下小雨。🔊 虽然天气不太舒服，我们还是很开心。🔊 玩了差不多一个小时，我们就回家了。🔊
▸ Pinyin Transcript
Jīn tiān shì chūn tiān， tiān qì bú cuò。 Gōng yuán jiù zài wǒ jiā páng biān， zǒu lù wǔ fēn zhōng jiù dào。 Wǒ hé péng you yì qǐ qù gōng yuán wán。 Wǒ men zài gōng yuán kàn shù， yě kàn niǎo。 Rán hòu tiān yīn le， kě néng yào xià xiǎo yǔ。 Suī rán tiān qì bú tài shū fu， wǒ men hái shi hěn kāi xīn。 Wán le chà bu duō yí ge xiǎo shí， wǒ men jiù huí jiā le。
▸ English Transcript
Today is spring; the weather isn't bad. The park is right next to my house; on foot it's five minutes away. My friend and I go to the park together to play. We look at trees in the park, and also watch birds. Then it got cloudy; it might rain lightly. Although the weather isn't very pleasant, we're still happy. After playing for about an hour, we went home.
Exercise 2: 晚饭在哪儿吃？ — Where to Eat Dinner Medium

Two-speaker dialogue. Listen and answer the questions below.

🔊 Listen to full dialogue (2 voices)

1. 他们晚饭要去哪儿吃？

▸ Pinyin
Tā men wǎn fàn yào qù nǎr chī？
▸ answer
他们要去A家旁边的那家饭店吃饭。
Tā men yào qù A jiā páng biān de nà jiā fàn diàn chī fàn。
They're going to eat at the restaurant next to A's house.

2. 那家饭店的菜怎么样？

▸ Pinyin
Nà jiā fàn diàn de cài zěn me yàng？
▸ answer
很好吃，跟上次说的那家差不多。
Hěn hǎo chī， gēn shàng cì shuō de nà jiā chà bu duō。
It's delicious, similar to the one mentioned last time.

3. B今天怎么回家？

▸ Pinyin
B jīn tiān zěn me huí jiā？
▸ answer
B也开车回家。
B yě kāi chē huí jiā。
B is also driving home.

4. A是怎么回家的？

▸ Pinyin
A shì zěn me huí jiā de？
▸ answer
A开车回家。
A kāi chē huí jiā。
A drove home.
▸ Hanzi Transcript
B: 喂，请问是李先生吗？🔊 A: 我就是，你好。🔊 B: 我们晚饭要去哪个饭店？🔊 A: 我家就在饭店旁边，我们去那儿吧。🔊 B: 好啊，那家饭店的菜好吃吗？🔊 A: 好吃，跟你上次说的那家差不多。🔊 B: 太好了。对了，你今天怎么回家？🔊 A: 我开车回家，你呢？你是怎么回家的？🔊 B: 我也开车。好，那晚点儿聊，下次见。🔊 A: 好的，再见。🔊
▸ Pinyin Transcript
B: Wèi， qǐng wèn shì Lǐ xiān sheng ma？ A: Wǒ jiù shì， nǐ hǎo。 B: Wǒ men wǎn fàn yào qù nǎ ge fàn diàn？ A: Wǒ jiā jiù zài fàn diàn páng biān， wǒ men qù nàr ba。 B: Hǎo a， nà jiā fàn diàn de cài hǎo chī ma？ A: Hǎo chī， gēn nǐ shàng cì shuō de nà jiā chà bu duō。 B: Tài hǎo le。 Duì le， nǐ jīn tiān zěn me huí jiā？ A: Wǒ kāi chē huí jiā， nǐ ne？ Nǐ shì zěn me huí jiā de？ B: Wǒ yě kāi chē。 Hǎo， nà wǎn diǎnr liáo， xià cì jiàn。 A: Hǎo de， zài jiàn。
▸ English Transcript
B: Hello, is this Mr. Li? A: That's me, hello. B: Which restaurant should we go to for dinner? A: My house is right next to a restaurant, let's go there. B: Sure, is the food at that restaurant good? A: It's good, similar to the one you mentioned last time. B: Great. By the way, how are you getting home today? A: I'm driving home. What about you? How are you getting home? B: I'm driving too. OK, talk later, see you next time. A: OK, bye.
Exercise 3: 我的健身房生活 — My Gym Life Harder

Longer narrative, single speaker. More complex grammar. Listen carefully.

🔊 Listen to full passage

1. 他一个星期去几次健身房？

▸ Pinyin
Tā yí ge xīng qī qù jǐ cì jiàn shēn fáng？
▸ answer
他一个星期去三次健身房。
Tā yí ge xīng qī qù sān cì jiàn shēn fáng。
He goes to the gym three times a week.

2. 运动完以后他觉得怎么样？

▸ Pinyin
Yùn dòng wán yǐ hòu tā jué de zěn me yàng？
▸ answer
运动完以后，他觉得非常舒服。
Yùn dòng wán yǐ hòu， tā jué de fēi cháng shū fu。
After exercising, he feels very comfortable.

3. 他的朋友觉得运动完吃肉夹馍怎么样？

▸ Pinyin
Tā de péng you jué de yùn dòng wán chī ròu jiā mó zěn me yàng？
▸ answer
他的朋友觉得很奇怪，可是他觉得很好吃。
Tā de péng you jué de hěn qí guài， kě shì tā jué de hěn hǎo chī。
His friend thinks it's strange, but he thinks it's delicious.

4. 运动完他的衣服怎么办？

▸ Pinyin
Yùn dòng wán tā de yī fu zěn me bàn？
▸ answer
他要用洗衣机洗衣服。
Tā yào yòng xǐ yī jī xǐ yī fu。
He needs to wash the clothes with the washing machine.

5. 下次他的朋友什么时候来接他？

▸ Pinyin
Xià cì tā de péng you shén me shí hou lái jiē tā？
▸ answer
半个小时就来接他。
Bàn ge xiǎo shí jiù lái jiē tā。
In half an hour, he'll come to pick him up.
▸ Hanzi Transcript
我每个星期去健身房三次，运动对身体很好。🔊 我的朋友是香港人，他常常跟我一起去。🔊 我们先跳绳十分钟，然后用划船机。🔊 划船机不容易，但是运动完以后，我觉得非常舒服。🔊 如果我不运动，身体会不舒服。🔊 朋友说，健身房里的人有时候很乱，因为人太多了。🔊 运动完以后，我们常常去吃一点儿东西，比如肉夹馍。🔊 朋友说，运动完吃肉夹馍很奇怪，可是我觉得很好吃。🔊 有时候我们也吃冰淇淋，虽然对身体不太好。🔊 朋友常常运动，我跟他差不多。🔊 运动以后，衣服要用洗衣机洗一下。🔊 我永不会不运动，因为运动让我很开心。🔊 下次朋友半个小时就来接我一起去健身房。🔊
▸ Pinyin Transcript
Wǒ měi ge xīng qī qù jiàn shēn fáng sān cì， yùn dòng duì shēn tǐ hěn hǎo。 Wǒ de péng you shì Xiāng gǎng rén， tā cháng cháng gēn wǒ yì qǐ qù。 Wǒ men xiān tiào shéng shí fēn zhōng， rán hòu yòng huá chuán jī。 Huá chuán jī bù róng yì， dàn shì yùn dòng wán yǐ hòu， wǒ jué de fēi cháng shū fu。 Rú guǒ wǒ bú yùn dòng， shēn tǐ huì bù shū fu。 Péng you shuō， jiàn shēn fáng lǐ de rén yǒu shí hou hěn luàn， yīn wèi rén tài duō le。 Yùn dòng wán yǐ hòu， wǒ men cháng cháng qù chī yì diǎnr dōng xi， bǐ rú ròu jiā mó。 Péng you shuō， yùn dòng wán chī ròu jiā mó hěn qí guài， kě shì wǒ jué de hěn hǎo chī。 Yǒu shí hou wǒ men yě chī bīng qí lín， suī rán duì shēn tǐ bú tài hǎo。 Péng you cháng cháng yùn dòng， wǒ gēn tā chà bu duō。 Yùn dòng yǐ hòu， yī fu yào yòng xǐ yī jī xǐ yí xià。 Wǒ yǒng bú huì bú yùn dòng， yīn wèi yùn dòng ràng wǒ hěn kāi xīn。 Xià cì péng you bàn ge xiǎo shí jiù lái jiē wǒ yì qǐ qù jiàn shēn fáng。
▸ English Transcript
I go to the gym three times a week; exercise is good for the body. My friend is from Hong Kong; he often goes with me. We first jump rope for ten minutes, then use the rowing machine. The rowing machine isn't easy, but after exercising I feel very comfortable. If I don't exercise, my body feels uncomfortable. My friend says the gym is sometimes chaotic because there are too many people. After exercising, we often go eat something, like a roujiamo (Chinese burger). My friend says it's strange to eat roujiamo after working out, but I think it's delicious. Sometimes we also eat ice cream, even though it's not great for the body. My friend exercises often; I'm about the same as him. After exercising, the clothes need to be washed with the washing machine. I will never stop exercising, because exercising makes me happy. Next time my friend will come in half an hour to pick me up and go to the gym together.
