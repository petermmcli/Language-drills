Exercise 1: 猫不见了 — The Cat Went Missing Easy

Short narrative, single speaker. Listen and answer the questions below.

🔊 Listen to full passage

1. 他家有几只猫？

▸ Pinyin
Tā jiā yǒu jǐ zhī māo？
▸ answer
他家有一只猫。
Tā jiā yǒu yì zhī māo。
He has one cat at home.

2. 猫什么时候不见了？

▸ Pinyin
Māo shén me shí hou bú jiàn le？
▸ answer
今天早上猫不见了。
Jīn tiān zǎo shang māo bú jiàn le。
The cat went missing this morning.

3. 猫在哪儿？

▸ Pinyin
Māo zài nǎr？
▸ answer
猫在树上。
Māo zài shù shang。
The cat was up in the tree.

4. 他为什么每天关上门？

▸ Pinyin
Tā wèi shén me měi tiān guān shàng mén？
▸ answer
因为他不想再丢猫了。
Yīn wèi tā bù xiǎng zài diū māo le。
Because he doesn't want to lose the cat again.
▸ Hanzi Transcript
我家有一只猫，猫很可爱。🔊 今天早上，我的猫不见了。🔊 我很伤心，我找猫，但是找不到。🔊 我问妈妈："你看见我的猫了吗？"🔊 妈妈说："我看见了，猫在树上。"🔊 然后我找到了猫，我很开心。🔊 我现在每天都关上门，因为我不想再丢猫了。🔊
▸ Pinyin Transcript
Wǒ jiā yǒu yì zhī māo， māo hěn kě ài。 Jīn tiān zǎo shang， wǒ de māo bú jiàn le。 Wǒ hěn shāng xīn， wǒ zhǎo māo， dàn shì zhǎo bú dào。 Wǒ wèn mā ma：“Nǐ kàn jiàn wǒ de māo le ma？” Mā ma shuō：“Wǒ kàn jiàn le， māo zài shù shang。” Rán hòu wǒ zhǎo dào le māo， wǒ hěn kāi xīn。 Wǒ xiàn zài měi tiān dōu guān shàng mén， yīn wèi wǒ bù xiǎng zài diū māo le。
▸ English Transcript
I have a cat at home; the cat is very cute. This morning, my cat went missing. I was very sad. I looked for the cat, but couldn't find it. I asked my mom, "Have you seen my cat?" Mom said, "I saw it — the cat is up in the tree." Then I found the cat, and I was very happy. Now I close the door every day, because I don't want to lose the cat again.
Exercise 2: 护照不见了 — The Missing Passport Medium

Two-speaker dialogue. Listen and answer the questions below.

🔊 Listen to full dialogue (2 voices)

1. 谁的护照不见了？

▸ Pinyin
Shéi de hù zhào bú jiàn le？
▸ answer
B 的护照不见了。
B de hù zhào bú jiàn le。
B's passport went missing.

2. 护照最后在哪儿找到的？

▸ Pinyin
Hù zhào zuì hòu zài nǎr zhǎo dào de？
▸ answer
护照在包里找到的。
Hù zhào zài bāo li zhǎo dào de。
It was found in the bag.

3. A 说这本护照怎么样？

▸ Pinyin
A shuō zhè běn hù zhào zěn me yàng？
▸ answer
A 说这本护照很重要。
A shuō zhè běn hù zhào hěn zhòng yào。
A says this passport is very important.

4. B 现在觉得怎么样？

▸ Pinyin
B xiàn zài jué de zěn me yàng？
▸ answer
B 说她挺好的。
B shuō tā tǐng hǎo de。
B says she's pretty good.
▸ Hanzi Transcript
A: 喂，你好，我在机场接人，你在哪儿？🔊 B: 我在机场，但是我的护照不见了！🔊 A: 什么？你的护照丢了吗？🔊 B: 是啊，我找了很长时间，但是找不到。🔊 A: 没关系，我们一起找。🔊 B: 哦，找到了！护照在我的包里。🔊 A: 这本护照很重要，你要小心。🔊 B: 你说得对，谢谢你。🔊 A: 不客气。你现在怎么样？🔊 B: 挺好的，谢谢你接我。🔊 A: 好，我们回家吧。🔊 B: 走吧。🔊
▸ Pinyin Transcript
A: Wéi， nǐ hǎo， wǒ zài jī chǎng jiē rén， nǐ zài nǎr？ B: Wǒ zài jī chǎng， dàn shì wǒ de hù zhào bú jiàn le！ A: Shén me？ Nǐ de hù zhào diū le ma？ B: Shì a， wǒ zhǎo le hěn cháng shí jiān， dàn shì zhǎo bú dào。 A: Méi guān xi， wǒ men yì qǐ zhǎo。 B: Ò， zhǎo dào le！ Hù zhào zài wǒ de bāo li。 A: Zhè běn hù zhào hěn zhòng yào， nǐ yào xiǎo xīn。 B: Nǐ shuō de duì， xiè xie nǐ。 A: Bú kè qi。 Nǐ xiàn zài zěn me yàng？ B: Tǐng hǎo de， xiè xie nǐ jiē wǒ。 A: Hǎo， wǒ men huí jiā ba。 B: Zǒu ba。
▸ English Transcript
A: Hello, I'm at the airport picking someone up. Where are you? B: I'm at the airport, but my passport is missing! A: What? Did you lose your passport? B: Yeah, I looked for a long time, but couldn't find it. A: It's okay, let's look together. B: Oh, found it! The passport is in my bag. A: This passport is very important — you need to be careful. B: You're right. Thank you. A: You're welcome. How are you now? B: Pretty good, thanks for picking me up. A: Good, let's go home. B: Let's go.
Exercise 3: 明年的计划 — Next Year's Plans Harder

Longer narrative, single speaker. More complex grammar. Listen carefully.

🔊 Listen to full passage

1. 冬天他想做什么运动？

▸ Pinyin
Dōng tiān tā xiǎng zuò shén me yùn dòng？
▸ answer
他想每天去滑冰。
Tā xiǎng měi tiān qù huá bīng。
He wants to go ice skating every day.

2. 他打字打得怎么样？

▸ Pinyin
Tā dǎ zì dǎ de zěn me yàng？
▸ answer
他打字打得不快。
Tā dǎ zì dǎ de bú kuài。
His typing isn't fast.

3. 他常常需要做什么？

▸ Pinyin
Tā cháng cháng xū yào zuò shén me？
▸ answer
他常常需要接单。
Tā cháng cháng xū yào jiē dān。
He often needs to accept orders.

4. 月中他要做什么？

▸ Pinyin
Yuè zhōng tā yào zuò shén me？
▸ answer
月中他要回美国去看家人。
Yuè zhōng tā yào huí měi guó qù kàn jiā rén。
Mid-month he's going back to the US to see his family.

5. 他的朋友常常问他什么？

▸ Pinyin
Tā de péng you cháng cháng wèn tā shén me？
▸ answer
朋友常常问他："你什么时候回美国来？"
Péng you cháng cháng wèn tā：“Nǐ shén me shí hou huí měi guó lái？”
His friend often asks him, "When are you coming back to the US?"
▸ Hanzi Transcript
明年就要到了，我有很多计划。🔊 冬天我想每天去滑冰，因为滑冰很有意思。🔊 我也想每天打拳，身体会更健康。🔊 我打字打得不快，所以月初我要多练习打字。🔊 我在网上兼职工作，我常常需要接单。🔊 有时候工作很多，我会很忙，但是我不怕忙。🔊 月中，我要回美国去看家人，也要去健身房运动，还要跑步。🔊 我也想每年读一本书，学习更多东西。🔊 我的朋友常常问我："你什么时候回美国来？"🔊 我告诉他："月底我就回美国来了。"🔊 明年，我希望打字更快，身体更健康，工作也更好。🔊
▸ Pinyin Transcript
Míng nián jiù yào dào le， wǒ yǒu hěn duō jì huà。 Dōng tiān wǒ xiǎng měi tiān qù huá bīng， yīn wèi huá bīng hěn yǒu yì si。 Wǒ yě xiǎng měi tiān dǎ quán， shēn tǐ huì gèng jiàn kāng。 Wǒ dǎ zì dǎ de bú kuài， suǒ yǐ yuè chū wǒ yào duō liàn xí dǎ zì。 Wǒ zài wǎng shàng jiān zhí gōng zuò， wǒ cháng cháng xū yào jiē dān。 Yǒu shí hou gōng zuò hěn duō， wǒ huì hěn máng， dàn shì wǒ bú pà máng。 Yuè zhōng， wǒ yào huí měi guó qù kàn jiā rén， yě yào qù jiàn shēn fáng yùn dòng， hái yào pǎo bù。 Wǒ yě xiǎng měi nián dú yì běn shū， xué xí gèng duō dōng xi。 Wǒ de péng you cháng cháng wèn wǒ：“Nǐ shén me shí hou huí měi guó lái？” Wǒ gào su tā：“Yuè dǐ wǒ jiù huí měi guó lái le。” Míng nián， wǒ xī wàng dǎ zì gèng kuài， shēn tǐ gèng jiàn kāng， gōng zuò yě gèng hǎo。
▸ English Transcript
Next year is coming soon, and I have a lot of plans. In winter, I want to go ice skating every day, because ice skating is very fun. I also want to practice martial arts every day, so my body will be healthier. I don't type fast, so at the start of the month I want to practice typing more. I work a part-time job online, and I often need to accept orders. Sometimes there's a lot of work and I'm very busy, but I'm not afraid of being busy. In the middle of the month, I'm going back to the US to see my family, and I'll also go to the gym to exercise and go running. I also want to read a book every year, to learn more things. My friend often asks me, "When are you coming back to the US?" I tell him, "At the end of the month I'll be back in the US." Next year, I hope my typing gets faster, my body gets healthier, and my work gets better too.
