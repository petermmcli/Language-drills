Exercise 1: 在家做饭 — Cooking at Home Easy

Short narrative, single speaker. Listen and answer the questions below.

🔊 Listen to full passage

1. 他想做什么菜？

▸ Pinyin
tā xiǎng zuò shén me cài ？
▸ answer
他想做印度菜。
tā xiǎng zuò yìn dù cài 。
He wants to make Indian food.

2. 做饭以前，他先做什么？

▸ Pinyin
zuò fàn yǐ qián ， tā xiān zuò shén me ？
▸ answer
他先把蔬菜洗干净。
tā xiān bǎ shū cài xǐ gān jìng 。
He first washes the vegetables clean.

3. 他为什么要小心拿刀？

▸ Pinyin
tā wèi shén me yào xiǎo xīn ná dāo ？
▸ answer
因为刀有一点儿危险。
yīn wèi dāo yǒu yì diǎn ér wēi xiǎn 。
Because the knife is a little dangerous.

4. 印度菜做好了以后，他把它放在哪儿？

▸ Pinyin
yìn dù cài zuò hǎo le yǐ hòu ， tā bǎ tā fàng zài nǎr ？
▸ answer
他把它放在桌子上。
tā bǎ tā fàng zài zhuō zi shàng 。
He put it on the table.
▸ Hanzi Transcript
今天我想在家做印度菜。🔊 做饭以前，我先把蔬菜洗干净。🔊 洗完以后，我把水关了。🔊 拿刀的时候，我要小心，因为刀有一点儿危险。🔊 我写下来了，因为我想记住怎么做印度菜。🔊 印度菜做好了，我把它放在桌子上。🔊 厨房很干净，我很开心。🔊
▸ Pinyin Transcript
jīn tiān wǒ xiǎng zài jiā zuò yìn dù cài 。 zuò fàn yǐ qián ， wǒ xiān bǎ shū cài xǐ gān jìng 。 xǐ wán yǐ hòu ， wǒ bǎ shuǐ guān le 。 ná dāo de shí hòu ， wǒ yào xiǎo xīn ， yīn wèi dāo yǒu yì diǎn ér wēi xiǎn 。 wǒ xiě xià lái le ， yīn wèi wǒ xiǎng jì zhù zěn me zuò yìn dù cài 。 yìn dù cài zuò hǎo le ， wǒ bǎ tā fàng zài zhuō zi shàng 。 chú fáng hěn gān jìng ， wǒ hěn kāi xīn 。
▸ English Transcript
Today I want to cook Indian food at home. Before cooking, I first wash the vegetables clean. After washing, I turned off the water. When holding the knife, I need to be careful, because the knife is a little dangerous. I wrote it down, because I want to remember how to make Indian food. The Indian food is ready; I put it on the table. The kitchen is very clean; I'm very happy.
Exercise 2: 家人一起吃晚饭 — Family Dinner Medium

Two-speaker dialogue. Listen and answer the questions below.

🔊 Listen to full dialogue (2 voices)

1. B 先做什么？

▸ Pinyin
B xiān zuò shén me ？
▸ answer
B 先把手洗干净。
B xiān bǎ shǒu xǐ gān jìng 。
B first washes his hands clean.

2. A 请 B 帮什么忙？

▸ Pinyin
A qǐng B bāng shén me máng ？
▸ answer
A 请 B 把碗和筷子放在桌子上。
A qǐng B bǎ wǎn hé kuài zi fàng zài zhuō zi shàng 。
A asks B to put the bowls and chopsticks on the table.

3. B 今天怎么送孩子去学校？

▸ Pinyin
B jīn tiān zěn me sòng hái zi qù xué xiào ？
▸ answer
B 骑自行车送孩子去学校。
B qí zì xíng chē sòng hái zi qù xué xiào 。
B rode his bike to take the kids to school.

4. 外面的天气怎么样？

▸ Pinyin
wài miàn de tiān qì zěn me yàng ？
▸ answer
外面有点儿潮湿。
wài miàn yǒu diǎn ér cháo shī 。
It's a bit damp outside.
▸ Hanzi Transcript
A: 你回来了！晚饭快做好了。🔊 B: 好的，我先把手洗干净。🔊 A: 你能帮我一下吗？桌子还没准备好。🔊 B: 没问题，我把碗和筷子放在桌子上。🔊 A: 谢谢你。对了，你今天骑自行车送孩子去学校了吗？🔊 B: 骑了，但是外面有点儿潮湿。🔊 A: 骑车安全吗？大路上车多不多？🔊 B: 安全的，我很小心。这个季节天气不错。🔊 A: 好，那我们吃饭吧。我还给你发短信了，你看了吗？🔊 B: 看了！谢谢你告诉我。我们开始吃饭吧。🔊
▸ Pinyin Transcript
A: nǐ huí lái le ！ wǎn fàn kuài zuò hǎo le 。 B: hǎo de ， wǒ xiān bǎ shǒu xǐ gān jìng 。 A: nǐ néng bāng wǒ yí xià ma ？ zhuō zi hái méi zhǔn bèi hǎo 。 B: méi wèn tí ， wǒ bǎ wǎn hé kuài zi fàng zài zhuō zi shàng 。 A: xiè xiè nǐ 。 duì le ， nǐ jīn tiān qí zì xíng chē sòng hái zi qù xué xiào le ma ？ B: qí le ， dàn shì wài miàn yǒu diǎn ér cháo shī 。 A: qí chē ān quán ma ？ dà lù shàng chē duō bù duō ？ B: ān quán de ， wǒ hěn xiǎo xīn 。 zhè ge jì jié tiān qì bú cuò 。 A: hǎo ， nà wǒ men chī fàn ba 。 wǒ hái gěi nǐ fā duǎn xìn le ， nǐ kàn le ma ？ B: kàn le ！ xiè xiè nǐ gào su wǒ 。 wǒ men kāi shǐ chī fàn ba 。
▸ English Transcript
A: You're back! Dinner is almost ready. B: Okay, I'll wash my hands first. A: Can you help me for a moment? The table isn't ready yet. B: No problem, I'll put the bowls and chopsticks on the table. A: Thanks. By the way, did you bike the kids to school today? B: I did; but it was a bit damp outside. A: Is biking safe? Is there a lot of traffic on the main road? B: It's safe, I was very careful. The weather is nice this season. A: Okay, let's eat then. I also texted you — did you see it? B: I saw it! Thanks for telling me. Let's start eating.
Exercise 3: 送礼物 — Sending a Gift Harder

Longer narrative, single speaker. More complex grammar. Listen carefully.

🔊 Listen to full passage

1. 他为什么给朋友发短信？

▸ Pinyin
tā wèi shén me gěi péng you fā duǎn xìn ？
▸ answer
因为他不知道送什么礼物，所以问朋友喜欢什么。
yīn wèi tā bù zhī dào sòng shén me lǐ wù ， suǒ yǐ wèn péng you xǐ huan shén me 。
Because he didn't know what gift to give, so he asked his friend what he likes.

2. 他想送朋友一辆自行车，为什么没有送？

▸ Pinyin
tā xiǎng sòng péng you yí liàng zì xíng chē ， wèi shén me méi yǒu sòng ？
▸ answer
因为自行车很贵，他钱不够。
yīn wèi zì xíng chē hěn guì ， tā qián bú gòu 。
Because the bicycle was expensive, and he didn't have enough money.

3. 谁帮他找便宜一点儿的礼物？

▸ Pinyin
shuí bāng tā zhǎo pián yi yì dian3r de lǐ wù ？
▸ answer
他妈妈帮他找。
tā mā ma bāng tā zhǎo 。
His mom helped him find one.

4. 他最后送了朋友什么礼物？

▸ Pinyin
tā zuì hòu sòng le péng you shén me lǐ wù ？
▸ answer
他送了朋友一个手表。
tā sòng le péng you yí ge shǒu biǎo 。
He gave his friend a watch.

5. 他怎么去朋友家？外面天气怎么样？

▸ Pinyin
tā zěn me qù péng you jiā ？ wài miàn tiān qì zěn me yàng ？
▸ answer
他骑自行车去，外面很冷，零下四十度。
tā qí zì xíng chē qù ， wài miàn hěn lěng ， líng xià sì shí dù 。
He rode his bike there; it was very cold outside, minus forty degrees.
▸ Hanzi Transcript
圣诞节快到了，我想送给朋友一个礼物。🔊 但是我不知道送什么，所以我给他发了短信，问他喜欢什么。🔊 他还没回短信，我又给他发了电子邮件。🔊 我想送他一件衣服，因为这个季节很冷。🔊 我记得他喜欢印度的衣服，因为他去过印度。🔊 春夏秋冬，他都喜欢在外面运动。🔊 所以我想送他一辆自行车。🔊 但是这辆自行车很贵，我钱不够。🔊 我给妈妈打电话，请妈妈帮我找一个便宜一点儿的礼物。🔊 妈妈说，让我看看网上的礼物。🔊 我在网上找到了一个漂亮的手表，不太贵。🔊 我把手表放在一个盒子里，准备送给他。🔊 我把礼物放在包里，骑自行车去他家。🔊 外面很冷，零下四十度，但是我不怕。🔊 到了他家，我把礼物给他，他很高兴。🔊
▸ Pinyin Transcript
shèng dàn jié kuài dào le ， wǒ xiǎng sòng gěi péng you yí ge lǐ wù 。 dàn shì wǒ bù zhī dào sòng shén me ， suǒ yǐ wǒ gěi tā fā le duǎn xìn ， wèn tā xǐ huan shén me 。 tā hái méi huí duǎn xìn ， wǒ yòu gěi tā fā le diàn zǐ yóu jiàn 。 wǒ xiǎng sòng tā yí jiàn yī fu ， yīn wèi zhè ge jì jié hěn lěng 。 wǒ jì de tā xǐ huan yìn dù de yī fu ， yīn wèi tā qù guo yìn dù 。 chūn xià qiū dōng ， tā dōu xǐ huan zài wài miàn yùn dòng 。 suǒ yǐ wǒ xiǎng sòng tā yí liàng zì xíng chē 。 dàn shì zhè liàng zì xíng chē hěn guì ， wǒ qián bú gòu 。 wǒ gěi mā ma dǎ diàn huà ， qǐng mā ma bāng wǒ zhǎo yí ge pián yi yì dian3r de lǐ wù 。 mā ma shuō ， ràng wǒ kàn kan wǎng shàng de lǐ wù 。 wǒ zài wǎng shàng zhǎo dào le yí ge piào liang de shǒu biǎo ， bú tài guì 。 wǒ bǎ shǒu biǎo fàng zài yí ge hé zi li ， zhǔn bèi sòng gěi tā 。 wǒ bǎ lǐ wù fàng zài bāo li ， qí zì xíng chē qù tā jiā 。 wài miàn hěn lěng ， líng xià sì shí dù ， dàn shì wǒ bú pà 。 dào le tā jiā ， wǒ bǎ lǐ wù gěi tā ， tā hěn gāo xìng 。
▸ English Transcript
Christmas is almost here, and I want to give my friend a gift. But I didn't know what to give, so I texted him, asking what he likes. He still hadn't replied to the text, so I emailed him too. I want to give him a piece of clothing, because it's cold this season. I remember he likes Indian clothes, because he has been to India. In every season, he likes to exercise outside. So I want to give him a bicycle. But this bicycle is expensive, and I don't have enough money. I called my mom, and asked her to help me find a cheaper gift. Mom said, let me look at gifts online. I found a pretty watch online — not too expensive. I put the watch in a box, getting ready to give it to him. I put the gift in my bag and rode my bike to his house. It was cold outside, minus forty degrees, but I wasn't afraid. When I got to his house, I gave him the gift, and he was very happy.
