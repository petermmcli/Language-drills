Exercise 1: 今天天气怎么样？ — How's the Weather Today? Easy

Short narrative, single speaker. Listen and answer the questions below.

🔊 Listen to full passage

1. 今天天气怎么样？

▸ Pinyin
Jīn tiān tiān qì zěn me yàng？
▸ answer
今天天气不好，天阴了，还有一点儿冷。
Jīn tiān tiān qì bù hǎo， tiān yīn le， hái yǒu yì diǎn er lěng。
The weather isn't good today, it's cloudy, and a bit cold too.

2. 昨天天气怎么样？

▸ Pinyin
Zuó tiān tiān qì zěn me yàng？
▸ answer
昨天是晴天。
Zuó tiān shì qíng tiān。
Yesterday was sunny.

3. 明天会下大雨吗？

▸ Pinyin
Míng tiān huì xià dà yǔ ma？
▸ answer
不会，明天会下小雨，不会下大雨。
Bú huì， míng tiān huì xià xiǎo yǔ， bú huì xià dà yǔ。
No, tomorrow it will rain a little, not rain heavily.

4. 他想出去吗？

▸ Pinyin
Tā xiǎng chū qù ma？
▸ answer
不想，他想在家休息。
Bù xiǎng， tā xiǎng zài jiā xiū xi。
No, he wants to stay home and rest.

5. 如果明天还是阴天，他会怎么样？

▸ Pinyin
Rú guǒ míng tiān hái shi yīn tiān， tā huì zěn me yàng？
▸ answer
他会穿外套。
Tā huì chuān wài tào。
He'll wear a coat.
▸ Hanzi Transcript
今天天气不好，天阴了，还有一点儿冷。🔊 昨天是晴天，但是今天是阴天。🔊 听说明天会下小雨，不会下大雨。🔊 我不太想出去，我想在家休息。🔊 如果明天还是阴天，我会穿外套。🔊 希望这个星期天气越来越好，不冷也不热，很舒服。🔊
▸ Pinyin Transcript
Jīn tiān tiān qì bù hǎo， tiān yīn le， hái yǒu yì diǎn er lěng。 Zuó tiān shì qíng tiān， dàn shì jīn tiān shì yīn tiān。 Tīng shuō míng tiān huì xià xiǎo yǔ， bú huì xià dà yǔ。 Wǒ bú tài xiǎng chū qù， wǒ xiǎng zài jiā xiū xi。 Rú guǒ míng tiān hái shi yīn tiān， wǒ huì chuān wài tào。 Xī wàng zhè ge xīng qī tiān qì yuè lái yuè hǎo， bù lěng yě bú rè， hěn shū fu。
▸ English Transcript
The weather isn't good today, it's cloudy, and a bit cold too. Yesterday was sunny, but today is cloudy. I heard it will rain a little tomorrow, not heavily. I don't really want to go out — I want to stay home and rest. If tomorrow is still cloudy, I'll wear a coat. I hope this week's weather gets better and better — not cold, not hot, very comfortable.
Exercise 2: 快递到了 — The Parcel Has Arrived Medium

Two-speaker dialogue: a delivery courier calls a customer about a Double Eleven order. Listen and answer the questions below.

🔊 Listen to full dialogue (2 voices)

1. B 在哪儿？

▸ Pinyin
B zài nǎ r？
▸ answer
B 到你家楼下了。
B dào jiā lóu xià le。
B has arrived downstairs at A's place.

2. A 要坐什么上楼？

▸ Pinyin
A yào zuò shén me shàng lóu？
▸ answer
要坐电梯上18楼。
Yào zuò diàn tī shàng shí bā lóu。
She needs to take the elevator up to the 18th floor.

3. 出电梯以后怎么走？

▸ Pinyin
Chū diàn tī yǐ hòu zěn me zǒu？
▸ answer
出电梯左转。
Chū diàn tī zuǒ zhuǎn。
Turn left out of the elevator.

4. 这个快递大不大？

▸ Pinyin
Zhè ge kuài dì dà bu dà？
▸ answer
有点儿大，所以有点花时间。
Yǒu diǎn er dà， suǒ yǐ yǒu diǎn huā shí jiān。
It's a bit big, so it'll take a little time.

5. 双十一送货上门要不要钱？

▸ Pinyin
Shuāng shí yī sòng huò shàng mén yào bu yào qián？
▸ answer
不要钱，送货上门是免费的。
Bú yào qián， shì miǎn fèi de。
No charge — it's free.
▸ Hanzi Transcript
B: 喂，你的快递到了。🔊 A: 太好了！谢谢你。你现在在哪儿？🔊 B: 我到你家楼下了。🔊 A: 好，进来以后坐电梯上18楼。🔊 B: 好的，出电梯左转，对吗？🔊 A: 对。你知道房间号是多少吗？🔊 B: 知道，1808。这个快递有点儿大，有点花时间。🔊 A: 没问题，谢谢你。双十一的东西真便宜！🔊 B: 是啊，送货上门是免费的。🔊 A: 太好了。等我收到了，我给你写好评！🔊 B: 谢谢！还有什么可以帮您的吗？🔊 A: 没有了，谢谢你。晚上好！🔊
▸ Pinyin Transcript
B: Wéi， nǐ de kuài dì dào le。 A: Tài hǎo le！ Xiè xie nǐ。 Nǐ xiàn zài zài nǎ r？ B: Wǒ dào nǐ jiā lóu xià le。 A: Hǎo， jìn lái yǐ hòu zuò diàn tī shàng shí bā lóu。 B: Hǎo de， chū diàn tī zuǒ zhuǎn， duì ma？ A: Duì。 Nǐ zhī dào fáng jiān hào shì duō shao ma？ B: Zhī dào， yī bā líng bā。 Zhè ge kuài dì yǒu diǎn er dà， yǒu diǎn huā shí jiān。 A: Méi wèn tí， xiè xie nǐ。 Shuāng shí yī de dōng xi zhēn pián yi！ B: Shì a， sòng huò shàng mén shì miǎn fèi de。 A: Tài hǎo le。 Děng wǒ shōu dào le， wǒ gěi nǐ xiě hǎo píng！ B: Xiè xie！ Hái yǒu shén me kě yǐ bāng nín de ma？ A: Méi yǒu le， xiè xie nǐ。 Wǎn shang hǎo！
▸ English Transcript
B: Hello, your parcel has arrived. A: Great! Thanks. Where are you now? B: I've arrived downstairs at your place. A: OK, after you come in, take the elevator up to the 18th floor. B: OK, turn left out of the elevator, right? A: Right. Do you know what the room number is? B: I know, 1808. This parcel is a bit big, it'll take a little time. A: No problem, thanks. Double Eleven stuff is really cheap! B: Yeah, home delivery is free. A: Great. Once I receive it, I'll write you a good review! B: Thanks! Is there anything else I can help you with? A: Not anymore, thanks. Good evening!
Exercise 3: 地板漏水了 — The Floor Is Leaking Harder

Longer narrative, single speaker. More complex grammar. Listen carefully.

🔊 Listen to full passage

1. 客厅的地板怎么了？

▸ Pinyin
Kè tīng de dì bǎn zěn me le？
▸ answer
客厅的地板在漏水。
Kè tīng de dì bǎn zài lòu shuǐ。
The living room floor is leaking.

2. 他为什么给房东打电话？

▸ Pinyin
Tā wèi shén me gěi fáng dōng dǎ diàn huà？
▸ answer
因为客厅的地板在漏水，他给房东打电话，告诉他这个问题。
Yīn wèi kè tīng de dì bǎn zài lòu shuǐ， tā gěi fáng dōng dǎ diàn huà， gào su tā zhè ge wèn tí。
Because the living room floor was leaking, he called the landlord and told him about the problem.

3. 师傅说地板怎么样？

▸ Pinyin
Shī fu shuō dì bǎn zěn me yàng？
▸ answer
师傅说地板很潮湿，不安全。
Shī fu shuō dì bǎn hěn cháo shī， bù ān quán。
The repairman said the floor was very damp, not safe.

4. 修地板要多长时间？

▸ Pinyin
Xiū dì bǎn yào duō cháng shí jiān？
▸ answer
可能要两个小时。
Kě néng yào liǎng ge xiǎo shí。
Maybe two hours.

5. 最后，他怎么样？

▸ Pinyin
Zuì hòu， tā zěn me yàng？
▸ answer
他很开心，因为没有问题了。
Tā hěn kāi xīn， yīn wèi méi yǒu wèn tí le。
He's very happy, because there's no more problem.
▸ Hanzi Transcript
昨天晚上，我看到客厅的地板在漏水。🔊 我有点儿怕，因为我不知道为什么会漏水。🔊 我给房东打电话，告诉他这个问题。🔊 房东说会找一个师傅检查一下。🔊 今天早上，师傅到了，他说地板很潮湿。🔊 他检查了一下，说这个不安全。🔊 他说要修，但是需要一点儿时间。🔊 我问他要多长时间，他说可能要两个小时。🔊 我觉得有点儿麻烦，因为我中午要上班。🔊 但是师傅说没问题，他会快一点儿。🔊 两个小时以后，他说都修好了，地板已经干净了。🔊 我谢谢他，给了他一些钱。🔊 现在我很开心，因为没有问题了。🔊
▸ Pinyin Transcript
Zuó tiān wǎn shang， wǒ kàn dào kè tīng de dì bǎn zài lòu shuǐ。 Wǒ yǒu diǎn er pà， yīn wèi wǒ bù zhī dào wèi shén me huì lòu shuǐ。 Wǒ gěi fáng dōng dǎ diàn huà， gào su tā zhè ge wèn tí。 Fáng dōng shuō huì zhǎo yí ge shī fu jiǎn chá yí xià。 Jīn tiān zǎo shang， shī fu dào le， tā shuō dì bǎn hěn cháo shī。 Tā jiǎn chá le yí xià， shuō zhè ge bù ān quán。 Tā shuō yào xiū， dàn shì xū yào yì diǎn er shí jiān。 Wǒ wèn tā yào duō cháng shí jiān， tā shuō kě néng yào liǎng ge xiǎo shí。 Wǒ jué de yǒu diǎn er má fan， yīn wèi wǒ zhōng wǔ yào shàng bān。 Dàn shì shī fu shuō méi wèn tí， tā huì kuài yì diǎn er。 Liǎng ge xiǎo shí yǐ hòu， tā shuō dōu xiū hǎo le， dì bǎn yǐ jīng gān jìng le。 Wǒ xiè xie tā， gěi le tā yì xiē qián。 Xiàn zài wǒ hěn kāi xīn， yīn wèi méi yǒu wèn tí le。
▸ English Transcript
Last night, I noticed the living room floor was leaking. I was a little scared, because I didn't know why it was leaking. I called the landlord and told him about the problem. The landlord said he'd find a repairman to check it. This morning, the repairman arrived — he said the floor was very damp. He checked it and said this wasn't safe. He said it needs fixing, but it would take a little time. I asked him how long it would take — he said maybe two hours. I felt it was a bit of a hassle, because I had to go to work at noon. But the repairman said no problem, he'd be quick. Two hours later, he said it was all fixed — the floor was already clean. I thanked him and gave him some money. Now I'm happy, because there's no more problem.
