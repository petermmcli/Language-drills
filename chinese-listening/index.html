Exercise 1: 生病以后 — After Getting Sick Easy

Short narrative, single speaker. Listen and answer the questions below.

🔊 Listen to full passage

1. 他为什么去医院？

▸ Pinyin
Tā wèi shén me qù yī yuàn？
▸ answer
因为他生病了。
Yīn wèi tā shēng bìng le。
Because he got sick.

2. 他要吃几次药？

▸ Pinyin
Tā yào chī jǐ cì yào？
▸ answer
每天三次。
Měi tiān sān cì。
Three times a day.

3. 谁每天照顾他？

▸ Pinyin
Shéi měi tiān zhào gù tā？
▸ answer
姐姐每天照顾他。
Jiě jie měi tiān zhào gù tā。
His older sister takes care of him every day.

4. 他现在觉得怎么样？

▸ Pinyin
Tā xiàn zài jué de zěn me yàng？
▸ answer
他身体更好了，很开心。
Tā shēn tǐ gèng hǎo le， hěn kāi xīn。
He feels better now and is very happy.
▸ Hanzi Transcript
我上个星期生病了，去了医院。🔊 医生说我要吃药，每天三次。🔊 前天我出院了，现在在家休息。🔊 姐姐每天照顾我，她对我很好。🔊 医生说我身体更好了，我很开心。🔊 以后我要回去工作。🔊
▸ Pinyin Transcript
Wǒ shàng ge xīng qī shēng bìng le， qù le yī yuàn。 Yī shēng shuō wǒ yào chī yào， měi tiān sān cì。 Qián tiān wǒ chū yuàn le， xiàn zài zài jiā xiū xi。 Jiě jie měi tiān zhào gù wǒ， tā duì wǒ hěn hǎo。 Yī shēng shuō wǒ shēn tǐ gèng hǎo le， wǒ hěn kāi xīn。 Yǐ hòu wǒ yào huí qù gōng zuò。
▸ English Transcript
I got sick last week and went to the hospital. The doctor said I need to take medicine, three times a day. The day before yesterday I was discharged, now I'm resting at home. My older sister takes care of me every day, she's very good to me. The doctor said my health is better now, I'm very happy. Later I need to go back to work.
Exercise 2: 去车站 — Going to the Station Medium

Two-speaker dialogue. Listen and answer the questions below.

🔊 Listen to full dialogue (2 voices)

1. A 几点要到车站？

▸ Pinyin
A jǐ diǎn yào dào chē zhàn？
▸ answer
三点要到车站。
Sān diǎn yào dào chē zhàn。
She needs to be at the station by 3 o'clock.

2. 去车站要怎么走？

▸ Pinyin
Qù chē zhàn yào zěn me zǒu？
▸ answer
先一直走，到红绿灯那儿左拐，就到了车站。
Xiān yì zhí zǒu， dào hóng lǜ dēng nàr zuǒ guǎi， jiù dào le chē zhàn。
Go straight first, turn left at the traffic light, and you'll arrive at the station.

3. B 要回去做什么？

▸ Pinyin
B yào huí qù zuò shén me？
▸ answer
B 要回去工作。
B yào huí qù gōng zuò。
B needs to go back to work.

4. 他们去车站以前先去哪儿？

▸ Pinyin
Tā men qù chē zhàn yǐ qián xiān qù nǎr？
▸ answer
他们先去外滩看一下。
Tā men xiān qù Wài tān kàn yí xià。
They go to look at the Bund first.
▸ Hanzi Transcript
B: 你几点要到车站？🔊 A: 我三点要到车站，可是我不知道怎么去。🔊 B: 没问题，先一直走，到红绿灯那儿左拐，然后就到了车站。🔊 A: 谢谢你！你可以送我去车站吗？🔊 B: 当然可以，我们一起走吧。在路上我们可以说话。🔊 A: 好，走吧！到了车站以后，你要回去吗？🔊 B: 是啊，我要回去工作，可是我们先去外滩看一下，好吗？🔊 A: 好主意！从车站到外滩不远，行吗？🔊 B: 行，我们走吧！🔊
▸ Pinyin Transcript
B: Nǐ jǐ diǎn yào dào chē zhàn？ A: Wǒ sān diǎn yào dào chē zhàn， kě shì wǒ bù zhī dào zěn me qù。 B: Méi wèn tí， xiān yì zhí zǒu， dào hóng lǜ dēng nàr zuǒ guǎi， rán hòu jiù dào le chē zhàn。 A: Xiè xie nǐ！ Nǐ kě yǐ sòng wǒ qù chē zhàn ma？ B: Dāng rán kě yǐ， wǒ men yì qǐ zǒu ba。 Zài lù shang wǒ men kě yǐ shuō huà。 A: Hǎo， zǒu ba！ Dào le chē zhàn yǐ hòu， nǐ yào huí qù ma？ B: Shì a， wǒ yào huí qù gōng zuò， kě shì wǒ men xiān qù Wài tān kàn yí xià， hǎo ma？ A: Hǎo zhǔ yi！ Cóng chē zhàn dào Wài tān bù yuǎn， xíng ma？ B: Xíng， wǒ men zǒu ba！
▸ English Transcript
B: What time do you need to get to the station? A: I need to be there by 3, but I don't know how to get there. B: No problem, go straight first, turn left at the traffic light, and then you'll arrive at the station. A: Thank you! Can you walk me to the station? B: Of course, let's go together. We can talk on the way. A: OK, let's go! After we get to the station, do you need to go back? B: Yeah, I need to go back to work, but let's go look at the Bund first, OK? A: Good idea! It's not far from the station to the Bund, is that OK? B: Sure, let's go!
Exercise 3: 房子漏水了 — The House Is Leaking Harder

Longer narrative, single speaker. More complex grammar. Listen carefully.

🔊 Listen to full passage

1. 他的房子是什么时候找到的？

▸ Pinyin
Tā de fáng zi shì shén me shí hou zhǎo dào de？
▸ answer
是九月的时候找到的。
Shì jiǔ yuè de shí hou zhǎo dào de。
He found it in September.

2. 昨天晚上他看见了什么？

▸ Pinyin
Zuó tiān wǎn shang tā kàn jiàn le shén me？
▸ answer
他看见地板上有水，是漏水了。
Tā kàn jiàn dì bǎn shang yǒu shuǐ， shì lòu shuǐ le。
He saw water on the floor — it was leaking.

3. 房东听了以后觉得怎么样？

▸ Pinyin
Fáng dōng tīng le yǐ hòu jué de zěn me yàng？
▸ answer
房东听了很生气。
Fáng dōng tīng le hěn shēng qì。
The landlord was very upset when he heard.

4. 谁修好了地板？

▸ Pinyin
Shéi xiū hǎo le dì bǎn？
▸ answer
房东找到的那个人修好了地板，修了两个小时。
Fáng dōng zhǎo dào de nà ge rén xiū hǎo le dì bǎn， xiū le liǎng ge xiǎo shí。
The person the landlord found fixed the floor — it took two hours.

5. 这次的问题让他知道了什么？

▸ Pinyin
Zhè cì de wèn tí ràng tā zhī dào le shén me？
▸ answer
让他知道房东和他们的关系很重要。
Ràng tā zhī dào fáng dōng hé tā men de guān xi hěn zhòng yào。
It made him realize the relationship with the landlord is very important.
▸ Hanzi Transcript
我住的房子是九月的时候找到的。🔊 这个房子很好，但是最近有点儿问题。🔊 昨天晚上，我看见地板上有水，是漏水了。🔊 我打电话给房东，告诉他这个问题。🔊 房东听了很生气，他说他会找人修地板。🔊 房东说这个问题和房子的年龄有关系。🔊 结果，房东找到了一个人修地板。🔊 下午，那个人开始修地板。🔊 他修了两个小时，地板终于好了。🔊 房东很有礼貌，他对我说对不起。🔊 我说没关系，谢谢你的帮助。🔊 这次的问题让我知道，房东和我们的关系很重要。🔊
▸ Pinyin Transcript
Wǒ zhù de fáng zi shì jiǔ yuè de shí hou zhǎo dào de。 Zhè ge fáng zi hěn hǎo， dàn shì zuì jìn yǒu diǎn r wèn tí。 Zuó tiān wǎn shang， wǒ kàn jiàn dì bǎn shang yǒu shuǐ， shì lòu shuǐ le。 Wǒ dǎ diàn huà gěi fáng dōng， gào su tā zhè ge wèn tí。 Fáng dōng tīng le hěn shēng qì， tā shuō tā huì zhǎo rén xiū dì bǎn。 Fáng dōng shuō zhè ge wèn tí hé fáng zi de nián líng yǒu guān xi。 Jié guǒ， fáng dōng zhǎo dào le yí ge rén xiū dì bǎn。 Xià wǔ， nà ge rén kāi shǐ xiū dì bǎn。 Tā xiū le liǎng ge xiǎo shí， dì bǎn zhōng yú hǎo le。 Fáng dōng hěn yǒu lǐ mào， tā duì wǒ shuō duì bu qǐ。 Wǒ shuō méi guān xi， xiè xie nǐ de bāng zhù。 Zhè cì de wèn tí ràng wǒ zhī dào， fáng dōng hé wǒ men de guān xi hěn zhòng yào。
▸ English Transcript
The house I live in, I found in September. This house is good, but recently there's been a bit of a problem. Last night, I saw water on the floor — it was leaking. I called the landlord and told him about this problem. The landlord was very upset hearing this, he said he'd find someone to fix the floor. The landlord said the problem is related to the age of the house. As a result, the landlord found someone to fix the floor. In the afternoon, that person started fixing the floor. He worked on it for two hours, and the floor was finally fixed. The landlord was very polite, he said sorry to me. I said it's OK, thank you for your help. This problem made me realize the relationship with the landlord is very important.
