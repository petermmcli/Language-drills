Exercise 1: 我不太舒服 — Not Feeling Well Easy

Short narrative, single speaker. Listen and answer the questions below.

🔊 Listen to full passage

1. 他今天怎么样？

▸ Pinyin
Tā jīn tiān zěn me yàng？
▸ answer
他不太舒服，咳嗽，还发烧了。
Tā bú tài shū fu， ké sou， hái fā shāo le。
He doesn't feel well; he's coughing and has a fever.

2. 他去商店买什么？

▸ Pinyin
Tā qù shāng diàn mǎi shén me？
▸ answer
他买感冒药和口罩。
Tā mǎi gǎn mào yào hé kǒu zhào。
He buys cold medicine and a face mask.

3. 店员说他应该做什么？

▸ Pinyin
Diàn yuán shuō tā yīng gāi zuò shén me？
▸ answer
多休息，多喝水。
Duō xiū xi， duō hē shuǐ。
Rest more and drink more water.

4. 他明天要做什么？

▸ Pinyin
Tā míng tiān yào zuò shén me？
▸ answer
他要好好休息。
Tā yào hǎo hāo xiū xi。
He's going to rest properly.
▸ Hanzi Transcript
今天我不太舒服。🔊 我咳嗽，嗓子也不好，还发烧了。🔊 我去商店买感冒药。🔊 我还买了口罩。🔊 店员说，我应该多休息，多喝水。🔊 我觉得有点儿困，想回家睡觉。🔊 明天我要好好休息。🔊
▸ Pinyin Transcript
Jīn tiān wǒ bú tài shū fu。 Wǒ ké sou， sǎng zi yě bù hǎo， hái fā shāo le。 Wǒ qù shāng diàn mǎi gǎn mào yào。 Wǒ hái mǎi le kǒu zhào。 Diàn yuán shuō， wǒ yīng gāi duō xiū xi， duō hē shuǐ。 Wǒ jué de yǒu diǎn er kùn， xiǎng huí jiā shuì jiào。 Míng tiān wǒ yào hǎo hāo xiū xi。
▸ English Transcript
I don't feel very well today. I'm coughing, my throat is bad too, and I even have a fever. I go to the store to buy cold medicine. I also bought a face mask. The shop assistant says I should rest more and drink more water. I feel a bit sleepy and want to go home to sleep. Tomorrow I'm going to rest properly.
Exercise 2: 老板的生日 — The Boss's Birthday Medium

Two-speaker dialogue. Listen and answer the questions below.

🔊 Listen to full dialogue (2 voices)

1. 他们很久没见了吗？

▸ Pinyin
Tā men hěn jiǔ méi jiàn le ma？
▸ answer
是啊，他们很久没见了。
Shì a， tā men hěn jiǔ méi jiàn le。
Yes, they haven't seen each other in a long time.

2. 为什么 B 听不清？

▸ Pinyin
Wèi shén me B tīng bù qīng？
▸ answer
因为这里的声音太大了。
Yīn wèi zhè lǐ de shēng yīn tài dà le。
Because the sound here is too loud.

3. A 觉得怎么样？

▸ Pinyin
A jué de zěn me yàng？
▸ answer
A 觉得有点没意思。
A jué de yǒu diǎn méi yì si。
A finds it a bit boring.

4. 他们最后吃什么？

▸ Pinyin
Tā men zuì hòu chī shén me？
▸ answer
他们吃蛋糕。
Tā men chī dàn gāo。
They eat cake.
▸ Hanzi Transcript
B: 好久不见！我们很久没见了。🔊 A: 是啊，最近你怎么样？🔊 B: 还行，工作很忙，经常出差。🔊 A: 今天是大老板的生日，他想让大家都来。🔊 B: 我听不清，这里的声音太大了。🔊 A: 我们去外面聊吧。🔊 B: 好。老板的朋友很多，大家都在寒暄。🔊 A: 我觉得有点没意思，我也许先回家。🔊 B: 别走，我们吃蛋糕吧。🔊 A: 好啊。你看，老板的蛋糕很大，也很甜。🔊
▸ Pinyin Transcript
B: Hǎo jiǔ bú jiàn！ wǒ men hěn jiǔ méi jiàn le。 A: Shì a， zuì jìn nǐ zěn me yàng？ B: Hái xíng， gōng zuò hěn máng， jīng cháng chū chāi。 A: Jīn tiān shì dà lǎo bǎn de shēng rì， tā xiǎng ràng dà jiā dōu lái。 B: Wǒ tīng bù qīng， zhè lǐ de shēng yīn tài dà le。 A: Wǒ men qù wài mian liáo ba。 B: Hǎo。 lǎo bǎn de péng you hěn duō， dà jiā dōu zài hán xuān。 A: Wǒ jué de yǒu diǎn méi yì si， wǒ yě xǔ xiān huí jiā。 B: Bié zǒu， wǒ men chī dàn gāo ba。 A: Hǎo a。 nǐ kàn， lǎo bǎn de dàn gāo hěn dà， yě hěn tián。
▸ English Transcript
B: Long time no see! We haven't seen each other in a very long time. A: Yes! How have you been lately? B: Not bad. Work is busy and I often travel for work. A: Today is the big boss's birthday, and he wants everyone to come. B: I can't hear clearly — the sound here is too loud. A: Let's go outside and chat. B: Okay. The boss has lots of friends, and everyone is exchanging pleasantries. A: I find it a bit boring. Maybe I'll go home first. B: Don't go — let's eat cake. A: Sure! Look, the boss's cake is huge, and sweet too.
Exercise 3: 去苏州出差 — A Business Trip to Suzhou Harder

Longer narrative, single speaker. More complex grammar. Listen carefully.

🔊 Listen to full passage

1. 他去哪儿出差？

▸ Pinyin
Tā qù nǎ er chū chāi？
▸ answer
他去苏州出差。
Tā qù Sū zhōu chū chāi。
He went to Suzhou on a business trip.

2. 他怎么去苏州？

▸ Pinyin
Tā zěn me qù Sū zhōu？
▸ answer
他坐地铁到火车站，然后上高铁。
Tā zuò dì tiě dào huǒ chē zhàn， rán hòu shàng gāo tiě。
He took the subway to the train station, then boarded the high-speed train.

3. 高铁要多长时间？

▸ Pinyin
Gāo tiě yào duō cháng shí jiān？
▸ answer
一个多小时。
Yí ge duō xiǎo shí。
A little over an hour.

4. 他为什么坐出租车去酒店？

▸ Pinyin
Tā wèi shén me zuò chū zū chē qù jiǔ diàn？
▸ answer
因为他的行李很大。
Yīn wèi tā de xíng li hěn dà。
Because his luggage was big.

5. 北京和苏州有时区问题吗？

▸ Pinyin
Běi jīng hé Sū zhōu yǒu shí qū wèn tí ma？
▸ answer
没有，北京时间和苏州时间一样。
Méi yǒu， Běi jīng shí jiān hé Sū zhōu shí jiān yí yàng。
No — Beijing time and Suzhou time are the same.
▸ Hanzi Transcript
上个星期我去苏州出差。🔊 我早上六点起床，七点一刻出门。🔊 我坐地铁到火车站，然后上高铁。🔊 高铁很快，一个多小时就到了。🔊 我出火车站，车很多，人也很多。🔊 我的行李很大，所以我坐出租车去酒店。🔊 酒店在市中心，很方便，离公司只有一刻钟。🔊 工作时间很长，我有点累，但是我不担心。🔊 晚上我刷牙，洗脸，很早睡觉。🔊 北京时间和苏州时间一样，没有时区问题。🔊 我觉得这次出差很不错，也许下个星期还去。🔊
▸ Pinyin Transcript
Shàng ge xīng qī wǒ qù Sū zhōu chū chāi。 Wǒ zǎo shang liù diǎn qǐ chuáng， qī diǎn yí kè chū mén。 Wǒ zuò dì tiě dào huǒ chē zhàn， rán hòu shàng gāo tiě。 Gāo tiě hěn kuài， yí ge duō xiǎo shí jiù dào le。 Wǒ chū huǒ chē zhàn， chē hěn duō， rén yě hěn duō。 Wǒ de xíng li hěn dà， suǒ yǐ wǒ zuò chū zū chē qù jiǔ diàn。 Jiǔ diàn zài shì zhōng xīn， hěn fāng biàn， lí gōng sī zhǐ yǒu yí kè zhōng。 Gōng zuò shí jiān hěn cháng， wǒ yǒu diǎn lèi， dàn shì wǒ bù dān xīn。 Wǎn shang wǒ shuā yá， xǐ liǎn， hěn zǎo shuì jiào。 Běi jīng shí jiān hé Sū zhōu shí jiān yí yàng， méi yǒu shí qū wèn tí。 Wǒ jué de zhè cì chū chāi hěn bú cuò， yě xǔ xià ge xīng qī hái qù。
▸ English Transcript
Last week I went to Suzhou on a business trip. I got up at six in the morning and left home at 7:15. I took the subway to the train station, then boarded the high-speed train. The high-speed train is fast — we arrived in just over an hour. I left the train station — lots of cars, and lots of people too. My luggage was big, so I took a taxi to the hotel. The hotel is in the city center, very convenient, only a quarter of an hour from the company. Working hours were long and I was a little tired, but I wasn't worried. In the evening I brushed my teeth, washed my face, and went to bed early. Beijing time and Suzhou time are the same, so there's no time-zone problem. I think this trip was great — maybe I'll go again next week.
