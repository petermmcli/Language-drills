► Pinyin
máng cóng

D. use the phone less — 少看手机 (shǎo kàn shǒu jī): 少 + verb = do less of [verb]. Opposite of 多 + verb.

1. much better

► Pinyin
A. 还可以 — hái kě yǐ
B. 好多了 — hǎo duō le
C. 放假 — fàng jià
D. 做完 — zuò wán
► Answer
B. 好多了 (hǎo duō le) — much better. 多了 intensifies: much MORE so. 还可以 means so-so, not much better.

2. environment / surroundings

► Pinyin
A. 众多 — zhòng duō
B. 折叠自行车 — zhé dié zì xíng chē
C. 环境 — huán jìng
D. 报告 — bào gào
► Answer
C. 环境 (huán jìng) — environment. Can refer to the natural environment or a social/workplace setting.

3. double-click / tap twice

► Pinyin
A. 保存 — bǎo cún
B. 少看手机 — shǎo kàn shǒu jī
C. 软件 — ruǎn jiàn
D. 点两下 — diǎn liǎng xià
► Answer
D. 点两下 (diǎn liǎng xià) — click/tap twice (double-click). 下 is a measure word counting the number of taps or clicks.

4. go to work / be at work

► Pinyin
A. 放假 — fàng jià
B. 上班 — shàng bān
C. 从 — cóng
D. 快回来 — kuài huí lái
► Answer
B. 上班 (shàng bān) — go to work / be at work. Opposite: 下班 (xià bān) = finish work. 放假 = take a holiday.

B. 盲从 — máng cóng

Section 3: Fill in the Blank

1. 下个月我们______，太好了！ 🔊

► Pinyin
Xià ge yuè wǒ men ______，tài hǎo le！
► Answer
B. 放假 — 下个月我们放假，太好了！
Xià ge yuè wǒ men fàng jià，tài hǎo le！
We're on holiday next month — great! — 放假 = to have a holiday/break. Opposite is 上班 (go to work).

2. 这个饥店的饥______。 🔊

► Pinyin
Zhè ge fàn diàn de fàn ______。
► Answer
C. 还可以 — 这个饥店的饥还可以。
Zhè ge fàn diàn de fàn hái kě yǐ。
The food at this restaurant is so-so. — 还可以 = not bad / so-so / passable. Less enthusiastic than 好吃 or 非常好吃.

3. 外面下雨了，你______！ 🔊

► Pinyin
Wài miàn xià yǔ le，nǐ ______！
► Answer
A. 快回来 — 外面下雨了，你快回来！
Wài miàn xià yǔ le，nǐ kuài huí lái！
It's raining outside — hurry back! — 快 before a verb = quickly / hurry up and... 快回来 = come back quickly / hurry back.

4. 我今天要写一份______。 🔊

► Pinyin
Wǒ jīn tiān yào xiě yī fèn ______。
► Answer
B. 报告 — 我今天要写一份报告。
Wǒ jīn tiān yào xiě yī fèn bào gào。
I need to write a report today. — 报告 = report (noun) or to report (verb). 份 (fèn) is the measure word for documents and reports.

5. ______北京到上海，要多长时间？ 🔊

► Pinyin
______ Běi jīng dào Shàng hǎi，yào duō cháng shí jiān？
► Answer
B. 从 — 从北京到上海，要多长时间？
Cóng Běi jīng dào Shàng hǎi，yào duō cháng shí jiān？
From Beijing to Shanghai, how long does it take? — 从 A 到 B = from A to B. 从 marks the starting point; 到 marks the endpoint.
Section 4: English → Chinese Sentence Translation

Read the English sentence. Write the Chinese translation on paper or in your head, then toggle the answer. Use 🔊 next to the Chinese answer to hear it.

1. Translate: "Rest more and drink more water."

► Answer
多休息，多喝水。 🔊
Duō xiū xi，duō hē shuǐ。
Rest more, drink more water.
多 + verb = do more of [verb]. This pattern is common for health advice: 多休息、多喝水、多吃水果.

2. Translate: "There are many choices here."

► Answer
这里有众多的选择。 🔊
Zhè lǐ yǒu zhòng duō de xuǎn zé。
There are many choices here.
众多 (zhòng duō) = numerous/many — more formal than 很多. Often used with abstract nouns like choices, people, or possibilities.

3. Translate: "I'm going out to buy coffee."

► Answer
我出去买咋啡。 🔊
Wǒ chū qù mǎi kā fēi。
I'm going out to buy coffee.
出去 + verb = go out to do [something]. No 了 needed when stating a plan or current intention.

4. Translate: "She bought a folding bicycle."

► Answer
她买了一辆折叠自行车。 🔊
Tā mǎi le yī liàng zhé dié zì xíng chē。
She bought a folding bicycle.
辆 (liàng) is the measure word for vehicles. 折叠 = to fold; 自行车 = bicycle.

5. Translate: "I haven't finished the report yet."

► Answer
我还没做完报告。 🔊
Wǒ hái méi zuò wán bào gào。
I haven't finished the report yet.
做完 = result complement: 做 (do) + 完 (finished). 还没 + verb = haven't yet done [verb].
