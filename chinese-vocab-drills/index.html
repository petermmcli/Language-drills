Section 1: Chinese → English

1. 牛津大学 🔊

► Pinyin
niú jīn dà xué
► Answer
B. Oxford University — 牛津大学 (niú jīn dà xué) is Oxford University. Compare: 剑桥大学 = Cambridge; 斯坦福大学 = Stanford.

2. 对了 🔊

► Pinyin
duì le
► Answer
D. by the way / oh right — 对了 (duì le) introduces something you just remembered to add to the conversation. It’s different from 对 alone (correct/right).

3. 晚点儿聊 🔊

► Pinyin
wǎn diǎnr liáo
► Answer
A. chat later — 晚点儿聊 (wǎn diǎnr liáo) means ‘chat later’. 晚 = late/later, 点儿 = a little, 聊 = chat. Compare 晚点儿见 = see you a bit later.

4. 初中 🔊

► Pinyin
chū zhōng
► Answer
B. middle school / junior high — 初中 (chū zhōng) = junior high school (grades 7–9 in China). Education chain: 小学 → 初中 → 高中 → 大学.

5. 通过考试 🔊

► Pinyin
tōng guò kǎo shì
► Answer
C. pass the exam — 通过考试 (tōng guò kǎo shì) = pass an exam. 通过 = pass/go through; 考试 = exam. Compare 考HSK = take the HSK.
Section 2: English → Chinese

Read the English. Choose the correct Hanzi. Use 🔊 on each option to hear it. Toggle ► Pinyin to reveal readings.

1. too expensive

► Pinyin
A. 不太贵 — bù tài guì
B. 太贵了 — tài guì le
C. 不打折 — bù dǎ zhé
D. 打七折 — dǎ qī zhé
► Answer
B. 太贵了 (tài guì le) — too expensive. 太…了 is a fixed intensifier pattern. Don’t confuse with 不太贵 (not too expensive) or 有点儿贵 (a bit expensive).

2. repair / fix

► Pinyin
A. 设计 — shè jì
B. 修 — xiū
C. 洗碗机 — xǐ wǎn jī
D. 通过考试 — tōng guò kǎo shì
► Answer
B. 修 (xiū) — to repair or fix. Common: 修车 (fix a car), 修手机 (fix a phone). Can also mean ‘to build’ in some contexts like 修路 (build a road).

3. not too expensive

► Pinyin
A. 真不错 — zhēn bú cuò
B. 太贵了 — tài guì le
C. 不太贵 — bù tài guì
D. 万 — wàn
► Answer
C. 不太贵 (bù tài guì) — not too expensive. Pattern: 不太 + adj = not very (adj). Compare with 太贵了 (too expensive) — the 太…了 vs 不太 pattern is key.

4. have a chat

► Pinyin
A. 晚点儿聊 — wǎn diǎnr liáo
B. 网上 — wǎng shàng
C. 咖啡店 — kā fēi diàn
D. 聊天儿 — liáo tiānr
► Answer
D. 聊天儿 (liáo tiānr) — to chat. 聊 = chat, 天儿 is a spoken suffix (Beijing dialect). Also written 聊天 without the 儿. Don’t confuse with 晚点儿聊 (chat later).

5. elementary school student

► Pinyin
A. 小学生 — xiǎo xué shēng
B. 初中 — chū zhōng
C. 高中 — gāo zhōng
D. 通过考试 — tōng guò kǎo shì
► Answer
A. 小学生 (xiǎo xué shēng) — elementary school student. Pattern: school level + 生 = student at that level. 初中 and 高中 are school levels, not students.
Section 3: Fill in the Blank

Choose the word that best completes the sentence. Use 🔊 to hear the full sentence with the correct answer filled in.

1. 这件衣服______。 🔊

► Pinyin
Zhè jiàn yī fu ______。
► Answer
B. 不打折 — 这件衣服不打折。
Zhè jiàn yī fu bù dǎ zhé。
This item of clothing has no discount. — 不打折 = no discount (literally ‘not hit-discount’). 打折 alone = apply a discount. Compare 打七折 = 30% off.

2. 你的中文说得______！ 🔊

► Pinyin
Nǐ de Zhōng wén shuō de ______！
► Answer
B. 真不错 — 你的中文说得真不错！
Nǐ de Zhōng wén shuō de zhēn bú cuò！
Your Chinese is really great! — 真 adds strong emphasis: 真不错 is much warmer praise than 不错 alone. Note: 不 becomes 2nd tone (bú) before 4th-tone 错.

3. 我们去______坐坐吧。 🔊

► Pinyin
Wǒ men qù ______ zuò zuo ba。
► Answer
B. 咖啡店 — 我们去咖啡店坐坐吧。
Wǒ men qù kā fēi diàn zuò zuo ba。
Let’s go sit for a bit at the coffee shop. — 坐坐 (verb reduplicated) softens the suggestion: ‘sit for a bit’. 吧 makes it a gentle suggestion. 咖啡店 = coffee shop (kafēi + diàn).

4. 这件衣服的______很漂亮。 🔊

► Pinyin
Zhè jiàn yī fu de ______ hěn piào liàng。
► Answer
B. 设计 — 这件衣服的设计很漂亮。
Zhè jiàn yī fu de shè jì hěn piào liàng。
The design of this clothing is very beautiful. — 设计 (shèjì) = design (noun or verb). As noun: 设计很漂亮 (beautiful design). The distractors 颜色 (color) and 大小 (size) are also common things to comment on about clothing.

5. 我在______买了一件衬衫。 🔊

► Pinyin
Wǒ zài ______ mǎi le yí jiàn chèn shān。
► Answer
B. 网上 — 我在网上买了一件衬衫。
Wǒ zài wǎng shàng mǎi le yí jiàn chèn shān。
I bought a shirt online. — 在网上 = online. Pattern: 在 + location + verb. 一 changes to t2 (yí) before t4 件.
Section 4: English → Chinese Sentence Translation

Read the English sentence. Write the Chinese translation on paper or in your head, then toggle the answer. Use 🔊 next to the Chinese answer to hear it.

1. Translate: "This shirt is 30% off — you pay 70% of the original price."

► Answer
这件衣服打七折。 🔊
Zhè jiàn yī fu dǎ qī zhé。
This item of clothing is 30% off (you pay 70% of the original price).
打X折 = pay X tenths of the price. 七折 = 70% of original = 30% discount. Lower number = bigger discount: 打五折 = 50% off.

2. Translate: "My younger sister is a high school student."

► Answer
我妹妹是高中生。 🔊
Wǒ mèi mei shì gāo zhōng shēng。
My younger sister is a high school student.
高中 = senior high school (grades 10–12 in China). Add 生 to make 高中生 = high school student. School chain: 小学 → 初中 → 高中 → 大学.

3. Translate: "This coat costs twenty thousand yuan."

► Answer
这件衣服要两万块钱。 🔊
Zhè jiàn yī fu yào liǎng wàn kuài qián。
This coat costs twenty thousand yuan.
万 (wàn) = 10,000. Chinese groups numbers by 万, not thousands: 20,000 = 两万; 100,000 = 十万; 1,000,000 = 百万. Use 两, not 二, before measure words.

4. Translate: "Does your kitchen have a dishwasher?"

► Answer
你家厨房有洗碗机吗？ 🔊
Nǐ jiā chú fáng yǒu xǐ wǎn jī ma？
Does your kitchen have a dishwasher?
洗碗机 = dishwasher, literally ‘wash-bowl-machine’. 厨房 = kitchen (from this lesson’s batch). Pattern 有…吗 = do you have…?

5. Translate: "Do you want to go to the coffee shop together?"

► Answer
你想一起去咖啡店吗？ 🔊
Nǐ xiǎng yī qǐ qù kā fēi diàn ma？
Do you want to go to the coffee shop together?
一起 (yīqǐ) = together. Always placed before the verb: 一起去 = go together, 一起吃 = eat together. This sentence combines two words from today’s drill!
