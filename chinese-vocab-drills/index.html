Section 1: Chinese → English

1. 每个夏天 🔊

► Pinyin
Měi gè xià tiān
► Answer
B. every summer — 每个夏天 (měi gè xià tiān): 每 = every, 个 = (measure word), 夏天 = summer. Compare 每个冬天 = every winter.

2. 往 🔊

► Pinyin
wǎng
► Answer
A. toward, in the direction of — 往 (wǎng) is a preposition indicating direction: 往左走 = go left, 往前走 = go forward. Don't confuse with 望 (wàng) = to gaze at.

3. 听着音乐做作业 🔊

► Pinyin
tīng zhe yīn yuè zuò zuò yè
► Answer
C. do homework while listening to music — 听着音乐做作业: the 着 after a verb signals a simultaneous action (V着…做). Compare 看着电视吃饭 = eat while watching TV.

4. 哈尔滨 🔊

► Pinyin
Hā ěr bīn
► Answer
A. Harbin — 哈尔滨 (Hā ěr bīn) is the capital of Heilongjiang province in northeast China, famous for its ice festival and Russian architectural influence.

5. 万圣节 🔊

► Pinyin
Wàn shèng jié
► Answer
D. Halloween — 万圣节 (Wàn shèng jié): 万 = ten thousand, 圣 = saint. Compare: 感恩节 = Thanksgiving, 圣诞节 = Christmas.
Section 2: English → Chinese

1. tired

► Pinyin
A. 饱 — bǎo
B. 累 — lèi
C. 一般 — yī bān
D. 雪 — xuě
► Answer
B. 累 (lèi) — tired, exhausted. 饱 (bǎo) = full (after eating). 一般 = so-so / ordinary. 雪 = snow.

2. help people

► Pinyin
A. 帮助人 — bāng zhù rén
B. 买东西 — mǎi dōng xi
C. 往左走 — wǎng zuǒ zǒu
D. 别忘了给我打电话 — bié wàng le gěi wǒ dǎ diàn huà
► Answer
A. 帮助人 (bāng zhù rén) — help people. 帮助 = to help, 人 = people. Can also say 帮人 in casual speech.

3. don't forget to call me

► Pinyin
A. 往后走 — wǎng hòu zǒu
B. 一件很快乐的事情 — yī jiàn hěn kuài lè de shì qing
C. 别忘了给我打电话 — bié wàng le gěi wǒ dǎ diàn huà
D. 请几天假 — qǐng jǐ tiān jià
► Answer
C. 别忘了给我打电话 (bié wàng le gěi wǒ dǎ diàn huà) — 别 = don't, 忘了 = forget, 给我打电话 = call me.

4. walk to the left / go left

► Pinyin
A. 往后走 — wǎng hòu zǒu
B. 往左走 — wǎng zuǒ zǒu
C. 一般 — yī bān
D. 往 — wǎng
► Answer
B. 往左走 (wǎng zuǒ zǒu) — go left. 往 + direction + 走. Compare: 往右走 = go right, 往前走 = go forward, 往后走 = go backward.

5. ordinary, so-so, generally

► Pinyin
A. 累 — lèi
B. 饱 — bǎo
C. 决定 — jué dìng
D. 一般 — yī bān
► Answer
D. 一般 (yī bān) — ordinary/so-so as adjective; generally/usually as adverb. A common polite understatement: 还行 and 一般 both mean "so-so".
Section 3: Fill in the Blank

Choose the word that best completes the sentence. Use 🔊 to hear the full sentence with the correct answer filled in.

1. 你的中文______很好。 🔊

► Pinyin
Nǐ de Zhōng wén ______ hěn hǎo。
► Answer
B. 发音 — 你的中文发音很好。
Nǐ de Zhōng wén fā yīn hěn hǎo。
Your Chinese pronunciation is very good. — 发音 (fāyīn) = pronunciation. 发 here means 'to produce/emit', 音 = sound.

2. 帮助别人是______。 🔊

► Pinyin
Bāng zhù bié rén shì ______。
► Answer
C. 一件很快乐的事情 — 帮助别人是一件很快乐的事情。
Bāng zhù bié rén shì yī jiàn hěn kuài lè de shì qing。
Helping others is a very happy thing. — 一件……的事情 is a common pattern meaning 'a … thing/matter'. 件 is the measure word for matters/events.

3. 我想______。 🔊

► Pinyin
Wǒ xiǎng ______。
► Answer
A. 请几天假 — 我想请几天假。
Wǒ xiǎng qǐng jǐ tiān jià。
I'd like to take a few days off. — 请假 = to request leave/time off. 请几天假 = take a few days off. Compare: 请一天假 = take one day off, 请一个星期假 = take a week off.

4. 不要______，要往前走。 🔊

► Pinyin
Bú yào ______，yào wǎng qián zǒu。
► Answer
B. 往后走 — 不要往后走，要往前走。
Bú yào wǎng hòu zǒu，yào wǎng qián zǒu。
Don't go backward, go forward. — 往后走 = go backward. 后 = back/behind. The 往 + direction + 走 pattern works for all directions.

5. 她生病了，要______。 🔊

► Pinyin
Tā shēng bìng le，yào ______。
► Answer
C. 请一个星期假 — 她生病了，要请一个星期假。
Tā shēng bìng le，yào qǐng yī gè xīng qī jià。
She is sick and needs to take a week off. — 请一个星期假 = take one week off. 一个星期 = one week. Compare 请两天假 = take two days off.
Section 4: English → Chinese Sentence Translation

Read the English sentence. Write the Chinese translation on paper or in your head, then toggle the answer. Use 🔊 next to the Chinese answer to hear it.

1. Translate: "When I eat, I don't use my phone."

► Answer
我吃饭的时候不用手机。 🔊
Wǒ chī fàn de shí hou bù yòng shǒu jī。
When I eat, I don't use my phone.
的时候 follows a verb or time expression to mean 'when / at the time of'. Pattern: [action]的时候 + [main clause].

2. Translate: "I haven't decided yet where to go on holiday."

► Answer
我还没决定假期去哪里。 🔊
Wǒ hái méi jué dìng jià qī qù nǎ lǐ。
I haven't decided yet where to go on holiday.
决定 (juédìng) = to decide; can also be a noun meaning 'decision'. 还没决定 = haven't decided yet.

3. Translate: "I'm full — I can't eat any more."

► Answer
我饱了，不能再吃了。 🔊
Wǒ bǎo le，bù néng zài chī le。
I'm full — I can't eat any more.
饱 (bǎo) = full after eating. The 了 after 饱 marks a change of state. Don't confuse with 累 (lèi) = tired.

4. Translate: "I want to go shopping with my friend this weekend."

► Answer
我想这个周末和朋友一起去买东西。 🔊
Wǒ xiǎng zhè ge zhōu mò hé péng yǒu yī qǐ qù mǎi dōng xi。
I want to go shopping with my friend this weekend.
买东西 (mǎi dōngxi) = buy things / go shopping. 东西 literally means 'east-west' but means 'things/stuff'.

5. Translate: "It snows in Harbin every winter."

► Answer
哈尔滨每个冬天都下雪。 🔊
Hā ěr bīn měi gè dōng tiān dōu xià xuě。
It snows in Harbin every winter.
下雪 (xià xuě) = to snow (lit. 'fall snow'). 每个冬天都 = every winter (都 adds emphasis to 'every'). Compare 下雨 = to rain.
