Section 1: Chinese → English

1. 太…了 🔊

► Pinyin
tài…le
► Answer
C. too...! — The pattern 太…了 (tài…le) expresses excess or strong degree: 太好了！= Great! / Awesome! 太贵了 = Too expensive. 太 intensifies the adjective; 了 marks the result as notable.

2. 有问题再找我 🔊

► Pinyin
yǒu wèntí zài zhǎo wǒ
► Answer
A. If you have a problem, come find me again — 有问题再找我 is a polite customer-service phrase. 有问题 = have a problem; 再 = again; 找我 = come find me / contact me. Often said by delivery people when dropping off packages.

3. 评论 🔊

► Pinyin
pínglùn
► Answer
C. comment / review — 评论 (pínglùn) means a comment or review. In online shopping: 好评 = good review, 差评 = bad review, 评论 = general comment/review. Don't confuse with 评价 (píngjià = overall rating/evaluation).

4. 亲人 🔊

► Pinyin
qīnrén
► Answer
A. family member / loved one — 亲人 (qīnrén) means close family or loved ones. 亲 = intimate/close; 人 = person. Compare: 亲爱的 = dear (term of endearment); 家人 = family members.

5. 周日 🔊

► Pinyin
zhōurì
► Answer
D. Sunday — 周日 (zhōurì) = Sunday (formal written style). Also: 星期天 / 周天 / 礼拜天. The weekdays go 周一, 周二…周六, then 周日. 日 is the formal character for "day," also meaning sun/Japan.
Section 2: English → Chinese

Read the English. Choose the correct Hanzi. Use 🔊 on each option to hear it. Toggle ► Pinyin to reveal readings.

1. Leave it on the first floor

► Pinyin
A. 在…上 — zài…shàng
B. 放在一楼 — fàng zài yī lóu
C. 发货 — fāhuò
D. 早 — zǎo
► Answer
B. 放在一楼 (fàng zài yī lóu) — Leave it on the first floor. 放在 = put/place at; 一楼 = first floor. This is a common delivery instruction: 放在一楼 or 放到门口.

2. To ship the goods

► Pinyin
A. 送货上门是免费的 — sòng huò shàng mén shì miǎnfèi de
B. 在…上 — zài…shàng
C. 放在一楼 — fàng zài yī lóu
D. 发货 — fāhuò
► Answer
D. 发货 (fāhuò) — to ship / dispatch goods. 发 = send/dispatch; 货 = goods/cargo. The opposite is 收货 (shōu huò) = receive goods. 发货时间 = shipping time.

3. Home delivery is free

► Pinyin
A. 放在一楼 — fàng zài yī lóu
B. 送货上门是免费的 — sòng huò shàng mén shì miǎnfèi de
C. 早 — zǎo
D. 发货 — fāhuò
► Answer
B. 送货上门是免费的 — Home delivery is free. 送货 = deliver goods; 上门 = to the door; 免费 = free of charge; 的 marks the predicate. 上门 literally means "up to the door" and implies door-to-door service.

4. on... / on top of... (pattern)

► Pinyin
A. 早 — zǎo
B. 在…上 — zài…shàng
C. 放在一楼 — fàng zài yī lóu
D. 发货 — fāhuò
► Answer
B. 在…上 (zài…shàng) — a location pattern meaning "on / on top of". E.g., 在桌子上 = on the table; 在网上 = online. 在 marks location; 上 specifies the surface. Compare 在…里 (inside) and 在…旁边 (next to).

5. early / good morning

► Pinyin
A. 在…上 — zài…shàng
B. 发货 — fāhuò
C. 早 — zǎo
D. 放在一楼 — fàng zài yī lóu
► Answer
C. 早 (zǎo) — early, or a casual "Good morning!" greeting. The full form is 早上好 (zǎošang hǎo). Just saying 早！ is very common among friends and colleagues. The same character forms 早饭 (breakfast) and 早上 (morning).
Section 3: Fill in the Blank

Choose the word that best completes the sentence. Use 🔊 to hear the full sentence with the correct answer filled in.

1. 他的工作是给顾客______。 🔊

► Pinyin
Tā de gōngzuò shì gěi gùkè ______。
► Answer
B. 送快递 — 他的工作是给顾客送快递。
Tā de gōngzuò shì gěi gùkè sòng kuàidì。
His job is to deliver packages to customers. — 送快递 = deliver express packages. The person who does it is 快递员 (kuàidìyén). 发货 (dispatch goods) is what sellers do; 送快递 is what the courier does in the field.

2. 快递员很好，我给他打了五______。 🔊

► Pinyin
Kuàidìyuán hěn hǎo，wǒ gěi tā dǎ le wǔ ______。
► Answer
C. 星 — 快递员很好，我给他打了五星。
Kuàidìyuán hěn hǎo，wǒ gěi tā dǎ le wǔ xīng。
The delivery person was great; I gave them 5 stars. — 星 here means "star" in a rating. 打五星 = give 5 stars. 五星好评 = 5-star review. 块 is a currency measure word; 分 means point/minute — both wrong here.

3. 我每天七点吃______。 🔊

► Pinyin
Wǒ měi tiān qī diǎn chī ______。
► Answer
B. 早饭 — 我每天七点吃早饭。
Wǒ měi tiān qī diǎn chī zǎofàn。
I eat breakfast every day at 7 o'clock. — 早饭 = breakfast (also 早餐). 七点 (7 o’clock) signals morning, ruling out 午饭 (lunch, eaten around noon) and 晚饭 (dinner, eaten in the evening).

4. 他______的时候，先换鞋。 🔊

► Pinyin
Tā ______ de shíhou，xiān huàn xié。
► Answer
A. 进门 — 他进门的时候，先换鞋。
Tā jìnmén de shíhou，xiān huàn xié。
When he enters the house, he first changes his shoes. — 进门 = enter through the door. Its opposite is 出门 (go out). 上楼 = go upstairs — shoe changing happens at the door, not on the stairs.

5. 他进来以后，跟大家说了______。 🔊

► Pinyin
Tā jìnlái yǐhòu，gēn dàjiā shuō le ______。
► Answer
B. 晚上好 — 他进来以后，跟大家说了晚上好。
Tā jìnlái yǐhòu，gēn dàjiā shuō le wǎnshang hǎo。
After he came in, he said "good evening" to everyone. — 晚上好 = good evening (greeting when meeting someone). 晚安 = good night (farewell before sleeping). The context — entering the room and greeting people — calls for 晚上好, not 晚安.
Section 4: English → Chinese Sentence Translation

Read the English sentence. Write the Chinese translation on paper or in your head, then toggle the answer. Use 🔊 next to the Chinese answer to hear it.

1. Translate: "Leave the food that hasn't been finished on the table."

► Answer
把还没吃完的饭放在桌子上。 🔊
Bǎ hái méi chī wán de fàn fàng zài zhuōzi shàng。
Leave the food that hasn't been finished on the table.
还没吃完的饭 is a 的-clause noun phrase: 还没吃完 (haven't finished eating) + 的 + 饭 (food). The 把 structure places the object before the verb phrase 放在桌子上.

2. Translate: "When using your phone, you should protect your eyes."

► Answer
用手机的时候，你应该保护眼睛。 🔊
Yòng shǒujī de shíhou，nǐ yīnggāi bǎohù yǎnjīng。
When using your phone, you should protect your eyes.
保护 (bǎohù) = to protect. 应该 + verb = should/ought to. 用手机的时候 is a time clause: "at the time of using a phone."

3. Translate: "The customer wrote a good review for the delivery person."

► Answer
顾客给快递员写了好评。 🔊
Gùkè gěi kuàidìyuán xiě le hǎopíng。
The customer wrote a good review for the delivery person.
顾客 (gùkè) = customer. Compare 买家 (buyer) and 卖家 (seller). 给快递员写了好评 = wrote a positive review for the delivery person. 好评 vs 差评 = good review vs bad review.

4. Translate: "I didn't finish my breakfast this morning."

► Answer
我今天早上没吃完早饭。 🔊
Wǒ jīntiān zǎoshang méi chī wán zǎofàn。
I didn't finish my breakfast this morning.
没吃完 = didn't finish eating (result complement negated with 没). Compare: 吃完了 = finished eating; 没吃完 = didn't finish; 还没吃完 = still haven't finished.

5. Translate: "Before going to sleep, we say good night."

► Answer
睡觉以前，我们说晚安。 🔊
Shuìjiào yǐqián，wǒmen shuō wǎnān。
Before going to sleep, we say good night.
晚安 (wǎn’ān) = good night (literally "peaceful evening"). Said when parting or going to bed. Compare: 晚上好 (good evening, a greeting); 晚安 (good night, a farewell before sleep).
