1. 印度菜 🔊

► Pinyin
yìndù cài
► Answer
B. Indian food — 印度菜 (yìndù cài) means Indian cuisine. 印度 = India, 菜 = cuisine/dish.

2. 大路 🔊

► Pinyin
dà lù
► Answer
C. main road — 大路 (dà lù) = main road or avenue. 大 = big, 路 = road. Don't confuse with 小路 = alley/small path.

3. 季节 🔊

► Pinyin
jì jié
► Answer
A. season — 季节 (jì jié) = season. The four seasons: 春夏秋冬 (spring, summer, autumn, winter).

4. 挺好的 🔊

► Pinyin
tǐng hǎo de
► Answer
D. pretty good — 挺好的 (tǐng hǎo de) is a casual way to say 'quite good / pretty good.' 挺 intensifies like 很 but sounds more colloquial.

5. 桌子上 🔊

► Pinyin
zhuōzi shàng
► Answer
B. on the table — 桌子上 (zhuōzi shàng) = on top of the table. 上 adds 'on top' to any noun: 桌子 (table) → 桌子上 (on the table).

1. wash clean

B. 安全 — ān quán

D. 写下来 — xiě xiàlái
► Answer
C. 洗干净 (xǐ gānjìng) — wash until clean. 干净 = clean; 洗干净 is a verb+result complement meaning 'wash clean.'

2. passport

► Pinyin
A. 护照 — hù zhào
B. 洗干净 — xǐ gānjìng
C. 电子邮件 — diànzǐ yóujiàn
D. 安全 — ān quán
► Answer
A. 护照 (hù zhào) — passport. 护 = protect, 照 = photo/document. Also 照 appears in 照片 (photo) and 驾照 (driver's license).

3. email

► Pinyin
A. 短信 — duǎnxìn
B. 护照 — hù zhào
C. 无人机 — wúrénjī
D. 电子邮件 — diànzǐ yóujiàn
► Answer
D. 电子邮件 (diànzǐ yóujiàn) — email. 电子 = electronic, 邮件 = mail/post. Compare 短信 = text message (shorter and more casual).

4. safe / safety

► Pinyin
A. 无人机 — wúrénjī
B. 安全 — ān quán
C. 大路 — dà lù
D. 洗干净 — xǐ gānjìng

B. 安全 (ān quán) — safe / safety. 安 = peaceful, 全 = complete/whole. Opposite: 危险 (wéixiǎn) = dangerous.

Section 3: Fill in the Blank

Choose the word that best completes the sentence. Use 🔊 to hear the full sentence with the correct answer filled in.

1. 我想______，但是手机没有电了。 🔊

► Pinyin
Wǒ xiǎng ______，dànshì shǒujī méiyǒu diàn le。
► Answer
B. 发短信 — 我想发短信，但是手机没有电了。
Wǒ xiǎng fā duǎnxìn，dànshì shǒujī méiyǒu diàn le。
I wanted to send a text, but my phone ran out of battery. — 发短信 = send a text (发=send, 短信=SMS). 没有电了 = no battery left.

2. 冬天的时候，我最喜欢______。 🔊

► Pinyin
Dōngtiān de shíhou，wǒ zuì xǐhuān ______。
► Answer
B. 滑冰 — 冬天的时候，我最喜欢滑冰。
Dōngtiān de shíhou，wǒ zuì xǐhuān huábīng。
In winter, I love ice skating the most. — 滑冰 = ice skate (滑=slide, 冰=ice). Compare 游泳 (swim) and 跑步 (run/jog).

3. 你能把你的地址______吗？ 🔊

► Pinyin
Nǐ néng bǎ nǐ de dìzhǐ ______ ma？
► Answer
B. 写下来 — 你能把你的地址写下来吗？
Nǐ néng bǎ nǐ de dìzhǐ xiě xiàlái ma？
Can you write down your address? — 写下来 = write it down (write + result complement 下来). The 把 structure moves the object before the verb.

4. ______，这是什么？ 🔊

► Pinyin
______，zhè shì shénme？
► Answer
C. 让我看看 — 让我看看，这是什么？
Ràng wǒ kànkan，zhè shì shénme？
Let me have a look — what is this? — 让我看看 = let me see. Doubling 看→看看 softens the tone. Compare 给我看 (show me) and 告诉我 (tell me).

5. 你有没有收到我的______？ 🔊

► Pinyin
Nǐ yǒu méi yǒu shōudào wǒ de ______？
► Answer
A. 短信 — 你有没有收到我的短信？
Nǐ yǒu méi yǒu shōudào wǒ de duǎnxìn？
Did you receive my text message? — 短信 = text/SMS (短=short, 信=letter/message). 有没有 forms a yes/no question without 吗.
Section 4: English → Chinese Sentence Translation

Read the English sentence. Write the Chinese translation on paper or in your head, then toggle the answer. Use 🔊 next to the Chinese answer to hear it.

1. Translate: "Oh I see — so you take the subway to work every day."

► Answer
哦，原来你每天坐地铁去上班。 🔊
Ò，yuánlái nǐ měitiān zuò dìtiě qù shàngbān。
Oh I see — so you take the subway to work every day.
哦 (ò, 4th tone) expresses sudden understanding. 原来 = 'so it turns out that / I see now.' 上班 = go to work.

2. Translate: "I bought two books at the bookstore."

► Answer
我在书店买了两本书。 🔊
Wǒ zài shūdiàn mǎile liǎng běn shū。
I bought two books at the bookstore.
本 (běn) is the measure word for books. 两本书 = two books. 书店 = bookstore. Compare: 一本书 (one book), 三本书 (three books).

3. Translate: "My passport is inside the bag."

► Answer
我的护照在包里。 🔊
Wǒ de hùzhào zài bāolǐ。
My passport is inside the bag.
包里 = inside the bag (包=bag, 里=inside). Location words like 里, 上, 下 follow the noun: 桌子上 (on the table), 包里 (inside the bag).

4. Translate: "Can you give him my phone number?"

► Answer
你能把我的电话号码给他吗？ 🔊
Nǐ néng bǎ wǒ de diànhuà hàomǎ gěi tā ma？
Can you give him my phone number?
给他 = give (to) him. With 把: 把…给他 = give [it] to him. This 把 structure moves the object before the verb.

5. Translate: "Please put your phone on the table."

► Answer
请把你的手机放在桌子上。 🔊
Qǐng bǎ nǐ de shǒujī fàngzài zhuōzi shàng。
Please put your phone on the table.
放在 = put at/on (放=place/put, 在=at). Structure: 把…放在… = put [object] at [location].
