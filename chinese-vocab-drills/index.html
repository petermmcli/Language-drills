Section 1: Chinese → English

1. 太好了 🔊

► Pinyin
tài hǎo le
► Answer
B. That's great! — 太好了 (tài hǎo le) is an exclamation meaning "That's great!" or "Wonderful!" 太 intensifies the adjective, and 了 marks a new situation. A go-to positive reaction.

2. 用……付钱 🔊

► Pinyin
yòng… fù qián
► Answer
A. to pay with… — 用……付钱 (yòng… fù qián): 用 = use/with, 付钱 = pay money. The pattern slots in the payment method: 用微信付钱 = pay with WeChat; 用现金付钱 = pay with cash.

3. 新疆 🔊

► Pinyin
Xīnjiāng
► Answer
D. Xinjiang — 新疆 (Xīnjiāng) is a large autonomous region in northwestern China. 新 = new, 疆 = boundary/frontier. Known for lamb skewers (羊肉串), naan bread, and Uyghur culture.

4. 试一下 🔊

► Pinyin
shì yī xià
► Answer
C. to give it a try — 试一下 (shì yī xià): 试 = to try, 一下 softens the action. Often used as a casual invitation: 你试一下 = You try it / Have a go. Also used as self-encouragement: 我试一下 = Let me give it a shot.

5. 发给 🔊

► Pinyin
fā gěi
► Answer
D. to send to — 发给 (fā gěi): 发 = to send/transmit, 给 = to/for (direction). E.g., 发给我 = send it to me; 把文件发给他 = send the file to him. Often used for messages, files, emails.
Section 2: English → Chinese

1. drink more water

► Pinyin
A. 鸡蛋汤 — jī dàn tāng
B. 多喝水 — duō hē shuǐ
C. 坐地铁回家 — zuò dìtiě huí jiā
D. 有点忙 — yǒudiǎn máng
► Answer
B. 多喝水 (duō hē shuǐ) — drink more water. 多 + verb = do more of that action. A beloved Chinese health reminder — you'll hear it constantly from family!

2. egg soup / egg drop soup

► Pinyin
A. 比昨天忙一点 — bǐ zuótiān máng yīdiǎn
B. 下载好了 — xiàzǎi hǎo le
C. 鸡蛋汤 — jī dàn tāng
D. 发给 — fā gěi
► Answer
C. 鸡蛋汤 (jī dàn tāng) — egg soup / egg drop soup. 鸡蛋 = egg (literally: chicken-egg), 汤 = soup. A simple, comforting dish. Don't confuse 蛋 (egg) with 但 (but) — similar pronunciation, very different meaning!

3. a bit busier than yesterday

► Pinyin
A. 有点忙 — yǒudiǎn máng
B. 太好了 — tài hǎo le
C. 星期五 — xīngqī wǔ
D. 比昨天忙一点 — bǐ zuótiān máng yīdiǎn
► Answer
D. 比昨天忙一点 (bǐ zuótiān máng yīdiǎn) — a bit busier than yesterday. 比 introduces a comparison: A 比 B + adjective. 一点 softens the degree. Compare with 有点忙 = somewhat busy (no comparison reference).

4. download complete / finished downloading

► Pinyin
A. 还没做完 — hái méi zuò wán
B. 试一下 — shì yī xià
C. 下载好了 — xiàzǎi hǎo le
D. 做好 — zuò hǎo
► Answer
C. 下载好了 (xiàzǎi hǎo le) — the download is done. 下载 = download, 好了 = done/ready (resultative complement). Compare with 还没做完 = haven't finished yet — opposite states. 好 as a resultative complement signals completion.

5. take the subway home

► Pinyin
A. 新疆 — Xīnjiāng
B. 在家休息 — zài jiā xiūxi
C. 找一下 — zhǎo yī xià
D. 坐地铁回家 — zuò dìtiě huí jiā
► Answer
D. 坐地铁回家 (zuò dìtiě huí jiā) — take the subway home. 坐 = ride/sit in, 地铁 = subway (literally: underground iron), 回家 = return home. 坐 is used for vehicles you sit in: 坐车, 坐飞机, 坐船.
Section 3: Fill in the Blank

1. 今天我______，所以没法来。 🔊

► Pinyin
Jīntiān wǒ ______，suǒyǐ méifǎ lái。
► Answer
B. 有点忙 — 今天我有点忙，所以没法来。
Jīntiān wǒ yǒudiǎn máng，suǒyǐ méifǎ lái。
Today I'm a little busy, so I can't make it. — 有点 + adjective = a little / somewhat. 没法 = have no way to, can't. 所以 = therefore, so.

2. 医生建议我______，对身体好。 🔊

► Pinyin
Yīshēng jiànyì wǒ ______，duì shēntǐ hǎo。
► Answer
B. 多做运动 — 医生建议我多做运动，对身体好。
Yīshēng jiànyì wǒ duō zuò yùndòng，duì shēntǐ hǎo。
The doctor suggested I exercise more — it's good for my health. — 多 + verb = do more of that action (多做运动, 多喝水, 多吃菜). 对身体好 = good for the body. A tricky distractor: 在家休息 (rest at home) might also be doctor's advice but doesn't fit grammatically as a general recommendation here.

3. 你可以______吗？ 🔊

► Pinyin
Nǐ kěyǐ ______ma？
► Answer
C. 帮我一个忙 — 你可以帮我一个忙吗？
Nǐ kěyǐ bāng wǒ yī gè mángma？
Can you do me a favor? — 帮我一个忙 = literally 'help me one busy-task'. 帮 + person + 一个忙 is a fixed polite request. The distractors 试一下 and 找一下 are grammatically possible but change the meaning entirely.

4. 你最近______？听说你生病了。 🔊

► Pinyin
Nǐ zuìjìn ______？Tīngshuō nǐ shēngbìng le。
► Answer
B. 身体好吗 — 你最近身体好吗？听说你生病了。
Nǐ zuìjìn shēntǐ hǎo ma？Tīngshuō nǐ shēngbìng le。
How have you been feeling lately? I heard you were sick. — 身体好吗 = How's your health? A warmer, more caring greeting than 你好吗. 最近 = recently/lately. 听说 = I heard that (hearsay marker).

5. 我们______下午三点开会，可以吗？ 🔊

► Pinyin
Wǒmen ______xiàwǔ sān diǎn kāihuì，kěyǐ ma？
► Answer
C. 星期五 — 我们星期五下午三点开会，可以吗？
Wǒmen xīngqī wǔ xiàwǔ sān diǎn kāihuì，kěyǐ ma？
Let's meet on Friday at 3 PM — does that work? — Days: 星期一 Mon, 星期二 Tue … 星期五 Fri, 星期六 Sat, 星期天/日 Sun. Time expressions come before the verb in Chinese: [when] + [where] + verb.
Section 4: English → Chinese Sentence Translation

Read the English sentence. Write the Chinese translation on paper or in your head, then toggle the answer. Use 🔊 next to the Chinese answer to hear it.

1. Translate: "I haven't finished the report yet."

► Answer
我还没做完报告。 🔊
Wǒ hái méi zuò wán bàogào。
I haven't finished the report yet.
还没 + verb = haven't [done] yet. 做完 = do to completion (完 is a resultative complement). This structure is common for ongoing tasks: 还没吃完, 还没写完, 还没看完.

2. Translate: "Please finish it properly before you submit."

► Answer
请提交之前把它做好。 🔊
Qǐng tíjiāo zhīqián bǎ tā zuòhǎo。
Please finish it properly before you submit.
做好 = do well / complete properly (好 as a result complement). 把 construction: 把 + object + verb-result emphasizes the disposal of the object. 之前 = before.

3. Translate: "This medicine is a bit bitter — can I add some sugar?"

► Answer
这个药有点苦，可以加点糖吗？ 🔊
Zhègè yào yǒudiǎn kǔ，kěyǐ jiā diǎn táng ma？
This medicine is a bit bitter — can I add some sugar?
苦 (kǔ, 3rd tone) = bitter. The four basic tastes: 甜 (tián) sweet, 酸 (suān) sour, 苦 (kǔ) bitter, 辣 (là) spicy. 加点糖 = add a little sugar (点 = a bit, used after verbs).

4. Translate: "You look a bit under the weather. It's better to stay home and rest today."

► Answer
你看起来有点不舒服，今天在家休息比较好。 🔊
Nǐ kànqǐlái yǒudiǎn bù shūfu，jīntiān zài jiā xiūxi bǐjiào hǎo。
You look a bit under the weather. It's better to stay home and rest today.
在家休息 = rest at home (在 + location + verb). 看起来 = to look/appear. 比较好 = comparatively better / it would be better to. 不舒服 = uncomfortable / unwell.

5. Translate: "Can you help me look for that file? I can't find it."

► Answer
你可以帮我找一下那个文件吗？我找不到。 🔊
Nǐ kěyǐ bāng wǒ zhǎo yīxià nàgè wénjiàn ma？Wǒ zhǎo bú dào。
Can you help me look for that file? I can't find it.
找一下 = look for / give it a search (一下 softens the request). 找不到 = can't find (potential complement: verb + 不 + result). Contrast with 找到了 = found it! and 找到没有 = did you find it?
