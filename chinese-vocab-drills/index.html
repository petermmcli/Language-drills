1. 收货地址 🔊

► Pinyin
shŏu huò dìzhǐ
► Answer
C. delivery address — 收货地址 (shŏu huò dìzhǐ) is specifically where you receive goods. Compare with 送货地址 = shipping/from address.

2. 送外卖的人 🔊

► Pinyin
sòng wàimài de rén
► Answer
B. food delivery person — 送外卖的人 = the person who delivers food. 送 = deliver, 外卖 = food delivery (takeout), 的人 = the person who...

3. 点 🔊

► Pinyin
diǎn
► Answer
A. to order / to tap — 点 (diǎn) in food context means to order dishes. Also means to tap/click in digital contexts (点外卖 = order delivery). Don’t confuse with 点 as a measure word for “a little”.

4. 美团 🔊

► Pinyin
Měituán
► Answer
B. Meituan — 美团 (Měituán) is China’s major platform for food delivery, hotel booking, and local services. 美 = beautiful/good, 团 = group/team.

5. 还 🔊

► Pinyin
hái
► Answer
D. still / also / even — 还 (hái) as an adverb means “still” or “also.” Note: when meaning “return” it’s pronounced huán. 还有 = there’s also..., 还是 = still is.

1. need to / must (obligation)

► Pinyin
A. 还 — hái
B. 得 — děi
C. 用 — yòng
D. 点 — diǎn
► Answer
B. 得 (děi) — means “need to / must” for obligation. Don’t confuse with 得 (de) = structural particle, or 得 (dé) = to get/obtain. Context: 我得去做检查 = I need to go get checked.

2. it depends / need to see

► Pinyin
A. 要看 — yào kàn
B. 用 — yòng
C. 去掉 — qù diào
D. 得 — děi
► Answer
A. 要看 (yào kàn) — literally “need to look,” used as “it depends” in conversation. E.g., 要看你想吸啥就吸啤 = it depends on what you want.

3. to use

► Pinyin
A. 得 — děi
B. 还 — hái
C. 点 — diǎn
D. 用 — yòng
► Answer
D. 用 (yòng) — to use. 我用美团点外卖 = I use Meituan to order delivery. 用什么付款 = pay with what?

4. Taobao Flash Buy (fast delivery app)

► Pinyin
A. 美团 — Měituán
B. 饿了么 — Èle me
C. 淘宝闪购 — Táobǎo Shǎngòu
D. 收货地址 — shŏu huò dìzhǐ
► Answer
C. 淘宝闪购 (Táobǎo Shǎngòu) — Taobao’s same-day/flash delivery service. 闪 = flash/instant, 购 = purchase. Part of Alibaba’s ecosystem.

5. to have finished writing (result complement)

► Pinyin
A. 去掉 — qù diào
B. 点 — diǎn
C. 写好 — xiě hǎo
D. 收 — shŏu
► Answer
C. 写好 (xiě hǎo) — to have finished writing. 好 after a verb = result complement indicating completion or satisfactory result. Pattern: 写好 = written (done), 下载好 = downloaded (done), 选好 = chosen (done).
Section 3: Fill in the Blank

Choose the word that best completes the sentence. Use 🔊 to hear the full sentence with the correct answer filled in.

1. 这个地址错了，把它______吧。 🔊

► Pinyin
Zhè ge dìzhǐ cuò le， bǎ tā ______ba。
► Answer
B. 去掉 — 这个地址错了，把它去掉吧。
Zhè ge dìzhǐ cuò le， bǎ tā qùdiào ba。
This address is wrong — let’s remove it. — 去掉 = remove/eliminate. 把...V掉 is the classic pattern: 把 it [verb]-away. 去 = go/away, 掉 = off/away result complement.

2. 医生说我得去做______。 🔊

► Pinyin
Yīshēng shuŏ wǒ děi qù zuò ______。
► Answer
B. MRI 检查 — 医生说我得去做MRI检查。
Yīshēng shuŏ wǒ děi qù zuò MRI jiǎnchá。
The doctor said I need to go get an MRI scan. — 得 (děi) here = need to/must. MRI检查 = MRI examination. 检查 = check/examine (also used for medical tests in general).

3. 货到了，我去______一下。 🔊

► Pinyin
Huò dào le， wǒ qù ______ yīxià。
► Answer
C. 收 — 货到了，我去收一下。
Huò dào le， wǒ qù shŏu yīxià。
The goods arrived — I’ll go receive them. — 收 (shŏu) = to receive/accept. Opposite of 送 (sòng) = to send/deliver. 收货 = receive goods, 送货 = deliver goods.

4. 请在这里填写你的______。 🔊

► Pinyin
Qǐng zài zhèlǐ tiánxiě nǐ de ______。
► Answer
B. 送货地址 — 请在这里填写你的送货地址。
Qǐng zài zhèlǐ tiánxiě nǐ de sòng huò dìzhǐ。
Please fill in your delivery address here. — 送货地址 = address for delivering goods (where items are sent TO). 收货地址 = address for receiving goods (same concept, slightly different framing). On app forms you often see both.

5. 我______这样的手术，知道不舒服。 🔊

► Pinyin
Wǒ ______ zhèyàng de shǒushù， zhīdào bù shūfu。
► Answer
B. 有过一次 — 我有过一次这样的手术，知道不舒服。
Wǒ yǒu guò yī cì zhèyàng de shǒushù， zhīdào bù shūfu。
I’ve had a surgery like this before — I know it’s uncomfortable. — 有过一次 = have had (it) once. 过 marks past experience. 一次 = one time/once. 手术 = surgery/operation.
Section 4: English → Chinese Sentence Translation

Read the English sentence. Write the Chinese translation on paper or in your head, then toggle the answer. Use 🔊 next to the Chinese answer to hear it.

1. Translate: "I use Ele.me to order food delivery."

► Answer
我用饿了么点外卖。 🔊
Wǒ yòng Èleme diǎn wàimài。
I use Ele.me to order food delivery.
饿了么 (Èleme) literally means “Are you hungry?” 点外卖 = order delivery. Pattern: 用[app]点外卖 = use [app] to order food.

2. Translate: "The doctor wants me to have an MRI scan to check the tumor."

► Answer
医生要我做 MRI 检查，看看肃瘦。 🔊
Yīshēng yào wǒ zuò MRI jiǎnchá， kànkan zhǒngliú。
The doctor wants me to get an MRI scan to check on the tumor.
肃瘦 (zhǒngliú) = tumor/growth. 看看 = take a look at (reduplication softens the verb). 检查 = examination/check.

3. Translate: "The goods have arrived. I need to go receive them."

► Answer
货到了，我得去收一下。 🔊
Huò dào le， wǒ děi qù shŏu yīxià。
The goods have arrived — I need to go receive them.
货 (huò) = goods/merchandise. 得 (děi) = need to/must. 收 (shŏu) = to receive. Note the V+一下 pattern (收一下) which softens the action: “just go receive it.”
