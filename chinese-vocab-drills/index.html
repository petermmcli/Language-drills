Section 1: Chinese → English

1. 搞笑 🔊

► Pinyin
gǎo xiào
► Answer
B. funny, hilarious — 搞笑 (gǎo xiào) means funny or hilarious. Don't confuse with 很搞笑 (very funny) — same meaning, just intensified with 很.

2. A和B差不多 🔊

► Pinyin
A hé B chàbuduō
► Answer
C. A and B are about the same — 差不多 (chàbuduō) means "about the same / more or less." Compare: A和B一样 means they are exactly the same.

3. 划船机 🔊

► Pinyin
huá chuán jī
► Answer
A. rowing machine — 划船机 (huá chuán jī): 划船 = to row a boat, 机 = machine. Compare 洗衣机 (xǐ yī jī) = washing machine.

4. 春天下小雨 🔊

► Pinyin
Chūntiān xià xiǎo yǔ
► Answer
B. It drizzles in spring — 春天下小雨: 春天 = spring, 下 = to fall (rain/snow), 小雨 = light rain/drizzle. Compare 下大雨 (heavy rain) and 下雪 (to snow).

5. 开车四十分钟就到 🔊

► Pinyin
Kāi chē sìshí fēnzhōng jiù dào
► Answer
C. It's only 40 minutes by car — 开车四十分钟就到: 开车 = to drive, 四十分钟 = 40 minutes, 就到 = just arrive / you're there. 就 softens the duration to convey "only / easily."
Section 2: English → Chinese

1. They must speak Greek.

► Pinyin
A. 洗衣机 — xǐ yī jī
B. 他们得说希腊语 — tāmen děi shuō Xīlà yǔ
C. 很搞笑 — hěn gǎo xiào
D. A和B一样 — A hé B yīyàng
► Answer
B. 他们得说希腊语 (tāmen děi shuō Xīlà yǔ) — 得 here means "must/have to" (not 得 as a structural particle). 希腊语 = Greek language.

2. Washing machine

► Pinyin
A. 划船机 — huá chuán jī
B. 很搞笑 — hěn gǎo xiào
C. 洗衣机 — xǐ yī jī
D. 他们得说希腊语 — tāmen děi shuō Xīlà yǔ
► Answer
C. 洗衣机 (xǐ yī jī) — 洗衣 = to wash clothes, 机 = machine. Compare with 划船机 = rowing machine (划船 = row a boat).

3. Strange, weird

► Pinyin
A. 搞笑 — gǎo xiào
B. 奇怪 — qí guài
C. 乱 — luàn
D. 很搞笑 — hěn gǎo xiào
► Answer
B. 奇怪 (qí guài) = strange or weird. 搞笑 = funny, 乱 = messy/chaotic. You might say 这很奇怪 = "This is very strange."

4. A and B are exactly the same

► Pinyin
A. A和B差不多 — A hé B chàbuduō
B. 他们得说希腊语 — tāmen děi shuō Xīlà yǔ
C. A和B一样 — A hé B yīyàng
D. 洗衣机 — xǐ yī jī
► Answer
C. A和B一样 (A hé B yīyàng) — 一样 = the same (identical). Contrast with 差不多 = about the same (approximately). 一样 is precise; 差不多 is approximate.

5. Very funny

► Pinyin
A. 奇怪 — qí guài
B. 很搞笑 — hěn gǎo xiào
C. 搞笑 — gǎo xiào
D. A和B一样 — A hé B yīyàng
► Answer
B. 很搞笑 (hěn gǎo xiào) — 很 intensifies: 搞笑 = funny, 很搞笑 = very funny. Note: 搞笑 alone (option C) is still correct for "funny" but doesn't include the intensifier 很.
Section 3: Fill in the Blank

1. 我的房间很______，到处是衣服和书。 🔊

► Pinyin
Wǒ de fángjiān hěn ______，dàochù shì yīfu hé shū。
► Answer
B. 乱 — 我的房间很乱，到处是衣服和书。
Wǒ de fángjiān hěn luàn，dàochù shì yīfu hé shū。
My room is very messy — there are clothes and books everywhere. — 乱 (luàn) describes disorder or chaos. 到处 = everywhere. 乱 can describe a messy room, chaotic situation, or confused thoughts.

2. 我家离地铁站很近，______。 🔊

► Pinyin
Wǒ jiā lí dìtiězhàn hěn jìn，______。
► Answer
A. 走路五分钟就到 — 我家离地铁站很近，走路五分钟就到。
Wǒ jiā lí dìtiězhàn hěn jìn，zǒulù wǔ fēnzhōng jiù dào。
My home is very close to the subway station — just a 5-minute walk. — 就 adds a "that's all it takes" sense: 走路五分钟就到 = walk 5 minutes and you're there. 离 marks distance from a point: 离地铁站很近 = close to the subway station.

3. 下雨天，他没有事情做，______。 🔊

► Pinyin
Xià yǔ tiān，tā méiyǒu shìqing zuò，______。
► Answer
C. 觉得无聊 — 下雨天，他没有事情做，觉得无聊。
Xià yǔ tiān，tā méiyǒu shìqing zuò，juéde wúliáo。
On a rainy day, he had nothing to do and felt bored. — 觉得 = to feel / to think. 无聊 = boring / bored. Together 觉得无聊 = feel bored. 有点儿伤心 would mean sad, which changes the emotional meaning.

4. 你看，______，我们快回家吧！ 🔊

► Pinyin
Nǐ kàn，______，wǒmen kuài huí jiā ba！
► Answer
A. 天阴了 — 你看，天阴了，我们快回家吧！
Nǐ kàn，tiān yīn le，wǒmen kuài huí jiā ba！
Look, it's getting cloudy — let's go home quickly! — 天阴了 uses 了 to signal a change of state (the sky has turned cloudy). Compare: 天气阴 (static description: the weather is cloudy) vs 天阴了 (change: it's just become cloudy).

5. 她在派对上，终于______。 🔊

► Pinyin
Tā zài pàiduì shàng，zhōngyú ______。
► Answer
B. 找到一个人 — 她在派对上，终于找到一个人。
Tā zài pàiduì shàng，zhōngyú zhǎodào yī gè rén。
At the party, she finally found someone. — 找到 is a result complement: 找 (search) + 到 (reached the result). 找 alone = to search; 找到 = to have found. 终于 = finally (after a wait or effort).
Section 4: English → Chinese Sentence Translation

Read the English sentence. Write the Chinese translation on paper or in your head, then toggle the answer. Use 🔊 next to the Chinese answer to hear it.

1. Translate: "There's a bug on the table."

► Answer
桌子上有一只虫子。 🔊
Zhuōzi shàng yǒu yī zhī chóngzi。
There's a bug on the table.
虫子 (chóngzi) = bug, insect, worm. The measure word is 只 (zhī), used for small animals and insects. 桌子上 = on the table (上 marks the surface).

2. Translate: "How did you get home?" (asking about a specific past trip)

► Answer
你是怎么回家的？ 🔊
Nǐ shì zěnme huí jiā de？
How did you get home?
是…的 emphasizes the manner/method of a past action. 你是怎么回家的？ = By what means did you get home (that time)? Contrast: 你怎么回家？ = How do you get home (generally)?

3. Translate: "How do you usually get home?" (general habit)

► Answer
你怎么回家？ 🔊
Nǐ zěnme huí jiā？
How do you get home?
Without 是…的, this is a general question about habit or method. 怎么 = how / by what means. Compare this pair: 你怎么回家？(habitual) vs 你是怎么回家的？(specific past instance).

4. Translate: "I'm feeling a little sad today."

► Answer
我今天有点儿伤心。 🔊
Wǒ jīntiān yǒudiǎnr shāngxīn。
I'm feeling a little sad today.
有点儿 softens an adjective and often implies the feeling is unwanted or unfortunate. 伤心 = sad / heartbroken. Note: 有点儿 comes before the adjective, never after.

5. Translate: "Hong Kong's spring is always very hot."

► Answer
香港的春天总是很热。 🔊
Xiānggǎng de chūntiān zǒngshì hěn rè。
Hong Kong's spring is always very hot.
总是 (zǒng shì) = always (habitual). Structure: [Place]的[season] 总是 很 [adjective]. The 的 links the possessive: Hong Kong's spring. 热 = hot.
