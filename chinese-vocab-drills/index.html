Section 3: Fill in the Blank

Choose the word that best completes the sentence. Use 🔊 to hear the full sentence with the correct answer filled in.

1. 明天______会下雨，你带伞吧。 🔊

► Pinyin
Míng tiān ______ huì xià yǔ， nǐ dài sǎn ba。
► Answer
B. 也许 — 明天也许会下雨，你带伞吧。
Míng tiān yě xǔ huì xià yǔ， nǐ dài sǎn ba。
Maybe it will rain tomorrow, so take an umbrella. — 也许 = perhaps; it goes before the verb.

2. 我们在北京见过一次，______就没见面了。 🔊

► Pinyin
Wǒ men zài Běi jīng jiàn guo yí cì， ______ jiù méi jiàn miàn le。
► Answer
B. 后来 — 我们在北京见过一次，后来就没见面了。
Wǒ men zài Běi jīng jiàn guo yí cì， hòu lái jiù méi jiàn miàn le。
We met once in Beijing, and afterwards we never met again. — 后来 = later / afterwards, about past events only.

3. 因为下雨，比赛______了。 🔊

► Pinyin
Yīn wèi xià yǔ， bǐ sài ______ le。
► Answer
A. 取消 — 因为下雨，比赛取消了。
Yīn wèi xià yǔ， bǐ sài qǔ xiāo le。
Because of the rain, the match was cancelled. — 取消 = to cancel (a plan, flight, or class).

4. 我每天早上起床以后先______，然后洗脸。 🔊

► Pinyin
Wǒ měi tiān zǎo shang qǐ chuáng yǐ hòu xiān ______， rán hòu xǐ liǎn。
► Answer
C. 刷牙 — 我每天早上起床以后先刷牙，然后洗脸。
Wǒ měi tiān zǎo shang qǐ chuáng yǐ hòu xiān shuā yá， rán hòu xǐ liǎn。
Every morning after getting up, I first brush my teeth and then wash my face. — 刷牙 = to brush teeth (verb + object).

5. 我刷了牙，也______了，现在可以出门了。 🔊

► Pinyin
Wǒ shuā le yá， yě ______ le， xiàn zài kě yǐ chū mén le。
► Answer
A. 洗脸 — 我刷了牙，也洗脸了，现在可以出门了。
Wǒ shuā le yá， yě xǐ liǎn le， xiàn zài kě yǐ chū mén le。
I brushed my teeth and washed my face; now I can go out. — 洗脸 = to wash one's face (verb + object).
Section 4: English → Chinese Sentence Translation

Read the English sentence. Write the Chinese translation on paper or in your head, then toggle the answer. Use 🔊 next to the Chinese answer to hear it.

1. Translate: "I often ride shared bikes."

► Answer
我经常骑共享自行车。 🔊
Wǒ jīng cháng qí gòng xiǎng zì xíng chē。
I often ride shared bikes.
共享 comes before the noun it describes: 共享自行车 = shared bike.

2. Translate: "This way is very convenient."

► Answer
这种方式很方便。 🔊
Zhè zhǒng fāng shì hěn fāng biàn。
This way is very convenient.
种 is the measure word for kinds/types: 这种方式 = this kind of way.

3. Translate: "How is the itinerary for this trip?"

► Answer
这次的行程怎么样？ 🔊
Zhè cì de xíng chéng zěn me yàng？
How is the itinerary for this trip?
次 counts occasions; 这次的 + noun = "this time's ...".

4. Translate: "I need to buy some cold medicine."

► Answer
我需要买一些感冒药。 🔊
Wǒ xū yào mǎi yì xiē gǎn mào yào。
I need to buy some cold medicine.
一些 = some; 需要 = need, followed directly by a verb phrase.

5. Translate: "We made small talk for a while."

► Answer
我们寒暄了一会儿。 🔊
Wǒ men hán xuān le yí huì er。
We made small talk for a while.
了 marks the completed action; 一会儿 shows a short duration.
