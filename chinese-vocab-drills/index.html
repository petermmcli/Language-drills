1. MRI检查 🔊

► Pinyin
MRI jiǎn chá
► Answer
B. MRI scan / examination — MRI检查 (MRI jiǎn chá) literally means 'MRI examination'. 检查 alone means 'to check' or 'examination/test'.

2. 一点 🔊

► Pinyin
yī diǎn
► Answer
A. a little / a bit — 一点 (yī diǎn) means 'a little'. Often used in 一点儿 in the north. Don't confuse with 点 alone, which can also mean 'o'clock' or 'to order'.

3. 还 🔊

► Pinyin
hái
► Answer
D. still / also / yet — 还 (hái) = 'still' or 'also'. Pronounced huán when it means 'to return (something)'. Context determines which meaning applies.

4. 下载 🔊

► Pinyin
xià zǎi
► Answer
C. to download — 下载 (xià zǎi) = 'to download'. Literally 'load downward'. The opposite is 上传 (shàng chuán) = 'to upload'.

5. 地址 🔊

► Pinyin
dì zhǐ
► Answer
B. address — 地址 (dì zhǐ) = 'address'. 收货地址 = shipping/delivery address; 家庭地址 = home address. 地 means 'ground/place' and 址 means 'location/site'.
Section 2: English → Chinese

1. to have chosen (well) / to have selected

► Pinyin
A. 要看 — yào kàn
B. 一点 — yī diǎn
C. 选好 — xuǎn hǎo
D. 下载 — xià zǎi
► Answer
C. 选好 (xuǎn hǎo) — 'to have chosen / selected well'. The 好 here acts as a result complement, indicating the action is complete and satisfactory. 你选好了吗？= Have you made your choice?

2. want to watch / need to see

► Pinyin
A. 要看 — yào kàn
B. 选好 — xuǎn hǎo
C. 快回来 — kuài huí lái
D. 还 — hái
► Answer
A. 要看 (yào kàn) — 'want to watch' or 'need to look at'. 我要看医生 = I need to see a doctor. 要 carries both 'want' and 'need to' depending on context.

3. Spring Festival / Chinese New Year

► Pinyin
A. 肿瘤 — zhǒng liú
B. 地址 — dì zhǐ
C. 过国庆节 — guò Guó qìng Jié
D. 春节 — Chūn Jié
► Answer
D. 春节 (Chūn Jié) — the Spring Festival, i.e. Chinese New Year. 过春节 = to celebrate Chinese New Year. Don't confuse with 国庆节 (Guó qìng Jié) = National Day (Oct 1).

4. goods / cargo / merchandise

► Pinyin
A. 外卖员 — wài mài yuán
B. 货 — huò
C. 外卖骑手 — wài mài qí shǒu
D. 收货地址 — shōu huò dì zhǐ
► Answer
B. 货 (huò) — 'goods, cargo, merchandise'. Appears in 收货 (receive goods), 货车 (truck/freight car), 百货 (general merchandise). Single character, but very common in compound words.

5. food delivery person (employee title)

► Pinyin
A. 收货地址 — shōu huò dì zhǐ
B. 货 — huò
C. 外卖员 — wài mài yuán
D. 外卖骑手 — wài mài qí shǒu
► Answer
C. 外卖员 (wài mài yuán) — a food delivery employee (company/platform context). Compare: 外卖骑手 = delivery rider (emphasizes the bike/scooter aspect). 员 is the suffix for 'person in a role' (员工=employee, 服务员=waiter).
Section 3: Fill in the Blank

1. 我不想去，但是______。 🔊

► Pinyin
Wǒ bù xiǎng qù，dàn shì ______。
► Answer
C. 没有别的选择 — 我不想去，但是没有别的选择。
Wǒ bù xiǎng qù，dàn shì méi yǒu bié de xuǎn zé。
I don't want to go, but there's no other choice. — 没有别的选择 = 'have no other choice'. 别的 = 'other'. Often follows 但是 (but) to explain why one must do something undesirable.

2. 国庆节要上班，明天可以______。 🔊

► Pinyin
Guó qìng Jié yào shàng bān，míng tiān kě yǐ ______。
► Answer
B. 调休 — 国庆节要上班，明天可以调休。
Guó qìng Jié yào shàng bān，míng tiān kě yǐ tiáo xiū。
I have to work on National Day, so I can take a compensatory day off tomorrow. — 调休 (tiáo xiū) refers to the Chinese system of shifting rest days: work on a holiday, get a weekday off instead. Very common around major holidays like 国庆节 and 春节.

3. 请填写你的______。 🔊

► Pinyin
Qǐng tián xiě nǐ de ______。
► Answer
B. 收货地址 — 请填写你的收货地址。
Qǐng tián xiě nǐ de shōu huò dì zhǐ。
Please fill in your shipping/delivery address. — 收货地址 is the full compound: 收 (receive) + 货 (goods) + 地址 (address). Option C 地址 alone is too generic; online shopping specifically asks for 收货地址.

4. 这种情况我______。 🔊

► Pinyin
Zhè zhǒng qíng kuàng wǒ ______。
► Answer
A. 有过一次 — 这种情况我有过一次。
Zhè zhǒng qíng kuàng wǒ yǒu guò yī cì。
I've experienced this situation once before. — 有过一次 uses the experiential 过, meaning 'have had / experienced once'. 这种情况 = 'this kind of situation'. 一次 = 'one time'.

5. 你们打算怎么______？ 🔊

► Pinyin
Nǐ men dǎ suàn zěn me ______？
► Answer
C. 过国庆节 — 你们打算怎么过国庆节？
Nǐ men dǎ suàn zěn me guò Guó qìng Jié？
How are you planning to spend National Day? — 过 here means 'to spend / celebrate (a holiday)'. 打算 = 'to plan'. 怎么过节 is a common question pattern for any holiday.
Section 4: English → Chinese Sentence Translation

Read the English sentence. Write the Chinese translation on paper or in your head, then toggle the answer. Use 🔊 next to the Chinese answer to hear it.

1. Translate: "I have to go to the hospital today."

► Answer
今天我得去医院。 🔊
Jīn tiān wǒ děi qù yī yuàn。
Today I have to go to the hospital.
得 (děi) means 'have to / must'. It is different from 得 (de) the structural particle and 得 (dé) meaning 'to obtain'. When meaning 'must', it is always děi and is usually placed between the subject and the main verb.

2. Translate: "When are you going back to the United States?"

► Answer
你什么时候回美国？ 🔊
Nǐ shén me shí hòu huí Měi guó？
When are you going back to the United States?
回美国 = 'return to America'. 什么时候 = 'when' (asking about time). The time phrase 什么时候 comes before the verb in Chinese, unlike English where 'when' can come at the start or end.

3. Translate: "There are more and more food delivery riders in China."

► Answer
中国的外卖骑手越来越多。 🔊
Zhōng guó de wài mài qí shǒu yuè lái yuè duō。
Food delivery riders in China are getting more and more numerous.
外卖骑手 = delivery rider (emphasizes riding a scooter). 越来越多 = 'more and more' — a very useful pattern: 越来越 + adjective.

4. Translate: "It's getting late — hurry back!"

► Answer
时间不早了，快回来！ 🔊
Shí jiān bù zǎo le，kuài huí lái！
It's not early anymore — come back quickly!
快 before a verb = 'hurry and do it / do it quickly'. 时间不早了 literally means 'the time is no longer early' — a polite Chinese way of saying it's getting late. 了 here marks a change of state.

5. Translate: "The doctor said this tumor needs to be removed."

► Answer
医生说这个肿瘤需要去掉。 🔊
Yī shēng shuō zhè ge zhǒng liú xū yào qù diào。
The doctor said this tumor needs to be removed.
肿瘤 (zhǒng liú) = tumor. 去掉 = 'to remove / get rid of' (a previously-learned word). 需要 = 'to need to'. The sentence structure: subject + 说 + reported speech is common in Chinese indirect speech.
