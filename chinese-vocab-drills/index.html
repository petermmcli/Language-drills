1. 缩写 🔊

► Pinyin
suō xiě
► Answer
B. abbreviation — 缩写 (suō xiě) means abbreviation or to abbreviate. 缩 = to shrink, 写 = to write.

2. 照片 🔊

► Pinyin
zhào piàn
► Answer
C. photo — 照片 (zhào piàn) = photograph. Also: 拍照 (pāi zhào) = to take a photo.

3. 会议 🔊

► Pinyin
huì yì
► Answer
A. meeting / conference — 会议 (huì yì). Common: 会议室 = conference room.

4. 负面 🔊

► Pinyin
fù miàn
► Answer
C. negative (side/aspect) — 负面 (fù miàn). Opposite: 正面 (zhèngmiàn) = positive side.

5. 可惜 🔊

► Pinyin
kě xī
► Answer
B. what a pity — 可惜 (kě xī) expresses regret. E.g., 可惜你没来 = it's a pity you didn't come.

D. 听起来像 — tīng qǐ lái xiàng

3. sounds like

Section 3: Fill in the Blank

Choose the word that best completes the sentence. Use 🔊 to hear the full sentence with the correct answer filled in.

1. 你的______是多少？ 🔊

► Pinyin
Nǐ de ______ shì duōshao？
► Answer
B. 车牌 — 你的车牌是多少？
Nǐ de chēpái shì duōshao？
What is your license plate number? — 车牌 = license plate. 是多少？= what is the number? Also: 车牌号 (chēpái hào).

2. 开会时间是下午两点______。 🔊

► Pinyin
Kāihuì shíjiān shì xiàwǔ liǎng diǎn ______。
► Answer
B. 一刻 — 开会时间是下午两点一刻。
Kāihuì shíjiān shì xiàwǔ liǎng diǎn yīkè。
The meeting time is 2:15 pm. — 一刻 = a quarter hour (15 min). 两点一刻 = 2:15. Also: 两点半 = 2:30, 两点三刻 = 2:45.

3. 那条鱼刚买回来就______了。 🔊

► Pinyin
Nà tiáo yú gāng mǎi huílái jiù ______ le。
► Answer
C. 死 — 那条鱼刚买回来就死了。
Nà tiáo yú gāng mǎi huílái jiù sǐ le。
The fish died right after we bought it. — 死 (sǐ) = to die. 刚...就... = just ... and immediately ... 条 is the measure word for fish.

4. 他喜欢玩______滑雪，不喜欢双板。 🔊

► Pinyin
Tā xǐhuān wán ______ huáxuě，bù xǐhuān shuāngbǎn。
► Answer
B. 单板 — 他喜欢玩单板滑雪，不喜欢双板。
Tā xǐhuān wán dānbǎn huáxuě，bù xǐhuān shuāngbǎn。
He likes snowboarding, not alpine skiing. — 单板 = snowboard (one board); 双板 = alpine skis (two boards). 单板滑雪 = snowboarding.

5. 我们可以喝咖啡或者茶，______。 🔊

► Pinyin
Wǒmen kěyǐ hē kāfēi huòzhě chá，______。
► Answer
B. 给你选 — 我们可以喝咖啡或者茶，给你选。
Wǒmen kěyǐ hē kāfēi huòzhě chá，gěi nǐ xuǎn。
We can have coffee or tea — I'll let you choose. — 给你选 = I'll let you choose / it's your pick. Natural at the end of a sentence offering options.
Section 4: English → Chinese Sentence Translation

Read the English sentence. Write the Chinese translation on paper or in your head, then toggle the answer. Use 🔊 next to the Chinese answer to hear it.

1. Translate: "What's the situation here?"

► Answer
这里的情况怎么样？ 🔊
Zhèlǐ de qíngkuàng zěnmeyàng？
What's the situation here?
情况 (qíngkuàng) = situation/circumstances. 怎么样？ = how is it / what's it like?

2. Translate: "This road is about three miles long."

► Answer
这条路大概三英里长。 🔊
Zhè tiáo lù dàgài sān yīnglǐ cháng。
This road is about three miles long.
英里 = mile (loanword). 公里 = kilometer. 大概 = approximately/about.

3. Translate: "There are cameras everywhere in the store."

► Answer
商店里到处都有摄像头。 🔊
Shāngdiàn lǐ dàochù dōu yǒu shèxiàngtóu。
There are cameras everywhere in the store.
摄像头 = video camera (security/webcam). 到处都有 = there is/are everywhere.

4. Translate: "Does this hotel have a swimming pool?"

► Answer
这家酒店有游泳池吗？ 🔊
Zhè jiā jiǔdiàn yǒu yóuyǒngchí ma？
Does this hotel have a swimming pool?
游泳池 = swimming pool. 游泳 (yóuyǒng) = to swim. 酒店 = hotel.

5. Translate: "Can you teach me how to use this app?"

► Answer
你能教我怎么用这个应用吗？ 🔊
Nǐ néng jiāo wǒ zěnme yòng zhège yìngyòng ma？
Can you teach me how to use this app?
教 (jiāo, 1st tone) = to teach. Don't confuse with 教 (jiào, 4th tone) in 教室 classroom. 能 = can/able to.
