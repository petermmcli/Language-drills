1. 要多长时间 🔊

► Pinyin
yào duō cháng shí jiān
► Answer
C. how long does it take? — 要多长时间 (yào duō cháng shí jiān) is a fixed question phrase meaning "how long will it take?" Used when asking about the duration of an activity or journey.

2. 烤鸭店 🔊

► Pinyin
kǎo yā diàn
► Answer
A. roast duck restaurant — 烤鸭店 (kǎo yā diàn): 烤 = roast, 鸭 = duck, 店 = shop/restaurant. Don't confuse with 烤鸭 (kǎo yā) = roast duck (the food itself).

3. 红酒 🔊

► Pinyin
hóng jiǔ
► Answer
C. red wine — 红酒 (hóng jiǔ): 红 = red, 酒 = alcohol. Compare: 白酒 (bái jiǔ) = baijiu/white spirits, 鸡尾酒 (jī wěi jiǔ) = cocktail.

4. 怎么用 🔊

► Pinyin
zěn me yòng
► Answer
B. how to use it — 怎么用 (zěn me yòng): 怎么 = how, 用 = use. "这个怎么用？" = How do you use this? A very practical question when handling unfamiliar objects or apps.

5. 无人驾驶车 🔊

► Pinyin
wú rén jià shǐ chē
► Answer
B. self-driving car — 无人驾驶车 (wú rén jià shǐ chē): 无人 = unmanned/no person, 驾驶 = to drive, 车 = car. You may also hear 自动驾驶车 for "autonomous vehicle".

1. Silk Street (famous Beijing market)

► Pinyin
A. 烤鸭店 — kǎo yā diàn
B. 特色 — tè sè
C. 秀水街 — Xiù shuǐ jiē
D. 拒绝 — jù jué
► Answer
C. 秀水街 (Xiù shuǐ jiē) — Beijing's famous Silk Street market known for silk goods, clothing and souvenirs. 秀 = elegant, 水 = water, 街 = street. It's a proper noun so 秀水街 is the full name, not translated.

2. to refuse / to reject

► Pinyin
A. 出差 — chū chāi
B. 行走 — xíng zǒu
C. 别担心 — bié dān xīn
D. 拒绝 — jù jué
► Answer
D. 拒绝 (jù jué) — to refuse, reject, or decline. "我拒绝了他的邀请" = I declined his invitation. Compare with 同意 (tóng yì) = to agree, which is the opposite.

3. cocktail

► Pinyin
A. 鸡尾酒 — jī wěi jiǔ
B. 白酒 — bái jiǔ
C. 红酒 — hóng jiǔ
D. 肝 — gān
► Answer
A. 鸡尾酒 (jī wěi jiǔ) — cocktail. Literally "chicken-tail alcohol" — 鸡尾 = rooster's tail (referring to the colorful appearance). Compare the three 酒: 红酒 = red wine, 白酒 = baijiu, 鸡尾酒 = cocktail.

4. baijiu / Chinese white spirits

► Pinyin
A. 怎么用 — zěn me yòng
B. 白酒 — bái jiǔ
C. 鸡尾酒 — jī wěi jiǔ
D. 红酒 — hóng jiǔ
► Answer
B. 白酒 (bái jiǔ) — baijiu, Chinese white spirits. Literally "white alcohol" — a strong distilled liquor, often 40–60% ABV. Very common at Chinese banquets. Don't confuse with 啤酒 (pí jiǔ) = beer.

5. one quarter / one fourth

► Pinyin
A. 行吗 — xíng ma
B. 四分之一 — sì fēn zhī yī
C. 很久 — hěn jiǔ
D. 要多长时间 — yào duō cháng shí jiān
► Answer
B. 四分之一 (sì fēn zhī yī) — one quarter. Chinese fractions follow the pattern: denominator + 分之 + numerator. So 三分之二 = two thirds, 五分之三 = three fifths.

1. 医生说我的______很好，不用担心。 🔊

► Pinyin
Yī shēng shuō wǒ de ______ hěn hǎo，bù yòng dān xīn。
► Answer
A. 肝 — 医生说我的肝很好，不用担心。
Yī shēng shuō wǒ de gān hěn hǎo，bù yòng dān xīn。
The doctor said my liver is fine, no need to worry. — 肝 (gān) = liver. Compare body organs: 肺 (fèi) = lungs, 肾 (shèn) = kidneys. 不用 = no need to, followed by a verb.

2. 她在上海______，发到了微信朋友圈。 🔊

► Pinyin
Tā zài Shàng hǎi ______，fā dào le wēi xìn péng yǒu quān。
► Answer
B. 拍视频 — 她在上海拍视频，发到了微信朋友圈。
Tā zài Shàng hǎi pāi shì pín，fā dào le wēi xìn péng yǒu quān。
She filmed a video in Shanghai and posted it to her WeChat Moments. — 拍视频 = film/take a video. Compare: 拍照 = take a photo. 发到 = post/send to. 朋友圈 = WeChat Moments (literally 'friends circle').

3. ______，这件事我来帮你解决。 🔊

► Pinyin
______，zhè jiàn shì wǒ lái bāng nǐ jiě jué。
► Answer
A. 别担心 — 别担心，这件事我来帮你解决。
Bié dān xīn，zhè jiàn shì wǒ lái bāng nǐ jiě jué。
Don't worry, I'll help you solve this. — 别担心 = don't worry. 别 + verb = don't do [verb]. 件 is the measure word for events/matters (事情). 来 here means 'will' / 'I'll take care of it'.

4. 在前面那个路口______，银行就在右边。 🔊

► Pinyin
Zài qián miàn nà ge lù kǒu ______，yín háng jiù zài yòu biān。
► Answer
C. 往左转 — 在前面那个路口往左转，银行就在右边。
Zài qián miàn nà ge lù kǒu wǎng zuǒ zhuǎn，yín háng jiù zài yòu biān。
At the next intersection, turn left — the bank is on the right. — 往 + direction + 转 = turn [direction]. 往左转 = turn left, 往右转 = turn right. 路口 = intersection. 就在 = is right at / is right on.

5. 我明天下午三点来找你，______？ 🔊

► Pinyin
Wǒ míng tiān xià wǔ sān diǎn lái zhǎo nǐ，______？
► Answer
B. 行吗 — 我明天下午三点来找你，行吗？
Wǒ míng tiān xià wǔ sān diǎn lái zhǎo nǐ，xíng ma？
I'll come find you tomorrow at 3pm — is that okay? — 行吗 = is that okay? / will that work? 行 (xíng) by itself means 'okay' or 'that works'. Compare: 对吧 = right? (seeking confirmation), 好的 = okay (agreement, not a question).
Section 4: English → Chinese Sentence Translation

Read the English sentence. Write the Chinese translation on paper or in your head, then toggle the answer. Use 🔊 next to the Chinese answer to hear it.

1. Translate: "We haven't seen each other for a long time!"

► Answer
我们很久没见面了！ 🔊
Wǒ men hěn jiǔ méi jiàn miàn le！
We haven't seen each other for a long time!
很久 = for a long time. 很久没…了 is a set pattern meaning 'haven't … for a long time'. Also useful: 好久不见 = long time no see (set greeting).

2. Translate: "He likes to walk through the old streets of the city."

► Answer
他喜欢在城市的老街上行走。 🔊
Tā xǐ huān zài chéng shì de lǎo jiē shàng xíng zǒu。
He likes to walk through the old streets of the city.
行走 (xíng zǒu) is more formal/literary than 走路 (zǒu lù = to walk). Both mean 'to walk', but 行走 appears more in writing or when describing extended movement through a place.

3. Translate: "Roast duck is Beijing's signature dish."

► Answer
烤鸭是北京的特色菜。 🔊
Kǎo yā shì Běi jīng de tè sè cài。
Roast duck is Beijing's signature dish.
特色 (tè sè) = special feature, hallmark characteristic. 特色菜 = signature dish / specialty dish. 特 = special, 色 = color/character. Also used for 特色小镇 (characteristic town), 特色经济 (distinctive economy).

4. Translate: "It's getting late, let's go!"

► Answer
已经很晚了，我们走吧！ 🔊
Yǐ jīng hěn wǎn le，wǒ men zǒu ba！
It's getting late, let's go!
我们走吧 = let's go. 吧 at the end softens the suggestion/proposal. 已经 = already. 很晚了 = very late (了 signals a changed state — it's become late).

5. Translate: "My boss is going on a business trip to Beijing next week."

► Answer
我的老板下个星期要去北京出差。 🔊
Wǒ de lǎo bǎn xià ge xīng qī yào qù Běi jīng chū chāi。
My boss is going on a business trip to Beijing next week.
出差 (chū chāi) = to go on a business trip. 出 = go out, 差 (chāi) = errand/assignment. Don't confuse 差 here (chāi, 1st tone) with 差 (chà, 4th tone) meaning 'bad/poor' or 差 (chā, 1st tone) meaning 'difference'.
