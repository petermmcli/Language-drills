Section 1: Chinese → English

1. 发错了 🔊

► Pinyin
fā cuò le
► Answer
C. sent to the wrong place — 发错了 (fā cuò le): 发 = to send/deliver; 错 = wrong/mistake; 了 = completion marker. Common when a delivery ends up at the wrong address.

2. 放在一楼 🔊

► Pinyin
fàng zài yī lóu
► Answer
A. leave it on the first floor — 放在一楼 (fàng zài yī lóu): 放 = put/place; 在 = at; 一楼 = first floor. A common instruction when receiving a delivery.

3. 坐电梯上18楼 🔊

► Pinyin
zuò diàntī shàng 18 lóu
► Answer
B. take the elevator up to floor 18 — 坐电梯上18楼 (zuò diàntī shàng 18 lóu): 坐电梯 = take the elevator; 上X楼 = go up to floor X. Direction given to a delivery person.

4. 把地址告诉她 🔊

► Pinyin
bǎ dìzhǐ gàosù tā
► Answer
D. tell her the address — 把地址告诉她 (bǎ dìzhǐ gàosù tā): 把 marks 地址 (address) as the object before the verb 告诉 (tell). Pattern: 把 [object] 告诉 [person].

5. 午觉 🔊

► Pinyin
wǔjiào
► Answer
A. afternoon nap — 午觉 (wǔjiào): 午 = midday/noon; 觉 = sleep/nap. The noun form. Compare 睡午觉 = "to take a nap" (verb phrase).
Section 2: English → Chinese

1. exit the elevator and turn left

► Pinyin
A. 坐电梯上18楼 — zuò diàntī shàng 18 lóu
B. 进来 — jìnlái
C. 出电梯左转 — chū diàntī zuǒ zhuǎn
D. 楼下 — lóuxià
► Answer
C. 出电梯左转 (chū diàntī zuǒ zhuǎn) — exit the elevator and turn left. 出 = exit; 电梯 = elevator; 左转 = turn left. Compare 右转 = turn right.

2. come in

► Pinyin
A. 进来 — jìnlái
B. 出电梯左转 — chū diàntī zuǒ zhuǎn
C. 楼下 — lóuxià
D. 五星 — wǔ xīng
► Answer
A. 进来 (jìnlái) — come in. 进 = enter; 来 = come (toward speaker). Opposite: 出去 = go out. Used to invite someone inside.

3. downstairs

► Pinyin
A. 进来 — jìnlái
B. 五星 — wǔ xīng
C. 午觉 — wǔjiào
D. 楼下 — lóuxià
► Answer
D. 楼下 (lóuxià) — downstairs / floor below. 楼 = building/floor; 下 = below. Opposite: 楼上 = upstairs. Don't confuse 五星 (five stars) with 午觉 (nap).

4. is there anything else I can help you with?

► Pinyin
A. 说一遍 — shuō yī biàn
B. 还有什么可以帮您的吗 — hái yǒu shénme kěyǐ bāng nín de ma
C. 什么时候能到 — shénme shíhou néng dào
D. 写好评 — xiě hǎo píng
► Answer
B. 还有什么可以帮您的吗 (hái yǒu shénme kěyǐ bāng nín de ma) — a polite service-industry phrase. 帮您 uses the formal 您 instead of 你. 还有 = still/also there is.

5. five stars

► Pinyin
A. 午觉 — wǔjiào
B. 楼下 — lóuxià
C. 五星 — wǔ xīng
D. 写好评 — xiě hǎo píng
► Answer
C. 五星 (wǔ xīng) — five stars. 五 = five; 星 = star. The full phrase is 五星好评 (five-star review). Note 写好评 = "write a good review" — that's the action, not the rating itself.
Section 3: Fill in the Blank

1. 如果你满意，请______。 🔊

► Pinyin
Rúguǒ nǐ mǎnyì，qǐng ______。
► Answer
B. 写好评 — 如果你满意，请写好评。
Rúguǒ nǐ mǎnyì，qǐng xiě hǎo píng。
If you're satisfied, please write a good review. — 好评 = good review; 差评 = bad review. 写好评 is a standard phrase in Chinese e-commerce and delivery apps.

2. 我没听清楚，你能再______吗？ 🔊

► Pinyin
Wǒ méi tīng qīngchu，nǐ néng zài ______ ma？
► Answer
C. 说一遍 — 我没听清楚，你能再说一遍吗？
Wǒ méi tīng qīngchu，nǐ néng zài shuō yī biàn ma？
I didn't hear clearly. Can you say it once more? — 遍 counts complete passes of an action from start to finish. 再说一遍 = say it one more time. Compare 次 (times/occasions) vs 遍 (full repetitions).

3. 这件衣服______，有小一号的吗？ 🔊

► Pinyin
Zhè jiàn yīfu ______，yǒu xiǎo yī hào de ma？
► Answer
B. 有点儿大 — 这件衣服有点儿大，有小一号的吗？
Zhè jiàn yīfu yǒu diǎnr dà，yǒu xiǎo yī hào de ma？
This piece of clothing is a bit big. Do you have one size smaller? — 有点儿 softens the adjective — a polite way to say something is a bit too big. 小一号 = one size smaller; 大一号 = one size bigger.

4. ______，这样不安全。 🔊

► Pinyin
______，zhèyàng bù ānquán。
► Answer
C. 骑车的时候别听音乐 — 骑车的时候别听音乐，这样不安全。
Qí chē de shíhou bié tīng yīnyuè，zhèyàng bù ānquán。
Don't listen to music when cycling — it's not safe. — …的时候 = when/while doing something. 别 = don't (imperative). 这样 = like this / in this way, referring back to what was just said.

5. 我在楼下等你，______？ 🔊

► Pinyin
Wǒ zài lóuxià děng nǐ，______？
► Answer
B. 什么时候能到 — 我在楼下等你，什么时候能到？
Wǒ zài lóuxià děng nǐ，shénme shíhou néng dào？
I'm waiting for you downstairs — when can you get here? — 能到 = can arrive/get there. 什么时候能到 is softer than 你几点到 (what time do you arrive), implying some uncertainty.
Section 4: English → Chinese Sentence Translation

Read the English sentence. Write the Chinese translation on paper or in your head, then toggle the answer. Use 🔊 next to the Chinese answer to hear it.

1. Translate: "After lunch, he likes to take a nap."

► Answer
午饭后，他喜欢睡午觉。 🔊
Wǔfàn hòu，tā xǐhuān shuì wǔjiào。
After lunch, he likes to take an afternoon nap.
睡午觉 is the verb phrase (to nap); 午觉 alone is the noun (the nap). 午饭后 = after lunch; 后 without 以 is common in spoken Chinese.

2. Translate: "Don't worry — home delivery is free."

► Answer
别担心，送货上门是免费的。 🔊
Bié dānxīn，sòng huò shàng mén shì miǎnfèi de。
Don't worry — home delivery is free.
送货上门 = deliver goods to your door (上门 = go to the door). 免费的 = free of charge. The 的 at the end is optional but common in speech.

3. Translate: "Are you going to buy anything on Double 11?"

► Answer
双十一你要买什么吗？ 🔊
Shuāng shíyī nǐ yào mǎi shénme ma？
Are you going to buy anything on Double 11?
双十一 (11/11) is China's biggest online shopping day, originally started by Alibaba. 双 means double; the date 11/11 has four ones. Also written as 双11.

4. Translate: "On weekends, you can sleep whenever you want."

► Answer
周末，什么时候想睡觉都可以。 🔊
Zhōumò，shénme shíhou xiǎng shuìjiào dōu kěyǐ。
On weekends, you can sleep whenever you want.
什么时候…都可以 = no matter when…it's fine. 都 reinforces the freedom/universality. This pattern works with any time/question word: 在哪儿都可以, 吃什么都可以.

5. Translate: "Do you have this type of flower?"

► Answer
你有这种花吗？ 🔊
Nǐ yǒu zhè zhǒng huā ma？
Do you have this type of flower?
种 (zhǒng) is a measure word for types, kinds, or species. 这种 = this kind/type. Contrast: 一朵花 uses 朵 (a round/fluffy thing) to count individual flowers; 种 counts varieties.
