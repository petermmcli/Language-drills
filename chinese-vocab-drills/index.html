1. 外卖 🔊

► Pinyin
wài mài
► Answer
B. takeout / food delivery — 外卖 (wài mài) means food delivery or takeout. The person who delivers it is the 外卖员 or 外卖骑手.

2. 检查 🔊

► Pinyin
jiǎn chá
► Answer
A. examination / medical check — 检查 (jiǎn chá) is used for medical exams, inspections, or checks. MRI 检查 = MRI scan.

3. 地址 🔊

► Pinyin
dì zhǐ
► Answer
C. address — 地址 (dì zhǐ) is used for both physical and online delivery addresses. 收货地址 = delivery address.

4. 骑手 🔊

► Pinyin
qí shǒu
► Answer
D. rider / delivery driver — 骑手 (qí shǒu) literally means someone who rides (a bike or scooter). 外卖骑手 is a food delivery rider. Don't confuse with 外卖员 (delivery worker, emphasizes the job role).

5. 老家 🔊

► Pinyin
lǎo jiā
► Answer
A. hometown / place of origin — 老家 (lǎo jiā) is one's hometown or original home. 回老家 = to go back to your hometown, especially common around Chinese holidays.
Section 2: English → Chinese

Read the English. Choose the correct Hanzi. Use 🔊 on each option to hear it. Toggle ► Pinyin to reveal readings.

1. Mid-Autumn Festival

► Pinyin
A. 国庆节 — Guóqìngjié
B. 中秋节 — Zhōngqiūjié
C. 外卖员 — wàimàiyuán
D. 老家 — lǎojiā
► Answer
B. 中秋节 (Zhōngqiūjié) — celebrated on the 15th day of the 8th lunar month. 国庆节 is National Day (Oct. 1). Both are major public holidays.

2. National Day (Oct. 1)

► Pinyin
A. 中秋节 — Zhōngqiūjié
B. 外卖 — wàimài
C. 调休 — diàoxiū
D. 国庆节 — Guóqìngjié
► Answer
D. 国庆节 (Guóqìngjié) — October 1, marking the founding of the PRC. The holiday week is called 黄金周 (golden week). Often involves 调休 (compensatory days off).

3. to confirm / certain

► Pinyin
A. 下载 — xiàzǎi
B. 骑手 — qíshǒu
C. 确定 — quèdìng
D. 地址 — dìzhǐ
► Answer
C. 确定 (quèdìng) — to confirm or be certain. Used in apps: click 确定 to confirm an order. Don't confuse with 确认 (quèrèn = to verify/acknowledge).

4. to download

► Pinyin
A. 收货 — shōuhuò
B. 下载 — xiàzǎi
C. 确定 — quèdìng
D. 检查 — jiǎnchá
► Answer
B. 下载 (xiàzǎi) — to download. When you've finished downloading: 下载好了. The reverse (upload) is 上传 (shàngchuán).

5. food delivery worker (restaurant/platform staff)

► Pinyin
A. 外卖 — wàimài
B. 骑手 — qíshǒu
C. 外卖员 — wàimàiyuán
D. 检查 — jiǎnchá
► Answer
C. 外卖员 (wàimàiyuán) — a food delivery worker (emphasizes the job role). 外卖骑手 emphasizes that they ride a scooter/bike. Both are used interchangeably in everyday speech.
Section 3: Fill in the Blank

Choose the word that best completes the sentence. Use 🔊 to hear the full sentence with the correct answer filled in.

1. 你的外卖到了，你可以去______了。 🔊

► Pinyin
Nǐ de wàimài dào le，nǐ kěyǐ qù ______ le。
► Answer
B. 收货 — 你的外卖到了，你可以去收货了。
Nǐ de wàimài dào le，nǐ kěyǐ qù shōuhuò le。
Your delivery has arrived, you can go receive it now. — 收货 (shōu huò) = receive goods/delivery. 收 = receive, 货 = goods. The opposite is 送货 (deliver goods). Context clue: 外卖到了 tells you something arrived, so you receive it.

2. 你______了吗？我们马上下单。 🔊

► Pinyin
Nǐ ______ le ma？Wǒmen mǎshàng xiàdān。
► Answer
C. 选好 — 你选好了吗？我们马上下单。
Nǐ xuǎnhǎo le ma？Wǒmen mǎshàng xiàdān。
Have you chosen (what you want)? We'll place the order right away. — Verb + 好 = completed action with a satisfying result. 选好 = finished choosing, 写好 = finished writing, 下载好 = finished downloading. The 3-3 sandhi rule applies: 选(t3)→colored orange, 好 stays green.

3. 你写的地址太小了，我______。 🔊

► Pinyin
Nǐ xiě de dìzhǐ tài xiǎo le，wǒ ______。
► Answer
A. 看不到 — 你写的地址太小了，我看不到。
Nǐ xiě de dìzhǐ tài xiǎo le，wǒ kàn búdào。
The address you wrote is too small, I can't see it. — 看不到 = can't see (potential complement: verb + 不 + 到/见). 找不到 = can't find. 收不到 = can't receive. Note: 不 before 到 (4th tone) changes to bú (2nd tone) — both the mark AND the color shift.

4. 他每天骑电动车______，一天能送几十单。 🔊

► Pinyin
Tā měitiān qí diàndòngchē ______，yī tiān néng sòng jǐshí dān。
► Answer
B. 送外卖 — 他每天骑电动车送外卖，一天能送几十单。
Tā měitiān qí diàndòngchē sòngwàimài，yī tiān néng sòng jǐshí dān。
He rides an electric scooter to deliver food every day — he can deliver dozens of orders in a day. — 送外卖 = deliver takeout (送 = deliver/send). 收外卖 = receive takeout (收 = receive). 点外卖 = order takeout (点 = to select/order). Three key actions around food delivery — memorize the verb!

5. 你把美团______了吗？可以用它点外卖。 🔊

► Pinyin
Nǐ bǎ Měituán ______ le ma？Kěyǐ yòng tā diǎnwàimài。
► Answer
C. 下载好 — 你把美团下载好了吗？可以用它点外卖。
Nǐ bǎ Měituán xiàzǎihǎo le ma？Kěyǐ yòng tā diǎnwàimài。
Have you finished downloading Meituan? You can use it to order food delivery. — 把 + object + verb结果补语 is a useful pattern: 把美团下载好 = download Meituan (to completion). 美团 is one of China's top food delivery apps alongside 饿了么. Note sandhi in 下载好: 载(t3)→orange, 好(t3)→green.
Section 4: English → Chinese Sentence Translation

Read the English sentence. Write the Chinese translation on paper or in your head, then toggle the answer. Use 🔊 next to the Chinese answer to hear it.

1. Translate: "We have no other choice — let's order delivery."

► Answer
我们没有别的选择，点外卖吧。 🔊
Wǒmen méiyǒu bié de xuǎnzé，diǎn wàimài ba。
We have no other choice — let's order delivery.
没有别的选择 = have no other choice (lit: don't have other choices). 点 here means 'to order' (点外卖 = order takeout). 吧 softens the suggestion. Note: 没有 = méiyǒu (3-3 sandhi applies: méi→orange, yǒu→green).

2. Translate: "She's going back to her hometown for National Day."

► Answer
她要回老家过国庆节。 🔊
Tā yào huílǎojiā guò Guóqìngjié。
She's going back to her hometown for National Day.
回老家 = go back to hometown (回 = return + 老家 = hometown). 过节 = to celebrate a holiday (过 = to pass/spend). This structure — 回 + location + 过 + festival — is a very common pattern during holidays.

3. Translate: "There are many food delivery riders in the city — it's very tiring work."

► Answer
城市里有很多外卖骑手，很辛苦。 🔊
Chéngshì lǐ yǒu hěn duō wàimài qíshǒu，hěn xīnkǔ。
There are many food delivery riders in the city — it's very tiring work.
外卖骑手 = food delivery rider (外卖 + 骑手). 辛苦 (xīnkǔ) = hard/tiring work (often used to acknowledge someone's effort). You can say 你辛苦了 to thank a delivery rider.

4. Translate: "Because of National Day, we have a compensatory day off on Friday."

► Answer
因为国庆节，我们星期五调休。 🔊
Yīnwèi Guóqìngjié，wǒmen xīngqīwǔ diàoxiū。
Because of National Day, we have a compensatory day off on Friday.
调休 (diàoxiū) = compensatory day off/work schedule adjustment. In China, public holidays often involve 调休 — working on a nearby Saturday or Sunday to give a longer holiday block. Very common topic in daily conversation.

5. Translate: "She started learning Chinese when she was a teenager."

► Answer
她十几岁就开始学中文了。 🔊
Tā shí jǐ suì jiù kāishǐ xué Zhōngwén le。
She started learning Chinese when she was a teenager.
十几岁 = teens (lit: ten-and-some years old). 就 + verb indicates 'as early as / already by then'. Structure: 十几岁就开始… = started doing X already in one's teens. 几 here means 'some number', so 十几 = somewhere in the range of 10-19.
