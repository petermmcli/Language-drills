1. 绿色 🔊

► Pinyin
Ŀsè
► Answer
C. green — 绿色 (Ŀsè) means green. Colors in Chinese use 色 (sè = color). Contrast: 蓝色 blue, 灰色 gray, 粉色 pink.

2. 蓝色 🔊

► Pinyin
lánsè
► Answer
A. blue — 蓝色 (lánsè) is blue. Don’t confuse with 绿色 (lǜ sè) green or 灰色 (huį sè) gray.

3. 出门 🔊

► Pinyin
chūmén
► Answer
C. go out / leave home — 出门 (chūmén) literally means ‘exit the door’. Used whenever you leave the house.

4. 这些 🔊

► Pinyin
zhèxiē
► Answer
D. these — 这些 (zhèxiē) = these (plural of 这个). Pair it with 那些 (nà xiē) = those.

5. 伤心 🔊

► Pinyin
shāngxīn
► Answer
D. sad / heartbroken — 伤心 (shāngxīn) literally ‘wound the heart’. Stronger than just 不开心 (unhappy); implies emotional pain.
Section 2: English → Chinese

1. gray

► Pinyin
A. 灰色 — huīsè
B. 蓝色 — lánsè
C. 粉色 — fěnsè
D. 绿色 — Ŀsè
► Answer
A. 灰色 (huīsè) — gray. The character 灰 (huī) on its own also means gray or ash.

2. wear / put on (clothing)

► Pinyin
A. 出门 — chūmén
B. 穿 — chuān
C. 着 — zhe
D. 衬衫 — chènshān
► Answer
B. 穿 (chuān) — to wear/put on clothing. Note: 穿 (chuān) is for clothes; use 戴 (dài) for accessories like hats, glasses, and jewelry. 着 (zhe) is an aspect particle, not the verb ‘to wear’.

3. bored / boring

► Pinyin
A. 伤心 — shāngxīn
B. 无聊 — wúliáo
C. 动作电影 — dòngzuò diànyǐng
D. 左 — zuǒ
► Answer
B. 无聊 (wúliáo) — bored/boring. Can describe either a feeling (我很无聊 = I’m bored) or a thing (这个很无聊 = this is boring).

4. hair / fur

► Pinyin
A. 衬衫 — chènshān
B. 角 — jiǎo
C. 穿 — chuān
D. 毛 — máo
► Answer
D. 毛 (máo) — hair/fur/feathers. Also means 0.1 yuan (one jiao). Note: 毛 is also a common Chinese surname (e.g., Mao Zedong 毛泽东).

5. pink

► Pinyin
A. 绿色 — Ŀsè
B. 粉色 — fěnsè
C. 灰色 — huīsè
D. 蓝色 — lánsè
► Answer
B. 粉色 (fěnsè) — pink. The character 粉 (fěn) also means powder/flour.
Section 3: Fill in the Blank

Choose the word that best completes the sentence. Use 🔊 to hear the full sentence with the correct answer filled in.

1. 我不喜欢这些，我想要______。 🔊

► Pinyin
Wǒ bù xǐhuān zhèxiē，wǒ xiǎng yào ______。
► Answer
A. 那些 — 我不喜欢这些，我想要那些。
Wǒ bù xǐhuān zhèxiē，wǒ xiǎng yào nàxiē。
I don’t like these; I want those. — 这些 (zhèxiē) = these; 那些 (nàxiē) = those. The suffix 些 (xiē) makes a pronoun plural, like English ‘-se’ in these/those.

2. 他今天穿着一件白色的______。 🔊

► Pinyin
Tā jīntiān chuānzhe yī jiàn báisè de ______。
► Answer
B. 衬衫 — 他今天穿着一件白色的衬衫。
Tā jīntiān chuānzhe yī jiàn báisè de chènshān。
He is wearing a white shirt today. — 穿着 (chuān zhe) = is wearing. 一件 is the measure word (classifier) for clothing items like shirts and jackets.

3. 三块錢等于三十______。 🔊

► Pinyin
Sān kuài qián děngyú sān shí ______。
► Answer
A. 角 — 三块錢等于三十角。
Sān kuài qián děngyú sān shí jiǎo。
Three yuan equals thirty jiao. — 角 (jiǎo) = 0.1 yuan, same as 毛 (máo) in spoken Chinese. 分 (fēn) = 0.01 yuan. 元 (yuán) = 1 yuan (formal form of 块).

4. 在路口向______拐就能到了。 🔊

► Pinyin
Zài lùkǒu xiàng ______ guǎi jiù néng dào le。
► Answer
A. 左 — 在路口向左拐就能到了。
Zài lùkǒu xiàng zuǒ guǎi jiù néng dào le。
Turn left at the intersection and you’ll be there. — 左 (zuǒ) = left; 右 (yòu) = right. 向左拐 = turn left. You can also say 左拐 or 左转.
Section 4: English → Chinese Sentence Translation

Read the English sentence. Write the Chinese translation on paper or in your head, then toggle the answer. Use 🔊 next to the Chinese answer to hear it.

1. Translate: "She is wearing a gray shirt."

► Answer
她穿着一件灰色的衬衫。 🔊
Tā chuānzhe yī jiàn huīsè de chènshān。
She is wearing a gray shirt.
着 (zhe) after a verb marks an ongoing state. 穿着 = is wearing (static). Compare 穿上 (chuān shàng) = put on (dynamic action).

2. Translate: "I’m bored. Do you want to watch an action movie?"

► Answer
我很无聊，你想看动作电影吗？ 🔊
Wǒ hěn wúliáo，nǐ xiǎng kàn dòngzuò diànyǐng ma？
I’m bored. Do you want to watch an action movie?
动作电影 (dòngzuò diànyǐng) = action movie. Short form: 动作片 (action film). 电影 alone just means ‘movie’.

3. Translate: "Is there a restaurant near here?"

► Answer
这附近有饭馆吗？ 🔊
Zhè fùjìn yǒu fànguǎn ma？
Is there a restaurant near here?
饭馆 (fànguǎn) = restaurant (casual/colloquial). More formal: 餐厅 (cāntīng). The character 馆 (guǎn) is used in 博物馆 (museum) and 酒馆 (hotel) too.

4. Translate: "At the next intersection, turn right."

► Answer
在下一个路口右拐。 🔊
Zài xià yī ge lùkǒu yòuguǎi。
At the next intersection, turn right.
右拐 (yòuguǎi) = turn right. 左拐 (zuǒguǎi) = turn left. 下一个 (xià yī ge) = next one.
