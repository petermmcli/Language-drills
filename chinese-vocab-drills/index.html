Section 1: Chinese → English

1. 大雨 🔊

► Pinyin
dà yǔ
► Answer
C. heavy rain — 大雨 (dà yǔ) means heavy rain. Contrast with 小雨 (xiǎo yǔ) = light rain.

2. 半个小时就来 🔊

► Pinyin
bàn gè xiǎo shí jiù lái
► Answer
B. be there in half an hour — 半个小时就来 (bàn gè xiǎo shí jiù lái) means "I'll be there in half an hour." 就 adds a sense of "soon/right away."

3. 小雨 🔊

► Pinyin
xiǎo yǔ
► Answer
A. light rain — 小雨 (xiǎo yǔ) is a light drizzle. The pattern 小+noun = small/light version: 小雨 (drizzle), contrast 大雨 (dà yǔ) = downpour.

4. 五分钟就到 🔊

► Pinyin
wǔ fēn zhōng jiù dào
► Answer
D. be there in 5 minutes — 五分钟就到 (wǔ fēn zhōng jiù dào) is a handy text phrase. 就 softens the promise: "I'll be right there."

5. 不舒服 🔊

► Pinyin
bù shū fu
► Answer
B. not feeling well — 不舒服 (bù shū fu) can mean physically unwell or uncomfortable. The opposite 舒服 (shū fu) = comfortable.
Section 2: English → Chinese

Read the English. Choose the correct Hanzi. Use 🔊 on each option to hear it. Toggle ► Pinyin to reveal readings.

1. who

► Pinyin
A. 谁 — shéi
B. 香港 — Xiāng gǎng
C. 春天 — chūn tiān
D. 我就是 — wǒ jiù shì
► Answer
A. 谁 (shéi) — "who." Used in questions: 你是谁？ = Who are you? Note: also read as shí in some contexts, but shéi is standard Mandarin.

2. jump rope (the activity)

► Pinyin
A. 肉夹馍 — ròu jiā mó
B. 差不多 — chà bù duō
C. 跳绳 — tiào shéng
D. 大雨 — dà yǔ
► Answer
C. 跳绳 (tiào shéng) — jump rope. 跳 = jump, 绳 = rope. Common school exercise and sport.

3. Hong Kong

► Pinyin
A. 谁 — shéi
B. 春天 — chūn tiān
C. 永不会 — yǒng bù huì
D. 香港 — Xiāng gǎng
► Answer
D. 香港 (Xiāng gǎng) — Hong Kong. 香 = fragrant/incense, 港 = harbour. "Fragrant Harbour" is its literal meaning.

4. spring (season)

► Pinyin
A. 大雨 — dà yǔ
B. 春天 — chūn tiān
C. 跳绳 — tiào shéng
D. 香港 — Xiāng gǎng
► Answer
B. 春天 (chūn tiān) — spring. The four seasons: 春 (spring), 夏 (summer), 秋 (autumn), 冬 (winter). 天 here means "sky/season."

5. That's me / I'm the one (emphatic identification)

► Pinyin
A. 春天 — chūn tiān
B. 不舒服 — bù shū fu
C. 差不多 — chà bù duō
D. 我就是 — wǒ jiù shì
► Answer
D. 我就是 (wǒ jiù shì) — "That's me" or "I'm exactly the one." 就 adds emphasis: it's precisely me, no doubt about it.
Section 3: Fill in the Blank

Choose the word that best completes the sentence. Use 🔊 to hear the full sentence with the correct answer filled in.

1. 天气这么热，我想吃______。 🔊

► Pinyin
Tiānqì zhème rè,wǒ xiǎng chī ______。
► Answer
B. 冰淇淋 — 天气这么热，我想吃冰淇淋。
Tiānqì zhème rè, wǒ xiǎng chī bīngqílín。
The weather is so hot, I want to eat ice cream. — 这么 = so/this [adjective]. 冰淇淋 is a phonetic loan from English 'ice cream.'

2. 他说他______忘记那段回忆。 🔊

► Pinyin
Tā shuō tā ______ wàngjì nà duàn huíyì。
► Answer
A. 永不会 — 他说他永不会忘记那段回忆。
Tā shuō tā yǒngbùhuì wàngjì nà duàn huíyì。
He says he will never forget those memories. — 永不会 = 永 (forever) + 不会 (won't). 那段回忆 = that period of memories.

3. 在西安，有名的小吃是______。 🔊

► Pinyin
Zài Xīān,yǒumíng de xiǎochī shì ______。
► Answer
C. 肉夹馍 — 在西安，有名的小吃是肉夹馍。
Zài Xīān, yǒumíng de xiǎochī shì ròujiāmó。
In Xi'an, the famous street food is the Chinese burger (rou jia mo). — 肉夹馍 literally = 'meat sandwiched in bread.' A popular street snack from Shaanxi.

4. 你是怎么回家的？——______。 🔊

► Pinyin
Nǐ shì zěnme huíjiā de？—— ______。
► Answer
A. 我开车回家 — 你是怎么回家的？——我开车回家。
Nǐ shì zěnme huíjiā de？—— wǒ kāichē huíjiā。
How do you get home? — I drive home. — 你是怎么…的？asks about the manner of a past or habitual action. 开车 = drive a car.

5. 这两个苹果______一样大。 🔊

► Pinyin
Zhè liǎnggè píngguǒ ______ yīyàng dà。
► Answer
C. 差不多 — 这两个苹果差不多一样大。
Zhè liǎnggè píngguǒ chàbùduō yīyàng dà。
These two apples are about the same size. — 差不多 = approximately / almost the same. Often used before 一样 (the same) to soften the comparison.
Section 4: English → Chinese Sentence Translation

Read the English sentence. Write the Chinese translation on paper or in your head, then toggle the answer. Use 🔊 next to the Chinese answer to hear it.

1. Translate: "In my home, it will never rain heavily."

► Answer
在我家，永不会下大雨。 🔊
Zài wǒjiā，yǒngbùhuì xià dàyǔ。
In my home, it will never rain heavily.
在+place sets the scene. 下大雨 = to rain heavily (literally 'down heavy rain'). 永不会 = will never.

2. Translate: "In spring, it rains a little."

► Answer
春天下一点儿雨。 🔊
Chūntiān xià yīdiǎnr yǔ。
In spring, it rains a little.
In Chinese, a season can work directly as a time adverbial without 的时候. 下一点儿雨 = to rain a little (一点儿 = a little/a bit).

3. Translate: "In spring, I didn't come to Hong Kong."

► Answer
春天的时候，我没来香港。 🔊
Chūntiān deshíhou，wǒ méi lái Xiānggǎng。
In spring, I didn't come to Hong Kong.
的时候 = 'when / at the time of.' 没 + verb = didn't (negation of a completed action, not 不).

4. Translate: "My home is right next to the restaurant."

► Answer
我家就在饭店旁边。 🔊
Wǒjiā jiùzài fàndiàn pángbiān。
My home is right next to the restaurant.
就在 = 'right at / exactly at' (就 adds emphasis). 旁边 = beside / next to. Note: 我家 = my home (no 的 needed).

5. Translate: "The autumn weather is very comfortable."

► Answer
秋天的天气非常舒服。 🔊
Qiūtiān de tiānqì fēicháng shūfu。
The autumn weather is very comfortable.
非常 = extremely / very (stronger than 很). 舒服 describes physical or environmental comfort.
