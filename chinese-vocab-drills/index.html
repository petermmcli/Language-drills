D. to write an email — 写邮件 (xiě yóujiàn) = write an email. 写 = to write, 邮件 = email/mail.

2. 报告 🔊

► Pinyin
bàogào
► Answer
C. report — 报告 (bàogào) = a report. Also used as a verb "to report". Common in work contexts: 写报告 = write a report.

3. 有点 🔊

► Pinyin
yǒudiǎn
► Answer
A. a little bit / somewhat — 有点 (yǒudiǎn) = somewhat, a bit. Used before adjectives/verbs: 有点贵 = a bit expensive. Compare: 一点儿 = a little (of a noun).

4. 春节 🔊

► Pinyin
Chūnjié
► Answer
B. Spring Festival (Chinese New Year) — 春节 (Chūnjié) = Chinese New Year / Spring Festival. 春 = spring, 节 = festival/holiday. The most important Chinese holiday.

5. 酸 🔊

► Pinyin
suān
► Answer
C. sour — 酸 (suān) = sour. The five Chinese tastes: 甜 (tián) sweet, 酸 (suān) sour, 苦 (kǔ) bitter, 辣 (là) spicy, 咸 (xián) salty.
Section 2: English → Chinese

Read the English. Choose the correct Hanzi. Use 🔊 on each option to hear it. Toggle ► Pinyin to reveal readings.

1. one thing / one matter

► Pinyin
A. 好多了 — hǎo duō le
B. 环境 — huánjìng
C. 一件事情 — yī jiàn shìqíng
D. 倒 — dào
► Answer
C. 一件事情 (yī jiàn shìqíng) = one thing / one matter. 件 is a measure word for matters and events. 事情 = matter, thing.

2. environment / surroundings

► Pinyin
A. 环境 — huánjìng
B. 上班 — shàngbān
C. 好多了 — hǎo duō le
D. 倒 — dào
► Answer
A. 环境 (huánjìng) = environment, surroundings. Often appears in 环境保护 (environmental protection). Also: 工作环境 = work environment.

3. to go to work / to be at work

► Pinyin
A. 倒 — dào
B. 上班 — shàngbān
C. 好多了 — hǎo duō le
D. 环境 — huánjìng
► Answer
B. 上班 (shàngbān) = to go to work, to be at work. Opposite: 下班 (xiàbān) = to get off work. 上班族 = office workers.

4. much better

► Pinyin
A. 一件事情 — yī jiàn shìqíng
B. 好多了 — hǎo duō le
C. 倒 — dào
D. 上班 — shàngbān
► Answer
B. 好多了 (hǎo duō le) = much better. The 了 marks a change of state. Often used when recovering from illness: 我好多了 = I'm much better now.

5. to pour; to fall over

► Pinyin
A. 上班 — shàngbān
B. 环境 — huánjìng
C. 倒 — dào
D. 好多了 — hǎo duō le
► Answer
C. 倒 (dào) = to pour (liquid), or to fall over. Context determines meaning: 倒水 = pour water. 倒下了 = fell over. Also: 倒 can mean "on the contrary" in some uses.
Section 3: Fill in the Blank

Choose the word that best completes the sentence. Use 🔊 to hear the full sentence with the correct answer filled in.

1. 她______我很好，每天给我做饭。 🔊

► Pinyin
Tā ______ wǒ hěn hǎo，měitiān gěi wǒ zuòfàn。
► Answer
A. 对 — 她对我很好，每天给我做饭。
Tā duì wǒ hěn hǎo，měitiān gěi wǒ zuòfàn。
She treats me very well and cooks for me every day. — 对……好 is the pattern 'to be good to / treat well'. 对 is followed by the person, then 好 follows. Don't confuse with 给 (to give/for).

2. 今天是我的生日，妈妈给我买了一个______。 🔊

► Pinyin
Jīntiān shì wǒ de shēngrì，māma gěi wǒ mǎile yīgè ______。
► Answer
C. 蛋糕 — 今天是我的生日，妈妈给我买了一个蛋糕。
Jīntiān shì wǒ de shēngrì，māma gěi wǒ mǎile yīgè dàngāo。
Today is my birthday. Mom bought me a cake. — 蛋糕 = cake. 蛋 = egg, 糕 = cake/pastry. 给我买了 = bought for me. The 了 marks completion.

3. 他昨天______，没有来上班。 🔊

► Pinyin
Tā zuótiān ______，méiyǒu lái shàngbān。
► Answer
B. 生了一天病 — 他昨天生了一天病，没有来上班。
Tā zuótiān shēngle yī tiān bìng，méiyǒu lái shàngbān。
He was sick for a day yesterday and didn't come to work. — 生了一天病 = got sick for one day. 病 is the object of 生, and 一天 is the duration. 没有来 = didn't come.

4. 这里的天气跟上海______。 🔊

► Pinyin
Zhèlǐ de tiānqì gēn Shànghǎi ______。
► Answer
C. 差不多 — 这里的天气跟上海差不多。
Zhèlǐ de tiānqì gēn Shànghǎi chàbuduō。
The weather here is about the same as in Shanghai. — 跟……差不多 = about the same as. Use 一样 for exactly the same, 差不多 for roughly the same. 差 = differ, 不多 = not much.

5. 写完以后，别忘了______文件。 🔊

► Pinyin
Xiěwán yǐhòu，bié wàngle ______ wénjiàn。
► Answer
B. 保存 — 写完以后，别忘了保存文件。
Xiěwán yǐhòu，bié wàngle bǎocún wénjiàn。
After finishing writing, don't forget to save the file. — 保存 = to save (a file). 别忘了 + Verb = don't forget to do something. 写完以后 = after finishing writing.
Section 4: English → Chinese Sentence Translation

Read the English sentence. Write the Chinese translation on paper or in your head, then toggle the answer. Use 🔊 next to the Chinese answer to hear it.

1. Translate: "I'm done eating — you all can go ahead."

► Answer
我吃好了，你们先走吧。 🔊
Wǒ chīhǎole，nǐmen xiān zǒu ba。
I'm done eating — you all can go ahead.
吃好了 = have finished eating. Verb + 好了 = have done something to completion. 先走吧 = go ahead first.

2. Translate: "I've already finished writing the report."

► Answer
我已经写好了报告。 🔊
Wǒ yǐjīng xiěhǎole bàogào。
I've already finished writing the report.
写好了 = have finished writing. 已经 = already, emphasizing completion. Notice 写好了 comes before the object 报告 in this structure.

3. Translate: "I'm going out to buy coffee. Do you want any?"

► Answer
我出去买咖啡，你要吗？ 🔊
Wǒ chūqù mǎi kāfēi，nǐ yào ma？
I'm going out to buy coffee. Do you want any?
出去 + Verb = go out and do something. 你要吗 = do you want (some)? A handy phrase for offering to bring something back.

4. Translate: "Be careful! Don't get hurt."

► Answer
小心！别受伤了。 🔊
Xiǎoxīn！Bié shòushāng le。
Be careful! Don't get hurt.
受伤 = to get hurt / be injured. 受 = to receive/suffer, 伤 = injury. 别 + Verb = don't do something. 小心 = be careful.

5. Translate: "We've finished ordering. Could you bring us some water?"

► Answer
我们点完了，可以给我们拿水吗？ 🔊
Wǒmen diǎnwánle，kěyǐ gěi wǒmen ná shuǐ ma？
We've finished ordering. Could you bring us some water?
点完了 = have finished ordering. Verb + 完了 = completed the action. 可以给我们……吗 = could you (please) … for us? A polite restaurant phrase.
